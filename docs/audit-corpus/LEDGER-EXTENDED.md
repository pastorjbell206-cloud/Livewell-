# The Corpus Ledger — Extended (non-essay surfaces)

> Pass 1 of the Level-Up Commission (`docs/CORPUS-DEPTH-AUDIT.md`), extending the
> essay ledger (`LEDGER.md`) to the surfaces the first audit never scored: the
> study guides, context guides, creeds, the theology library (doctrines, biblical
> theology, church history), and the free books.
>
> Method: for each surface, the total prose length of every item was measured and
> the surface's median taken; an item under 45% of its surface median is flagged
> "thin" for a human read. Length is a proxy, not a verdict — a flagged item is
> then read to see whether it is genuinely thin writing or a piece that is
> complete at its natural length (a meta page, a reference list). The commission
> forbids padding a complete short piece to a number.

## The one-line finding

**The non-essay corpus is uniformly deep and even. Nothing needs remediation.**
Across 178 items on seven surfaces, the depth scan flagged nine outliers; on
reading, every one is either a meta/navigation page that is correct to be brief
or a reference list that is naturally compact. There is not one genuinely thin
study guide, context guide, creed, doctrine, or book. The unevenness the
commission set out to find lived entirely in the essay stubs (`LEDGER.md`), which
are being closed separately (the 27 hand-written commission essays plus the
publish-bridge repair of the other ~129).

## The flagged items, read

- **`theology/methodology` (7.3k)** — title "How to Use This Section." A meta/how-to page. Correct to be brief.
- **`theology/paths` (9.8k)** — the reading-paths navigation page at `/theology/paths`, not a doctrine. Correct to be brief.
- **`theology/theology-creeds`, `theology/theology-hermeneutics`** — overview/primer pages, not contested doctrines; complete at their length.
- **`church-history-councils` (9.6k), `church-history-heresies` (10k)** — reference lists (the councils; the named heresies), naturally more compact than the narrative `figures`/`timeline`. Not thin writing.
- **Books `essays-biblical-theology` (42k) … `come-to-the-table` (65k)** — the essay-volume compilations that gather fewer or shorter chapters. Each is still a full read; padding them would betray the source essays.

## Pass 3 — the evenness read

Read as one body, the non-essay corpus has no seam. A reader moving from a study
guide to a context guide to a doctrine finds the same weight behind each door.
The only door that ever opened onto thinness was an essay stub, and that is a
wiring-and-writing problem being closed on its own track — not a depth problem on
these surfaces.

**Recommendation:** no remediation on the non-essay surfaces. Direct all
level-up effort at the essay stubs until `LEDGER.md` shows zero, then re-run this
scan to confirm the whole corpus reads even.
