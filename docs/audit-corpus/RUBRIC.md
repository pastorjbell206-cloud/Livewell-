# The Corpus Rubric

> Deliverable 1 of the Corpus Audit. The Revision Pass and Formation Test from
> `CLAUDE.md`, operationalized into a five-minute instrument. Every check is
> pass/fail. A piece's tier follows mechanically from its failures — no vibes.

## The twelve checks

**Structure & honesty (from the first pass)**
1. **MISSION** — the piece serves the platform's central question and one of
   the four audiences; it is not content for content's sake.
2. **ARC** — it reaches past the proximate cause toward the longer history or
   deeper assumption. Fails if it starts and ends at this week's surface.
3. **SELF-IMPLICATION** — the writer is inside the indictment. Fails if the
   piece only points at "them" (the culture, the progressives, the
   nationalists, the church-as-other).
4. **STEELMAN** — any contested position gets its strongest form before the
   verdict. Fails if an opponent is met only in caricature. (Pass by default
   if nothing is contested.)
5. **VERDICT** — it ends on weight, not a recap, a tidy application box, or a
   comfortable closing.

**Formation (from the Formation Test)**
6. **FORMS, NOT INFORMS** — a reader finishes more thoughtful, more humble,
   or better able to see — not merely more informed or more certain of their
   side. Fails on the certainty trade and the flattery loop.

**Rhythm & sentence (from the second pass)**
7. **RHYTHM** — sentence lengths vary; verdicts land short. Fails on three-plus
   medium sentences in a row as the piece's default gait.
8. **NO TICS** — "Not X. Y." at most once per movement; no forced triplets; no
   signposting ("here is what I mean," "let me explain").

**Words & mechanics (from the third pass)**
9. **FORBIDDEN LANGUAGE** — zero hits on the forbidden words, phrases, and
   therapy-speak lists. Machine-checkable.
10. **EXCLAMATION** — exclamation points only inside verbatim quoted
    Scripture. Machine-checkable.
11. **HEDGE** — no "it could be argued," "in many ways," "perhaps," "I think"
    doing a verdict's job.

**Trust (the fabrication check)**
12. **CITATIONS** — every named scholar, book, date, study, statistic, and
    Scripture reference is either verifiable or flagged for verification.
    A quotation that cannot be sourced is an automatic CUT-tier failure —
    the one unforgivable scholarly act.

## Tiering (mechanical)

- **FLAGSHIP** — 12/12, and the piece could only have been written by this
  author. Lead with it.
- **SOLID** — passes 10–11, no failure among checks 3, 5, 6, or 12. Ship as is.
- **REVISE** — fails 2–4 checks, or any single failure among 3, 5, or 6.
  Name the failure; fixable in one pass.
- **CUT/REWRITE** — fails 5+ checks, or any failure of check 12
  (unverifiable citation), or the piece would embarrass the platform in
  front of the skeptic it claims to serve.

## Scoring notes

- Judge the piece that exists, not the piece it could become.
- A short piece is not penalized for being short; it is judged in the
  short-form register.
- When two checks blur, the skeptic reader breaks the tie: did they feel
  handled or met?
