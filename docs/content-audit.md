# LiveWell by James Bell — Content Audit

*Prepared as a board-level review of the published catalog, the Substack body of work, and the gaps between them. Figures are from the live site (`posts.navIndex`, June 2026) and the Substack feed.*

---

## 1. Executive summary

The catalog is **strong in subject and uneven in shape**. Nothing is off-brand; the problem the brand audit named — *volume and duplication, not topic drift* — holds up against the data. Three findings drive every recommendation below:

1. **The categories were never populated** until this review. ~65 articles sat in a "Theological Depth" catch-all. All 152 are now sorted into the five pillars and 18 sub-pathways.
2. **A handful of real duplicates** undercut the "serious, carefully-weighted" promise. They are specific and removable (Section 3).
3. **The Substack is the missing half of the website.** It is almost entirely *Prophetic Disruption* — the site's thinnest pillar. Importing it roughly quadruples that pillar (Section 5).

## 3. Duplicates to retire (the credibility fix)

These are genuine near-duplicates — a short "stub" version sitting beside a fuller treatment. Keep the fuller one; **retire (unpublish) the stub** via the *Find duplicate articles* tool.

| Retire | Keep | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `justice-not-political` | `justice-is-not-political-it-is-theological` | Same thesis; the second is the developed essay. |
| `poor-not-ministry` | `the-poor-are-not-a-ministry-category-test-of-the-church` | Stub vs. full. |
| `your-city-is-mission-field` | `your-city-is-your-mission-field-biblical-theology-of-place` | Stub vs. full. |
| `consistent-ethic-life` | `consistent-pro-life-ethic` | Two takes on one idea; keep the titled one. |

**Consolidate, don't just delete:** the *Christian Nationalism & the Kingdom of God* study guide is split across three confusingly-named records (`…-study-guide`, `…-study-guide-part-2`, `…-part-1`). Merge into one clearly-numbered 2-part series.

**Also review (likely thin seed stubs):** `repentance-and-renewal`, `hope-of-resurrection`, `scripture-meditation`, `the-examined-life`, `what-image-of-god-means` — confirm each is a finished piece, not a placeholder.

---

## 4. Over-represented: the "Foundations" tier

~40 articles follow the templates *"What does the Bible say about X"* and *"How to X."* They are useful for search traffic and for newcomers, but they read thinner than Bell's essay voice and should not sit shoulder-to-shoulder with the flagship work.

**Recommendation:** keep them, but present them as a distinct **Foundations / FAQ** tier — a reference layer beneath the essays, not competing with them in the pillar dropdowns. This protects the "opening a serious book in a quiet room" promise while keeping the SEO value.
