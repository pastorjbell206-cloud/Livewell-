# Grow libraries anecdote ledger, sweep 2: life pages, how-tos, wisdom topics

This ledger records every change made in the second integrity sweep of the Grow libraries: the 69 Everyday Life pages (`client/public/life/domains/*.json`), the 132 how-to guides (sources `scripts/articles/*.json`, 11 files), and the 208 wisdom topics (sources `client/public/wisdom/topics/*.json`, 26 files). It follows the method of the study-guide sweep (`anecdote-ledger.md`).

What was removed. James Bell's verified biography is only this: he came to faith from atheism; he was raised without a father; he has five sons; he is lead pastor of First Baptist Church of Fenton, Michigan; he founded the Pastors Connection Network; he is an author. A bare mention of fifteen years of ministry may stay. Everything else told as his life (scenes, counseling cases, funerals, conversations, his marriage, his sons' reactions, what moved him out of atheism, habits confessed as past events) was generalized or cut. The sweep also cut or sourced statistics that had no named, checkable source, fixed the flagged misattributions, and rephrased a forbidden phrase.

How to read it. Each row gives the library, the source file, the page (life), article slug (how-to) or topic id (wisdom), the JSON field path inside that file, the original sentence or sentences verbatim, the action, and the new text. Actions:

- **generalized**: the teaching point kept, the story or first-person claim turned into a common pattern ("many of us", "most of us", "pastors", a type figure) or, where the writer implicates himself, the approved form "I am a pastor, and pastors ...".
- **cut**: the added biography or unsourced figure removed and the sentence repaired, or the sentence removed.
- **corrected**: a misattribution or unsourced figure fixed, with the reason in brackets. Most are the site rule that "James" means James Bell, so the New Testament letter is "the letter of James" and its author "James, the Lord's brother". The brief named the how-tos; the same wording in life pages and wisdom topics was fixed the same way.
- **rephrased**: forbidden language rewritten.

To restore a story that is true, put James's own wording back in the same field and rebuild the index. The changes are in the working tree only; nothing has been committed. The sources were edited string by string inside the existing JSON text, so every file keeps its exact formatting and key order (68 life files and all 11 how-to files are `JSON.stringify(x, null, 2)` plus a newline; `unemployment-and-the-lost-job.json` keeps its one-line connection objects; the wisdom files keep their formatting and their Berean Standard Bible `verses`, which were not touched). The outputs were then rebuilt with `node scripts/build-howtos-index.mjs` (client/public/howtos/), `node scripts/build-wisdom-index.mjs` (client/public/wisdom/topics.json) and `node scripts/build-life-index.mjs` (client/public/life/domains-index.json). A script check confirms each of the 106 source files equals its pre-sweep snapshot plus exactly the edits listed here.

Totals: 195 changes (137 generalized, 14 cut, 43 corrected, 1 rephrased).
