## Book Description (Amazon "Description" field)

The church has been having this conversation badly for decades. On one side, verses quoted like weapons until certainty curdles into cruelty. On the other, Scripture set aside entirely in the name of love. Both responses betray the gospel, and people made in God's image have paid the price.

James C. Bell offers something the debate rarely allows: a fair, careful hearing. He walks verse by verse through every passage Scripture brings to bear — Sodom and Genesis 19, the holiness code of Leviticus, Romans 1, and the disputed Greek words of 1 Corinthians 6 — and shows why sincere Christians read the same texts and reach different conclusions. He presents the affirming case and the traditional case each at full strength, names the church's real failures toward LGBTQ+ people without flinching, and refuses to pretend the questions are simpler than they are.

This is not a book that hands you a slogan. It is a book for the pastor, the parent whose child has just come out, and the believer carrying a question the church has been too afraid to discuss. Bell holds conviction and compassion in the same two hands and asks the church to do the same. The table, he argues, is bigger than the argument.
