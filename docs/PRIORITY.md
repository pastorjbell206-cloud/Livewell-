# The Priority Decision — what gets built this quarter

Source inputs: `docs/content/depth-audit.md`, `docs/content/gap-analysis.md`,
`docs/tools/portfolio.md`. Every recommended asset and tool was scored on
three axes — **value** to the body of work, **fit** to what Bell wants to be
known for, **effort** to build (10 = cheapest). The purpose of this filter is
to stop a library of good ideas from becoming a backlog that never clears.

**The rule: three things this quarter. No more.**

---

## BUILD THIS QUARTER — the three

### 1. Finish *Born Again from Atheism* — essay first, then the manuscript
**Value 10 · Fit 10 · Effort 5**

The single point where every audit converges. The depth audit's #1 upside
(a 10/10 sample with no book behind it). The gap analysis's #1 gap (the
platform's most disarming asset — a pastor who was the skeptic — is invisible
in the essay layer the #1 audience actually reads). Sequence: adapt the
existing sample into the signature conversion essay and publish it as a
cornerstone; then complete the full manuscript chapter by chapter behind it.
The essay ships in days and starts earning trust while the book is written.

### 2. The justice-cluster steelman pass (~40 essays)
**Value 9 · Fit 10 · Effort 6**

The largest block of below-bar content on the site, and the one place the
corpus breaks the platform's own credibility rule (the guardrail firing in
one political direction only). The both-sides pass (steelman the contested
position, move symptom → cause → wisdom, restore ESV discipline, kill the
doubled last line, pick one canonical piece per duplicated topic) is a
repeatable editorial template applied across the cluster. This is the pass
that protects the skeptic's trust everywhere else on the site.

### 3. Leadership Formation Inventory → signature tool
**Value 8 · Fit 9 · Effort 8**

The portfolio audit's single bet: the highest-intent user on the site (a
pastor examining his own character), a moat no competitor can clone (the
feedback prose), and a designed retention loop that only needs closing.
Scope: longitudinal trend chart across retakes (data already persisted), a
printable/PDF result, the six-month retake reminder, and closing the
retake-persistence gap across the sibling assessments. Lowest lift, highest
tool ceiling, feeds the PCN channel.

## Start first

**The conversion essay (item 1's first half).** It is the top of both content
rankings, small enough to ship inside a week, and it unblocks the manuscript
rather than waiting on it. Nothing else buys as much trust per word.
