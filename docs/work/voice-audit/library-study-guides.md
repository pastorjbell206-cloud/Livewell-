# Voice audit: study guides

Library: `/home/user/Livewell-/client/public/studyguides/` (68 guides, about 1.6 million words). Read-only audit; nothing was edited.

**human_score: 4 / 10. voice_score: 4 / 10.** (The six newer guides alone would score about 8 and 8; the sixty-two older ones about 3 and 4.)

## Verdict

Two libraries share this folder. Six newer guides (youth-who-am-i, forty-days, when-our-parents-grow-old, divorce-and-blended-families, waiting-for-the-light, addiction-and-recovery) are in the mature register: contractions, paragraphs, Scripture quoted with its reference, no third-person 'Bell', help lines present. The other sixty-two were built from one template and read as machine-made in bulk: a narrator describing 'Bell' inside his own teaching (1,873 times), the editorial rulebook written into the prose (steelman, verdict, indictment, 'this platform'), his biography stamped as a credential on 49 guides, 'It is not X. It is Y.' about 1,850 times, 'honest' about 3,350 times, zero contractions, and every teaching one unbroken paragraph of 750 to 1,570 words. Mechanically the library is clean: no em-dashes, no exclamation points outside Scripture, no live Forbidden Language beyond 'the blessed life'.

## Most machine-sounding and most his

Most machine-sounding: witness, deconstruction, identity, the-undivided-life, character-before-competence, economic-justice, womanhood (session 4 teaching), baptism-and-communion, doubt, aging-and-legacy, mishpat, resurrection.

## Rules for the fixer (apply library-wide)

1. Never 'Bell' inside summary, teaching, objections, FAQ or scripts. In teaching, James speaks as 'I' or 'we'. In leader notes, say 'the study' or 'this session', or just say the thing.
2. Keep the biography in at most one place per guide (the one teaching where it bears on the subject, e.g. fatherhood session 1, prayer session 1), in his own first person, one plain sentence. Delete it from summaries, primers, FAQ, facilitator openings and all promo copy.
3. Delete every use of steelman/steel-man, 'inside the indictment', 'self-implication', 'even-handedness', 'this platform', 'the method is the message/mercy', and 'verdict' used of the study's own conclusion. Do the fair thing; don't name it.
4. Cut announced turns: 'Then the turn.', 'Then the reversal.', 'This is the hinge.', 'Grant all of it.', 'But here is what the objection misses', 'Now the verdict...'. Say the next thing.
5. Leave one 'not X, it is Y' per session at the real hinge; turn the rest into plain positive statements. Rebuild quote cards so most are not antithesis pairs.
6. 'Honest' once per session at most.
7. Add contractions wherever the line is spoken or addressed to 'you' (facilitator script first, then teaching). Leave Scripture and prayers alone.
8. Break each teaching into paragraphs of roughly 100 to 160 words at its existing turns. Do not reorder.
9. Vary or drop the shared stamps ('Come honest, not finished.', 'Every question carries the answer behind it...', 'No, and the study is careful here.', 'It will not do that, and the leader must never let it.').
10. Verify each bibliography entry, then delete every 'Leader to confirm...' note.
11. Use the six newer guides as the model for all of the above.
