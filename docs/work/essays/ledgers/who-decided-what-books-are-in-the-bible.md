## Opening (Athanasius's letter of 367)

Search, as summarized: Pauline Allen, "The Festal Letters of the Patriarchs of Alexandria" (acuresearchbank.acu.edu.au): from the third century onward the patriarchs of Alexandria announced by letter each year the date of Easter, with the start of the Lenten fast and Pentecost; Athanasius kept the practice even in exile. The library heading of Letter 39 reads "on the Paschal festival".
