# wmap ledger: where each point on the wider maps comes from

Coordinates are (latitude north, longitude east), decimal degrees. The maps are
teaching sketches, not surveys: coasts and rivers are simplified to a few dozen
points and drawn as they are today. Sources were read by web search on
2026-11-19 (Wikipedia infoboxes and mirrors, Pleiades, CDLI, OpenBible, the
UNESCO Babylon nomination, Bible encyclopedias). "Checked" means a search
result gave the figure to within about 0.02 degrees; "approximate" means the
point is placed from general reference and the shape it gives was checked by
eye against the checked points around it, not looked up one by one.

## The wide frame ("fertile-crescent", 29 to 49 E, 28 to 38 N)

Mediterranean coast, checked: Iskenderun 36.58, 36.17; Latakia 35.52, 35.78;
Tripoli 34.44, 35.83; Beirut 33.89, 35.50; Gaza 31.52, 34.45; El Arish 31.13,
33.80; Port Said 31.26, 32.30; Damietta mouth 31.52, 31.84 (Pleiades);
Rosetta 31.40, 30.42; Alexandria 31.20, 29.92. Tyre, Carmel and Jaffa are the
old map's own points. Approximate (general reference, not looked up one by
one): the Anatolian coast west of Iskenderun (Yumurtalik 36.77, 35.79;
Karatas 36.57, 35.38; Mersin 36.80, 34.63; Silifke 36.32, 33.88; Anamur
36.02, 32.83; Alanya 36.54, 32.0; Antalya 36.88, 30.70; Kemer, Finike, Kas),
Arsuz 36.41, 35.88, Ras el-Bassit 35.85, 35.83, Tartus 34.89, 35.88, Rafah
31.29, 34.22, the Bardawil lagoon coast 31.2, 33.1, the delta's northern bulge
31.58, 31.1, Abu Qir 31.32, 30.06, and the coast leaving the frame at 30.85,
28.9.

Cyprus, checked at its capes: Apostolos Andreas 35.70, 34.59; Gata 34.57,
33.02; Arnauti 35.05, 32.27. The rest of its outline (Paphos, Larnaca, Cape
Greco, Famagusta, Kyrenia, Kormakitis, Morphou) is approximate.

Nile, checked: Qanatir, where the branches part, 30.19, 31.13; Rosetta and
Damietta mouths as above. Approximate: Minya 28.1, 30.75; Beni Suef 29.07,
31.10; Cairo 30.05, 31.23; Benha 30.46, 31.18; Mansoura 31.04, 31.38.

Gulf of Suez: head at Suez 29.97, 32.55 (checked). Gulf of Aqaba: head at
Eilat 29.55, 34.95 and Aqaba 29.53, 35.00 (checked). Both shores of each arm
are approximate (Ain Sokhna, Zafarana, Ras Gharib, El Tor, Abu Rudeis, Ras
Sudr; Taba, Nuweiba, Dahab, Haql). Ras Muhammad, where the arms meet (27.72,
34.25, checked), lies just below the frame.

Euphrates, checked: Jarabulus (Carchemish) 36.82, 38.01; Raqqa 35.95, 39.01;
Deir ez-Zor 35.33, 40.14; Abu Kamal 34.45, 40.94 (Mari, 34.55, 40.89, lies by
it); Babylon 32.54, 44.42; Nasiriyah 31.05, 46.27; Qurna, where it meets the
Tigris, 31.02, 47.43. Approximate: Samsat 37.58, 38.48; Birecik 37.03, 37.98;
Tabqa 35.85, 38.55; Anah 34.47, 41.99; Haditha 34.13, 42.38; Hit 33.64, 42.83;
Ramadi 33.42, 43.30; Fallujah 33.35, 43.78; Kufa 32.03, 44.40; Samawah 31.31,
45.28; the marsh course from Nasiriyah to Qurna.

Tigris, checked: Tikrit 34.61, 43.68; Kut 32.51, 45.82; Qurna as above.
Approximate (searches did not return them): Diyarbakir 37.91, 40.24;
Hasankeyf 37.71, 41.41; Cizre 37.33, 42.19; Mosul 36.34, 43.13; Samarra 34.20,
43.87; Baghdad 33.31, 44.36 (85 to 90 km north of Babylon, which fits);
Amarah 31.84, 47.14.

Shatt al-Arab and the Gulf: Qurna to Basra (30.51, 47.81, approximate) to the
sea just south of Al-Faw (29.98, 48.47, checked). The head of the Gulf
(Bubiyan, Kuwait Bay, the coast south to Ras al-Khafji) is approximate; it is
the shoreline of today, which has moved since antiquity.

Dead Sea and Jordan at this scale: a small block from 31.77 to 31.05 N and a
line from the Upper Jordan to the Dead Sea; not detailed.

## The Canaan frame ("canaan", 34.0 to 36.4 E, 30.8 to 33.7 N)

The old map's coast, Sea of Galilee and Jordan, unchanged. Added south of
Ashkelon: Gaza 31.52, 34.45 (checked), Khan Yunis 31.35, 34.29 and Rafah
31.29, 34.22 (approximate), and the coast turning west into Sinai, leaving
the frame near 31.16, 33.9 (approximate; El Arish, checked at 31.13, 33.80, is
just outside). The whole Dead Sea: north shore 31.77 as on the old map; the
Lisan peninsula at about 31.25, 35.45 (checked); the southern basin to about
31.03, drawn as it was before the modern evaporation ponds, roughly.
