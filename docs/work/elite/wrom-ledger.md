# Ledger: The Roman world (portrait at /resources/context/world/roman-world)

Every specific claim in `client/public/context/worlds/roman-world.json`, its
source, and how it was checked (2026-11-19). Web searches were run with the
WebSearch tool; direct fetches of LacusCurtius, Perseus mirrors, Fordham and
Gutenberg were refused by this machine's network, so ancient texts were matched
through search results that quote or summarize them, and the Latin of Pliny
was matched through the Dickinson College Commentaries text. Bible references
in the prose were read against the Study Bible's BSB text in
`client/public/bible/text/`. "Standard" means a fact found in every reference
work and not separately searched; these are listed again at the end.

No ancient author is quoted in English on the page except Suetonius, Nero 16.2
(J. C. Rolfe's Loeb translation, the wording confirmed in search results).
Everything else from an ancient writer is paraphrase, or a short Latin or Greek
phrase confirmed in a search.
