A simpler LiveWell

Seven pages, before and after, at phone width (390) and desktop width (1440). Each picture is the first two screens of the page; where the change sits lower down, a third picture shows that part. The palette, type and drawings are the site's own and did not change; the structure, the density and some interface words did.

Both sides were taken on 2026-11-27 from local builds served the way Vercel serves them (before: origin/main; after: this branch), with no database behind them. Two things look empty on both sides and are not broken on the real site: the film posters (stored on Vercel's file storage, which this session's network refuses) and the latest essays on Home (they come from the database). Phone pictures are at twice the pixel density; desktop at one.

The ten changes, ranked

The full diagnosis, page by page, is diagnosis.md beside this file. Changes 1 to 9 are in this preview; 10 is left for James.

  1. Fold a Bible book's guide into rows that open in place, the first open, with Start at chapter 1 and the chapters first.
  2. One way to begin on Home: Start here, beside the search box, in place of seven chips.
  3. Four big cards, then "See everything" on Home and each door's page; nothing leaves the site.
  4. A next step at the end of every page: one band, one button, at most a quiet second.
  5. Video near the top where a page has a film (the Bible door, a book with a film).
  6. The ask box is the Questions door; two questions per topic with All behind them.
  7. A few good first tools, then the four groups as rows that open.
  8. One "more help" list on the Family door, every link kept.
  9. Shorten Home: the picker, the second tonight band and the history strip live on their doors.
  10. Quiet the chapter's top before verse 1 (not built: the most visited page, his call).
