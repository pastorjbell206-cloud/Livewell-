# The writing wave: what changed, what was put back, and what waits for you

James, you asked that the writing on the site sound like you and like a human being, with none of the habits that give machine writing away. Nine writers each took one part of the site. A second reader then went through every change before it was merged, to protect the writing from the writer. This is the plain account of what happened.

Nothing in any of these passes changed a fact, a name, a date, a number, a Scripture reference, a doctrine, a catechism answer or a title on purpose. Where the second reader found one that had moved, it was put back (see "What the second reader put back").

## What changed in each part of the site

### Find Help (the forty care pages)

About 234 small edits. The pages leaned on the same few stamps over and over, and those were thinned.

- "Honest" and its cousins: 182 times before, 114 after.
- "I'm a pastor" in any form: 44 before, 24 after. The confession "I'm a pastor and I'm inside this" now stands only on the church-hurt and pastors pages, where a pastor's failure is the subject.
- "I came to faith from atheism": on 23 pages before, 9 after.
- "Five sons": on 17 pages before, 8 after.
- "The usual explanations each hold something true": on 33 pages before, 5 after.
- "Verdict": 44 before, 20 after. "Indictment": 7 before, none after.

Every mention of 988, 911, hotlines, doctors, counselors, police and "this is not medical advice" was counted page by page before and after, and every one is still there.

Three examples:

- Doubt. Before: "I'm a pastor, and this indictment includes me. We have too often met a teenager's honest question with a verse and a warning." After: "We have too often met a teenager's question with a verse and a warning."
- Anxiety. Before: "Anxiety is not a verdict on your faith. It's a weight on body and soul together... and it deserves real care." After: "Anxiety doesn't mean your faith is weak. It's a weight on body and soul together... and it needs real care."
- Dating. Before: "I have five sons, and I'd rather they bring this question to me than hide it from me, which means I have to listen before I lecture." After: "If you'd rather your child bring this question to you than hide it, you'll have to listen before you lecture."

### Honest Answers (the 35 answers)

138 sentence-level edits; 98 of the audit's 101 findings applied.

- Long dashes: 54 before, none after.
- "Honest": 12 before, 3 after.

- Rulebook words (verdict, indictment, full strength, first-order and second-order, confessional): 21 before, none after.
- Contractions: 3 before, 88 after.

Three examples:

- Anger at God. Before: "The opposite of faith was never anger. It was the silence of walking away. You are still yelling at him. So was his Son." After: "Anger isn't the opposite of faith. Walking away in silence is. You are still yelling at him, and so was his Son."

You can close this page holding either answer and still be my brother or sister..."

One factual correction: the father in Mark 9 is no longer described as having "a dying child". The boy was tormented, so it now reads "a suffering son".

## What the second reader put back

- **Find Help, money.** The writer cut "You don't have to fix it tonight." It is a sentence of care for someone frightened about money, and it is back. (A note on the anxiety page also lost its full stop; that is fixed.)
- **Church history, the Reformation.** "Say it without flinching and without the anachronism that everyone did it, as though that closes the matter" had become "Everyone did it, and that doesn't close the matter," which concedes the very excuse the essay refuses. It now reads: "It won't do to say that everyone did it, as though that closes the matter."
- **Family, thankfulness.** "Give thanks in all circumstances, not for all circumstances" had become "every circumstance". It is back to "all", as 1 Thessalonians 5:18 has it.
- **About.** "I'm trying to put theology back in contact with ordinary life" had dropped the depth and the weight from your own tagline. It reads again: "taking the depth of theology and putting it back in contact with the weight of an ordinary life."
- **The two book pages.** The headings "The twelve questions" and "The chapters" lost what they told the reader. They are back to "Twelve questions, from the cosmos to the cross" and "Thirteen movements, from Hosea to the cross".
- **Where two writers touched the same thing.** The study-guide writer and the safety writer both added a line about reporting abuse to the deconstruction guide; the safety writer's lines are kept and the duplicate is gone. On the Speaking page, the sentence about "a network of pastors" stays removed, as you asked, with the writer's plainer wording around it. On the home page, the newsletter box keeps the new home design, so the writer's edit to its old description is not used.

Everything else was read and kept. Most of the cuts take out a stamp and leave the sentence saying the same thing more plainly.

**The study guides.** The interrupted writer reached the teaching sections and a few other places, but "Bell" still appears about 1,700 times across the 68 guides, mostly in summaries, leader's notes, questions and promotional text. The audit's plan was to keep your story in one place per guide, in your own first person, and take it out of everything else. The guides also still carry about 300 "Leader to confirm" notes on their reading lists, which tell a reader the books were never checked. This needs a full second pass, guide by guide, and then the printed guides need to be rebuilt.

**The Study Bible notes.** Only the audit's 65 named fixes were made. The same stock openings ("the objection is strong", "several misreadings recur") still run through the rest of the 1,189 chapter notes, and Jonah 4 still gives no help line beside a prophet who asks to die. A pass across all the notes, done by pattern and then read in context, is still owed.

Smaller things still open: the downloadable devotion cards and printed study guides do not yet carry the new wording (they are rebuilt from the text when the downloads are next made), and nobody has yet read every Scripture reading, question and prayer on the forty Find Help pages word for word.

## The Bible chapter page that jumped while it loaded

On a slow phone the chapter page jumped twice as it finished loading: the verses were first drawn in a stand-in typeface and then redrawn when the reading typeface arrived, and the "Whole summary" link appeared a moment late and pushed the text down a line. The page now waits for its typeface before it appears and places that link before it is first shown, so on repeated slow loads of John 3 and Psalm 23 nothing moves at all (the measured jump fell from 0.28 to 0.00, against Google's limit of 0.1).
