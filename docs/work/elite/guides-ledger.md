# Study guides pilot: ledger of claims checked

Every quotation of a person (other than Scripture), every book, date and biographical claim the three old guides carried, and what happened to it. "Searched" means a web search I ran on 2026-11-18 (11 of the 15 allowed). "Known" means a widely documented fact I did not search and judged safe; James or an editor may want to spot-check these.

Scripture is quoted from the ESV, the text the three guides already used (none declares a translation). I quoted from memory of the ESV and kept quotations short where I was less sure; the ESV is not in this repository, so the Scripture wording was not machine-checked. Memory verses that had been trimmed or re-punctuated were restored to ESV wording (Lamentations 3:22-23, 2 Corinthians 1:3-4, Psalm 13:1, Psalm 13:5, Job 42:7, 2 Corinthians 12:9).
