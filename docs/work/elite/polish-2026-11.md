# Polish run, November 2026: the most-visited pages as a first-time reader sees them

James, 2026-11-27: "continue to improve the writing and the design and the overall website."

Branch `elite/polish`, from `claude/todo-implementation-hhp4bm` at d8b1ba29.

## How it was looked at

`pnpm build`, `pnpm sitemap`, `pnpm prerender`, then `scripts/serve-like-vercel.mjs --port 4613`. Each page was opened in Chromium through Playwright at 390 by 844 and 1440 by 900, device scale 1, in light and in dark (`html.dark` added after load), scrolled once top to bottom so every lazy part had mounted, then photographed one screen at a time. The screens were read as contact sheets of four. The same pass measured sideways scroll, the H1's size, every control under 44px, and text under 4.5:1 against its real background.

Screenshots are outside the repo, in `/home/user/polish-shots/` (`g1/` to `g4/`; each folder's `sheets/` holds the contact sheets that were read, named `<page>-<width>-<mode>-s<n>.png`).

Two things the local server cannot show: pictures served from outside the site (the /about portrait on CloudFront, the film posters on Vercel Blob) do not load here, so they show as empty frames in the screenshots; and the suggested-gift sheet and the giving wording on /downloads need the live API, so the page was seen in its giving-off state.

Dark mode: `ThemeProvider` is mounted with `switchable` off and the CSS has no `prefers-color-scheme` rule, so no reader can reach dark mode today. It was still checked, because the tokens are there.

Across every page: no sideways scroll at 390, every H1 in Cormorant at the size its PageHead calls for (doors 40px phone and 78px desktop, inner pages 36 and 56).
