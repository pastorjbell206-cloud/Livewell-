# Facts ledger: the seven older /faq pages

Run of 2026-11-19 on branch elite/voice. Every figure, quotation, date and named source on the seven pages in `client/src/pages/faq/` (WhyArePeopleLeavingChurch, WhyDoYoungPeopleLeaveChurch, WhatDoChristiansBelieveAboutHell, WhatIsTheDifferenceBetweenCatholicAndProtestant, WhatIsDeconstruction, WhatIsReligiousTrauma, WhatDenominationShouldIJoin) was checked against its publisher. The lists came from the "Sixth run" section of `voice.md` and `voice-ledger.md`; items found on reading are marked "(found)".

Method. About 45 WebSearch calls. No primary page could be opened: WebFetch to vatican.va, pewresearch.org, intratext.com and brnow.org failed to resolve (outside this session's allowed network). So "search" below means the search tool's reading of result pages, which named the publisher's own page where one is given (news.gallup.com, pewresearch.org, prri.org, news.lifeway.com, fulleryouthinstitute.org, barna.com, umc.org, ccel.org). Scripture was compared by script with a local ESV copy (lguenth/mdbible `json/ESV.json`, "English Standard Version 2011", Crossway), the same copy the essay ledgers used.

Decisions: CONFIRMED (left, or attribution fixed to match), CORRECTED (changed to what the source says, source named in the sentence), SOFTENED (no source for the exact figure; restated without false precision), CUT.

Only the sentence carrying each item changed. No component structure, import, SEOMeta string, JSON-LD shape, href or help sentence was touched.

## Why do young people leave church

16. **Was:** Kinnaman, *You Lost Me*, six reasons: overprotective, shallow, anti-science, repressive about sexuality, exclusive, unfriendly to doubt. **Searched:** Barna "Six Reasons Young Christians Leave Church". **Source:** Barna Group (barna.com, 2011) and Christianity Today interview (Dec 2011): overprotective, shallow, antiscience, repressive, exclusive, doubtless; book 2011 (Baker). **CONFIRMED.** **Now:** unchanged.
17. **Was:** Growing Young "tend to share three characteristics: they take the faith of young people seriously..., they engage with the hard questions..., and they give young adults meaningful responsibility..." 37. **Was:** "The Westminster Confession of Faith... calls the Pope the Antichrist... but it still recognizes baptized Catholics as having received a valid Christian sacrament." **Searched:** WCF 25.6; Reformed view of Roman baptism. **Source:** WCF 25.6 (1646 text): the Pope "is that Antichrist, that man of sin, and son of perdition"; American Presbyterians struck the clause in 1789 (Crossway, 2014). It says nothing directly about Catholic baptism, but most Reformed churches since Calvin have accepted it as valid and haven't rebaptized Catholics who join them."
