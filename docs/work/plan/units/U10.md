# U10 Church-year rules and the generated year map

## What changed

- `scripts/lib/church-year.mjs` (new). Pure rules on ISO calendar dates, with
  arithmetic in UTC so no time zone or DST change moves a day: Easter (the
  anonymous Gregorian computus, Meeus/Butcher), Ash Wednesday (Easter - 46),
  Palm Sunday and Holy Saturday, Pentecost (Easter + 49), Advent Sunday (the
  fourth Sunday before Christmas), Christmas and its twelve days to January 5,
  Epiphany, All Saints (November 1), US Thanksgiving (fourth Thursday of
  November). `seasonsOf(year)` lists a church year's seasons and feasts with
  inclusive dates; a church year is named by its Easter (church year 2027
  begins Advent Sunday 2026). `weekSeason(sunday)`: a week runs Sunday to
  Saturday and keeps the season of its Sunday; a feast (Epiphany, All Saints,
  Thanksgiving) claims the plain or Christmas week it falls in. This gives
  exactly the plan's "this winter's five" plain weeks (January 10 to February
  7, 2027): the week of Ash Wednesday stays plain and Lent's weeks start on
  the first Sunday in Lent.
- `scripts/lib/family-year.mjs` (new). Builds the map from the data:
  - Range: church years 2026 to 2031 (`FIRST_YEAR`, `LAST_YEAR`), November 30,
    2025 to November 29, 2031: today and Thanksgiving 2026 are on it, and it
    reaches five years ahead. 313 weeks.
  - Daily sets: `SETS` places the three sets in `family-seasonal.json` (Advent
    December 1 to 25, per James's 2026-10-26 answer; Holy Week Palm Sunday to
    Easter; Thanksgiving the Friday before to Thanksgiving Day). A week in a
    season with a set carries `set` and `devotion: null`; `FAMILY_SETS` lists
    each set's dates per year so a page can find tonight's day. The build
    stops if the seasonal file gains a set `SETS` cannot place, or a set's
    length changes (the windows assume 25, 8, and 7 days).
  - Rotation: every other week takes the next Foundations devotion
    (`family-devotions.json` then `family-devotions-2.json`, file order, a
    repeated id used once and noted). It counts from `ROTATION_EPOCH`, Advent
    Sunday 2026, so the first week after Advent 2026 (Christmas week, December
    27) opens the rotation, weeks before count backward, and moving the map's
    first year never reshuffles anything (tested). The 70 unique ids cycle in
    about eighteen months today (46 rotating weeks a year; corrected in
    review, see above); all of them are used (tested).
  - Seasons with no family set yet (Christmas, Epiphany, Lent, Easter,
    Pentecost, All Saints) keep their season name and take the rotation, so
    no week is empty. When U43 (Lent) or U52 (Easter to Pentecost, All Saints)
    adds a set to `family-seasonal.json`, it adds one entry to `SETS` and
    regenerates; those weeks then go to the set.
  - Overrides: `client/public/family-year-overrides.json` (new, empty) pins
    `{ week: <Sunday>, devotion: <id in any family-devotions*.json>, note }`.
    A pin does not use up a rotation slot (the displaced devotion moves to the
    next week). Bad pins stop the build: not a Sunday, off the map, pinned
    twice, unknown id, unknown field, malformed date.
  - `horizonProblem(through, today)`: fails when the map ends less than twelve
    months after today.
- `scripts/build-family-year.mjs` (new). Thin CLI: writes the module, or with
  `--check` exits 1 if it is stale, an input has a problem, or under twelve
  months remain; `--today=YYYY-MM-DD` runs the horizon as of another date.
- `client/src/lib/familyYear.generated.ts` (new, generated, 30 KB source,
  4.3 KB gzipped). Types plus `FAMILY_YEAR_FROM`, `FAMILY_YEAR_THROUGH`,
  `FAMILY_ROTATION`, `FAMILY_SEASONS`, `FAMILY_SETS`, `FAMILY_WEEKS`. Nothing
  imports it yet, so no bundle changes in this unit.
- `client/src/lib/familyYear.ts` (new, not named in the card). A small reader
  so U13 and U17 do not each re-derive it: `familyWeekOn(date)`,
  `familySeasonOn(date)`, `familySetDayOn(date)` (`{ set, day }`, day 1 being
  the set's first day), `onFamilyMap`, and `localISODate()`, which reads the
  reader's own clock (at 9 pm in Michigan the UTC date is already tomorrow).
  Static, no network; call `localISODate` after mount.
- `.github/workflows/ci.yml`: `node scripts/build-family-year.mjs --check`
  added to the content-gates step, right after `validate-family.mjs`.
- Tests: `server/church-year.test.ts` (21) and
  `client/src/lib/familyYear.test.ts` (5). The roadmap named
  `scripts/lib/church-year.test.mjs`, but Vitest only collects `server/**`
  and `client/src/**`; the test sits in `server/` beside
  `help-printables.test.ts`, which tests `scripts/lib` the same way, so
  `vitest.workspace.ts` (shared with other units) is untouched.
