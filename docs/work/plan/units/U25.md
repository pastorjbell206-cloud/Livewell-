## What the 2026-10-12 fold decided, and why a topic page differs

On 2026-10-12 James folded `/justice`, `/disruption`, `/post-christian`,
`/exile`, the capture pages, and the `/pathways` index into shelf sections and
`/study`. They were categories with thin pages under them. A topic page is
not a category: it is one question with one assembled answer across formats
(a short answer, the essay to read first, Scripture read in its world, one
witness from church history, a devotion for the family table, and what to
use), and it exists only where no single page on the site already answers the
question. Where one page does (a care page, an Honest Answer, a study guide),
the `/topics` row opens that page and no topic page is built. Three pages,
at most six until search data argues for more (the gate enforces it). If
James reads the preview as the hub coming back, it does not ship.

One in, one out: adds `/topics` and three topic pages. Retires nothing yet:
the three pathways the plan pairs them with (`christian-nationalism`,
`progressive-captivity`, `parenting`) wait on question 6 and are linked from
the topic pages, not redirected (`folds: []` in each page).

## Deviations from the 4.4 shape (small, and why)

- `essay`, `essayStandIn`, `alsoEssays` are links `{ href, label, line }`,
  not slugs, so the children page can stand in with a care page
  (`essay: null`).
- `care` is a list of at most two (the children page needs both parenting
  and fatherless).
- Added: `section` (the post-Christian page's companion shelf section),
  `bothWays` (the politics page), `table.devotion: "tonight"`, `table.also`
  (the catechism), `use.paths` (the pathways, linked rather than folded).
- Minutes show only for a rewritten essay (its file is static); a database
  essay's minutes would need the API, and no topic page calls it.
