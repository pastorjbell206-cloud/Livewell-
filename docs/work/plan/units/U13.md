## What changed

**Order (`client/public/family-seasonal.json`).** "The Night Before" (Luke
2:1-5, the road to Bethlehem) moved from December 24 to December 20. "No Room"
(2:6-7), "Good News for Shepherds" (2:10-11), "Mary Treasured It" (2:19) and
"A Light to Everyone" (2:30-32) each moved one day later, to December 21 to 24.
Only `day` and `label` changed. Every title, passage, retelling, reflection,
question and prayer is word for word as it was. This order was chosen because
the journey is the only text that can come before the birth without
rewriting, and with it the calendar reads Luke 2 in order through Christmas
Eve. A reader may notice two things. Both were left alone because fixing them
means new writing:
- Day 20's question, "What are you most looking forward to about tomorrow?",
  was written for Christmas Eve. On December 20, tomorrow's reading is the
  birth, so it still makes sense. James may want to look at it.
- Simeon (Luke 2:30-32, forty days after the birth) now falls on Christmas
  Eve. His reflection ends "The long wait was nearly over," which fits that
  night. Christmas Day reads Luke 2:11 again (December 22 reads 2:10-11), as
  before.

**Booklet copy (`scripts/lib/help-printables.mjs`).** The Advent booklet's
cover keeps its title and subtitle ("Twenty-five days for families, December 1
to Christmas"). Below the paragraph on how to use it, it adds one paragraph:
"Advent begins on the fourth Sunday before Christmas, which falls between
November 27 and December 3. This booklet is an Advent calendar: one reading for
each day from December 1 to Christmas Day." It names no year because families
keep a printed booklet. `SEASON_META` takes an optional `note`; only Advent has
one. The booklet's days follow the new order, since it prints from the JSON.

**How-to copy (`scripts/articles/family-discipleship-howtos.json`, rebuilt
`client/public/howtos/a/fam-how-to-keep-advent-as-a-family.json`).** "How to
Keep Advent as a Family" said to light one candle "for each Sunday of December".
It also said to set the candles out "before the first Sunday of December" and
light the first "on that Sunday." In 2026 the first Sunday of December is
Advent's second Sunday. It now says "each Sunday of Advent" and "the first
Sunday of Advent." Only those two phrases changed.
`node scripts/build-howtos-index.mjs` rewrote only that one file.

Other Advent copy was checked and is accurate:
- the Advent study guide (`waiting-for-the-light.json`, "a short reading for
  each day from December 1 to Christmas Day")
- the family-devotions care page ("the four Sundays before Christmas")
- day 1's "We start Advent here, in the wreckage" (where in the story, not a
  date)
- Resources, Downloads, the catalogue and `llms.txt`

**Reading the year map (`client/src/lib/familyAdvent.ts`, new).**
- `adventSeasonFor(date)` finds the Advent whose calendar has not yet ended
  and returns three dates: Advent Sunday, December 1 and December 25. It reads
  them from `FAMILY_SEASONS` and `FAMILY_SETS`.
- `adventTonight(date)` returns that Advent from seven days before Advent
  Sunday through December 25, and null at any other time. It adds tonight's
  day (null before December 1) and whether Advent Sunday is still ahead.
- In years when Advent Sunday falls on December 2 or 3 (2028 and 2029), the
  calendar's first days come before Advent, and the copy says so.
- It needs no network, and U10's files are untouched.

**The surfaces (`client/src/components/AdventAtTheTable.tsx`, new).** One
component with two shapes. It renders nothing outside the window. The wording
for each part of the window, with 2026 dates:
- **November 22 to 28.** Home: "Advent begins Sunday, November 29. The family
  Advent calendar runs from December 1 to Christmas: see the readings." The
  `/family` card heading is "Advent begins Sunday, November 29", and its text
  is "The family Advent calendar has a short reading for each day from
  December 1 to Christmas, with a question to talk about and a prayer."
- **November 29 and 30** (U10's "nights with nothing"): "Advent has begun. The
  family Advent calendar starts Tuesday, December 1."
- **December 1 to 24.** Home: "Tonight at the table: December 6 in the family
  Advent calendar." The `/family` card heading is "Tonight, December 6", with
  the links Open the reading and All the readings.
- **December 25.** Home says "Christmas Day at the table: ..." and the card
  heading is "Christmas Day."
- The card always offers the booklet (US Letter, A4).

The plan's sketch in 3.2 ("Advent, day 6: ...") was not used. On December 6,
"Advent, day 6" says Advent began December 1, which is what the card forbids.

Home shows the date but not the day's title, because Home never fetches and
the generated map carries no titles. U18 also needs weekly devotion titles on
Home. It can add titles to the generated map and then put the day's title in
this line.

The date comes from the reader's own clock (`localISODate()`) on the client's
first render. The app mounts with `createRoot`, and the prerendered HTML never
contains the line, so this is after mount in the plan's sense. Rendering in
the first frame rather than in an effect keeps the doors from jumping.

**Home (`Home.tsx`).** One import and one line, at the top of the doors
section, right under the hero. The hero and the prerender are untouched.

**`/family` (`Family.tsx`).** One import, and the card between the hero and
the catechism card.

**`/family/devotions` (`FamilyDevotions.tsx`).**
- `?tab=advent` (or `thanksgiving`, `holy-week`) opens that tab. `&day=N`
  opens that day and scrolls to it once the data is in. An unknown season
  falls back to Weekly.
- Choosing a tab writes `?tab=` into the address with `replaceState`,
  following the StudyGuidesIndex and Explore pattern. A parent can then send
  "the Advent readings."
- Each seasonal day gets a DOM id (`advent-20`) and is marked "Tonight" when
  it is tonight's day of its set.
- The Advent tab gains one line above the booklet link: "Advent begins on the
  fourth Sunday before Christmas (November 29, 2026). This calendar has a
  reading for each day from December 1 to Christmas Day."
