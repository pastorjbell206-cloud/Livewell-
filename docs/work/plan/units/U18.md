# U18 Home: Find and tonight

Card (MASTER-PLAN 10.6, with 3.2, 9.8 and roadmap.json): add the Find field
(parser on focus) and one tonight line (the Advent day in season, otherwise
Tonight at the table) with Continue only when the device has something; the
doors row gains Family (Topics when U25 ships). No Today in Proverbs, no
questions list. Done when first paint is unchanged, Lighthouse on home is no
worse than after U08, and the tonight line is empty-safe.

Surface: one page (Home) and two library helpers. No API procedure, no
schema, no route added or moved, so the 9.3 checklist does not apply. The
static hero and `scripts/prerender-heads.mjs` are untouched.

One in, one out: Home's separate Advent line (U13's `AdventAtTheTable`
"line" variant) is folded into the tonight line and its code removed.

## What changed

- **Find** (`client/src/pages/home/HomeFind.tsx`, new). One labelled field
  in a search landmark, under the hero: "Look up a passage or ask a
  question", placeholder "John 3:16, or your question", and a Find button.
  It uses U15's `lib/siteSearch` exactly as the header does: the module and
  the reference parser are a dynamic import made on focus, never on mount.
  While a reference is typed, "Open John 3:16 in the Study Bible" appears
  under the field (44 px). Submitting goes through `findHref`: a chapter or
  verse to the Study Bible, anything else (a bare book name included) to
  `/search?q=`.
- **Tonight** (`client/src/pages/home/HomeTonight.tsx`, new). A pure
  `homeTonight(date)`, then the component:
  - An ordinary night: "Tonight at the table: *Strong Enough To Be Gentle*."
    The title links straight to the devotion page (`/family/devotions/<id>`),
    which has "Start at the table" on it. That is the two taps from Home.
  - A day of a daily set: "... *It Is Finished*, a reading for Holy Week."
  - A day of the Advent calendar, in U13's words: "Tonight at the table:
    *A Child Is Given*, December 6 in the family Advent calendar." On
    December 25 it is "Christmas Day at the table: ...". In a year when the
    calendar starts before Advent Sunday (2028), "Advent begins Sunday,
    December 3." comes first.
  - From the Sunday a week before Advent Sunday until the calendar starts,
    tonight's devotion and U13's Advent line beneath it (November 22 to 28,
    2026: Thanksgiving week's reading, then "Advent begins Sunday, November
    29. The family Advent calendar runs from December 1 to Christmas: see the
    readings."). On Advent Sunday and the Monday after (Nov 29 and 30), the
    year map hands tonight December 1's reading early (U17's `tonightOn`);
    Home does not repeat that and shows only "Advent has begun. The family
    Advent calendar starts Tuesday, December 1: see the readings." /family
    still shows the early reading in its Tonight card.
  - **Continue** beside the line, only when this device has something:
    "Continue the Life Audit", "Continue your eight-week plan".
  - Off the map with nothing to continue, the whole block renders nothing.
  - The clock and storage are read on the client's first render (a `useState`
    initializer, as U13 and U17 do): the app mounts with `createRoot`, the
    prerendered HTML never holds this line, and reading there keeps the doors
    from jumping. No fetch: a test asserts no family file is requested.
- **Titles without the network.** Home never fetches, and the year map
  carried only ids. `scripts/lib/family-year.mjs` now also reads each
  devotion's title and each set day's title, and `familyYear.generated.ts`
  gains `FAMILY_TITLES` (every devotion a week names, 72) and
  `FAMILY_SET_TITLES` (each day of the three sets). The generator refuses a
  named devotion or a set day with no title. `familyTable.ts` gains
  `tonightTitle(t)`. Regenerated; `build-family-year.mjs --check` passes.
- **Continue's source** (`lib/myProgress.ts`, `continueHere()`): the most
  recent of an eight-week plan not yet walked through (`lw-plan-<slug>`,
  fewer than 8 weeks done) and a self-check left part-way (its own
  `livewell-progress-*` key with answers saved after its last finished
  result in `livewell-history-<id>`, or never finished): Life Audit,
  Emotional Health, Spiritual Gifts, Marriage, Financial Health.
- **Home** (`client/src/pages/Home.tsx`): Find and Tonight at the top of the
  doors section, right under the hero, on the same cream (no new section,
  so the cream and black alternation is unchanged). A fourth door, "Family
  devotions" to `/family`: "A devotion for tonight's table, about ten minutes
  long: a passage, one idea to talk about, questions, and a prayer. There is
  one for every week of the year." It sits third, before The books.
- **`AdventAtTheTable.tsx`**: the "line" variant is removed (Home builds its
  line from the same `adventCopy`); the /family card is unchanged.
- **`index.css`**: a `.home-find` / `.home-tonight` block after the hero's.
  Tokens only, no hex; mustard only as link underlines. Inline links carry
  vertical padding for a 44 px target without changing the line.

New reader-facing copy is interface text only: the field's label and
placeholder, "Find", the tonight and Continue wording, and the Family door's
note. No em-dashes, nothing in James's first person, no biography.

## Not verified, or left out

- **On a phone, Find and Tonight are on the second screen.** They sit right
  under the hero (top at about 1,020 px at 390 x 844; at 844 px on a
  1440 x 900 screen, just below the fold). The hero is James's copy and the
  static hero U08 tuned; the card says it should not change. Bringing Find
  onto the first phone screen needs a shorter hero, which is a question for
  the lead and James, not this unit.
- **"christian nationalism goes to the topic."** It goes to `/search` today,
  where the existing essays answer it. The topic pages are U25, built in
  this wave; once they are a search source (or `findHref` learns topic
  slugs), the same field reaches them with no change here.
- **The Topics door on Home** waits for U25 to merge ("Topics when U25
  ships").
- **Continue does not offer essays or history sessions.** The site
  remembers only essays a reader finished (`markEssayRead` fires at the
  foot), never one begun, so there is nothing honest to continue; history
  sessions are U32. Plans show as "your eight-week plan" because their
  titles live in `/plans/plans-index.json`, a fetch Home does not make.
- **A self-check whose answers were changed and then put back exactly**
  records no new result (SelfCheckHistory skips identical answers), so it
  would read as part-way and offer Continue. Rare and harmless (the tool
  shows the answers), but known.
- **Parallel units touching the same files.** U11 ("year map feed behind the
  flag") and U21 (family JSON) may change `scripts/lib/family-year.mjs` or
  its inputs: after merging, run `node scripts/build-family-year.mjs` and
  commit the map, or `--check` fails. U19 changes the header, not Home's
  doors. `Family.tsx` is not touched here.
