# U15 Find: passage lookup and plain-language search

Card (MASTER-PLAN 10.6, with 3.2, 6.2 "Smaller", 7.1, 11): `lib/siteSearch.ts`
recognizes references first (to the verse), drops stop words for plain
questions, and adds wisdom topics, Honest Answers, family devotions and the
catechism, Christ and the Nation, and the essays override manifest to the
sources; the header search, `/search`, and a "Go to" field on the Study Bible
front door and reader. Parser loaded on focus. Done when "John 3:16,"
"jn 3 16," "how to talk to my kids about God," and "is america a christian
nation" each return the right thing in a test.

Surface: component and library code, one generated data file, one care-page
search phrase. No API procedure (search of rewrites is on the client, as plan
section 2 says), no schema, no route added or moved.

## What changed

- `client/src/lib/siteSearch.ts`
  - Passages: `loadPassageParser()` (dynamic import of `lib/scripture-ref`,
    its own 10 KB chunk; a failed load is forgotten so the next focus
    retries), `passageFor(q, parser)` (a reference to its verse or chapter;
    a book named in full on its own, "Romans", "1 John", "Psalm", to the
    book page; abbreviations alone, "ps" or "mk", are left to search), and
    `findHref(q)` (a chapter or verse straight to the Study Bible, anything
    else, a bare book included, to `/search?q=`). U18's Home Find field can
    call `findHref` as the header does.
  - Plain questions: `searchWords(q)` drops the small words of a question
    (how, to, my, about, is, a, what, does, say...) and keeps negatives
    ("cant", "not") and nouns like "will"; a light stem ("praying" finds
    "pray", "worries" finds "worry", "nations" finds "nation"); three word
    families (kids/children, mom/mother, dad/father), matched as whole words
    (review: "mom" no longer finds "moment"; "children's" joins the family).
  - Ranking: every content word must still appear. +20 when every word is in
    a care page's askedAs (the whole-phrase +40 stays first), and +5 per word
    that is a whole word of the title, so "nation" ranks "Was America Founded
    as a Christian Nation?" ahead of "...Christian Nationalism in America".
    "Honest Answers" joins the tie-break priorities at 2. `queryWords` and
    `hasWordStarts` (used by `/tools`, `/plans`, `/downloads`) are unchanged.
  - Sources as pure functions, used by `/search` and by the tests on the
    committed data: `libraryEntries` (moved from `Search.tsx`),
    `indexEntries` (the new index), `essayEntries` (the overrides manifest,
    public only).
- `scripts/build-search-index.mjs` (new, run with tsx because Honest Answers
  is a TS module; `pnpm run search:index`, `--check` in CI) writes
  `client/public/search-index.json`: wisdom topics, Honest Answers
  (`/answers#<slug>`), the 72 weekly family devotions, the catechism
  questions, and the 11 Christ and the Nation essays. Review: one entry per
  question per page, because the catechism asks five questions twice (in
  their part and again in the last) and both opened the same page as two
  identical results. 397 entries, 125 KB raw, 40 KB gzipped, fetched only by
  `/search`. Nothing in it is written copy; every line is read from the
  page's own source.
- `client/src/pages/Search.tsx`: fetches the index with the existing
  manifests (fail-soft); adds the public rewrites from
  `essayOverrides.generated.ts`; drops a server essay row whose slug has a
  rewrite search can show (review: only public rewrites, so no essay drops
  out of search entirely); loads the parser on mount and shows "In the Study
  Bible" with the passage first; "Nothing here" no longer shows when a
  passage was found; the result group is headed "Across the site"; new
  placeholder and empty-state line; result keys no longer collide. Review:
  a 16 px side gutter at phone width (`px-4` on the container; the page sat
  flush to the screen edge at 390 px before this unit, and U15 makes it the
  Find page).
- `client/src/components/MinimalNav.tsx` (header search): `lib/siteSearch`
  and the parser load when the field is focused (checked in `dist`: the entry
  chunk holds only the dynamic import, and `index.html` modulepreloads
  neither); Enter on a reference goes straight to the verse; while a
  reference is typed, a 44 px link "Open John 3:16 in the Study Bible"
  appears under the field; eyebrow, placeholder, and the desktop button's
  label say passage or question.
- `client/src/pages/study-bible/GoToPassage.tsx` (new), on the Study Bible
  front door and in the reader: a reference or a book opens; a verse of the
  chapter already on screen is selected and scrolled to in place; anything
