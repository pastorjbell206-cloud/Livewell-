# U14 Study primitives, first three (Sheet, Stepper, PageHead)

Card (MASTER-PLAN 10.6, with 8.1 and 8.2): `Sheet`, `Stepper`, `PageHead` with
`.st-` and `.pg-` styles and tests for keyboard and ARIA. Adopt `PageHead` on
one existing page to prove it. Files: `client/src/components/study/`,
`client/src/components/editorial/PageHead.tsx`, `index.css`. Done when the
tests pass and the proving page is screenshotted at both widths.

Nothing in the card waited on another unit or on an open James question; all
of it is built.

## What changed

Files: `client/src/components/study/Sheet.tsx` and `Sheet.test.tsx`,
`client/src/components/study/Stepper.tsx` and `Stepper.test.tsx`,
`client/src/components/editorial/PageHead.tsx` and `PageHead.test.tsx` (all
new), `client/src/index.css`, `client/src/pages/Study.tsx`.

- **Sheet** (`components/study/Sheet.tsx`). A bottom sheet on phones and
  tablets (max 720 px wide, at most 85% of the screen tall, body scrolls), a
  440 px panel at the right edge from 1024 px. Built on
  `@radix-ui/react-dialog`, already installed and chunked in `vendor-ui`
  (nothing imports `Sheet` yet, so no page's bundle changes until U16 does).
  Radix gives the focus trap, Escape, `role="dialog"` named by the title and
  described by the optional line, the page behind hidden from assistive
  technology (`aria-hidden` on siblings) and held still (scroll lock); the
  component adds `aria-modal="true"`, focuses the panel itself on open (so a
  screen reader starts at the title and Tab reaches Close next), and returns
  focus to whatever was focused when it opened, which matters for a
  controlled sheet that has no Radix trigger (VerseRef will be one). Visible
  "Close" text button (44 px), an optional pinned `footer` for actions ("Open
  in the Study Bible"). API: `open`/`onOpenChange` or `trigger`, `title`,
  `description`, `footer`, `closeLabel`.
- **Stepper** (`components/study/Stepper.tsx`). One step rendered at a time
  inside a named `role="group"`; Back and Next are real buttons in a bar that
  is sticky at the bottom of the step, so they stay in thumb reach while a
  long step scrolls; the step fills the screen less the 70 px header
  (`--st-stepper-top` lets U17's table mode drop the header). The bar says
  "2 of 5" (a position, never a score); the step heading is described by it.
  After a move, focus goes to the new step's heading (never on first render).
  Back is disabled on step one. On the last step Next becomes `onFinish`
  with `finishLabel` (U17: "We did this tonight"), or stays disabled when
  there is nothing to finish. Controlled (`step` + `onStepChange`, so U17 can
  keep the step in the URL) or `defaultStep`. No library.
- **PageHead** (`components/editorial/PageHead.tsx`). Eyebrow (the existing
  `.eyebrow`), the page's one H1, one line, optional `actions`, left-aligned
  on cream with a hairline under it; a `div`, not a landmark (the layout's
  `<main>` holds it). `titleRef` makes the H1 focusable without joining the
  tab order (for pages like My Library that move focus to their heading);
  `width` matches the column of the page below. Actions take the shared
  `pg-action` / `pg-action pg-action--quiet` classes.
- **index.css.** One new token, `--scrim` (the page dimmed behind a sheet,
  dark in both themes), in `:root`. New blocks after the editorial system:
  `.pg-head*`, a shared action style (`.pg-action`, `.st-action`, `--quiet`;
  48 px tall, bone on ink with the mustard rule, hover through `color-mix`
  of tokens so it also reads in dark mode), `.st-sheet*`, `.st-stepper*`.
  Entry movement uses `--dur`, `--motion-ease`, `--motion-rise`; the global
  reduced-motion guard removes it. No hex or `rgba()` outside the token.
- **The proving page: `/study`** (`pages/Study.tsx`). The charcoal prose hero
  is replaced by `PageHead` (eyebrow "Study"; the same title; the first
  sentence of the old paragraph as the one line). The old paragraph's second
  sentence (twenty centuries of Christians, the creeds, the ancient world) is
  dropped; the Church history group's own intro already says it, and no new
  copy was written. "The Study Bible" heading steps down from 40 to 60 px to
  32 to 44 px so it sits under the 36 to 56 px H1, and its section's top
  padding from 64 to 48 px. Section order is now cream (head and Study
  Bible), charcoal, cream-warm, cream: still alternating, never three alike.

One in, one out: this unit adds no reader-facing surface; it retires the
charcoal prose hero on `/study`. Why `/study`: it is a door James keeps (his
2026-10-26 answer), no other planned unit rebuilds it, and the parallel
units this week touch the family pages, the essay page and the Study Bible
reader, not this file.
