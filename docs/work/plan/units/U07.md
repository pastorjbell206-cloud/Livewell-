## The contract for U30 and U32

`verify-sources.mjs` reads every JSON file under
`client/public/history/sources/` and `client/public/history/sessions/`. Any
object with an `"excerpt"` key (except a `"type": "scripture"` block, which is
U27's) and every `"type": "source"` card is checked against the stored text
named by its own `"corpus"` key or the nearest enclosing object's. It is
matched against the author's text; an excerpt that quotes an editor's or
translator's note (Pliny's letter in McGiffert's note to Eusebius 3.33) carries
its own `"inNote": true` and is matched against the notes alone, and the card
should name the note's author or translator, not the ancient author's work
alone. An omission ("...") may pass over at most 2,000 characters. U30 can
shape `sources/<id>.json` freely so long as excerpts use those keys. The
registry's `sources.json` can carry the manifest's provenance fields by id.

### Found and fixed (commit a448388b)

- **An editor's note passed as the author's words.** Each note was searched
  as freely as the text, so "The blood of Christians is the seed of the
  Church." passed as a card citing Tertullian's *Apology*: a bracketed
  editor's note gives that form, while the text (chapter 50) reads "the blood
  of Christians is seed". Now an excerpt is matched against the author's text
  (passage 0) unless its card says `"inNote": true`, in which case it is
  matched against the notes alone; the failure says which side it was found
  on. `inNote` must be a boolean. Pliny's letter (in McGiffert's note) now
  carries the flag in the tests and in the contract above.
- **Omissions were unbounded.** Pieces either side of "..." only had to be in
  order somewhere in one passage, and a body passage is a whole book (City of
  God 5 is 115,000 characters). "the Christian emperors ... are to be
  worshipped" passed from pieces 112,000 characters apart, a claim Augustine
  argues against. Now each piece must begin within 2,000 characters (about
  350 words) of the end of the one before; every occurrence of a piece is
  tried, with memoized backtracking, so a later occurrence that fits is
  found. A whole Diognetus chapter 5 (about 2,000 characters) still passes
  with its middle left out.
- **`pnpm format` would have broken the gate.** `prettier --write .` reformats
  the 22 stored HTML texts (changing every sha256) and the generated
  `PROVENANCE.md` list. `scripts/data/history-sources/` is now in
  `.prettierignore` (an additive line; no sibling touches that file).
- **Docs.** `PROVENANCE.md` states both new rules, with the Tertullian example,
  and says plainly that the stored author's text still includes the editors'
  chapter headings and the italic summaries at the head of some letters,
  which the gate cannot tell apart. `SURVEY.md`: the Loeb Julian is three
  volumes, 1913 to 1923, with the letters in the third (it said 1913); and it
  no longer says every printed work is long out of copyright (the 1947 Rule of
  Benedict and Smith's Small Catechism are twentieth-century, as their rows
  already said).
- Tests: four new (the note rule on the real Tertullian and Pliny texts; the
  long-omission rule on City of God 5 and Diognetus 5; backtracking past a
  first occurrence that does not fit; a seeded session card quoting a note as
  the author, and a non-boolean `inNote`) and the existing ones updated for
  the note rule.
