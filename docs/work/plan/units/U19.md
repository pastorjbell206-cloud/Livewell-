Read the diff against 3.3, the card, NOTES (James's answer 3 of
2026-10-26), and this run's constraints. The map, the folds, the tests, the
footer, the Decision Log entry, and the llms.txt sentence hold up: every old

routes `/topics` and `/topics/:slug`, so `AWAITING_ITS_PAGE` becomes a no-op
once U25 is in.

Three real problems, all from turning About (a plain link until 2026-10-26),
Topics, and Family into menus. Each was seen in Chromium before the fix, and
each has a test in `siteNav.test.tsx` that fails without its fix (checked by
stashing the component):

1. **Desktop: hover, then click, folded the menu.** The door opens on
   mouseenter and the click toggled it, so a mouse user who moved to
   *About* and clicked it (the habit from when it was a link) saw the menu
   open and shut. Seen at 1440 x 900: hover opened it, the click closed it.
   Now the click right after a hover keeps that menu open; a second click,
   Enter from the keyboard, Escape, and leaving the door behave as before.
   An Android-style tap at 1024 wide still opens and then closes on a second
   tap.
2. **Desktop: no "you are here" on the menu doors.** On `/about` the About
   link used to be mustard with `aria-current="page"`; as a menu button it
   showed nothing, and neither did Books, Resources, Family, or Topics pages.
   The door that holds the page in view now reads as current
   (`--mustard-text` and `aria-current="true"`), chosen by its longest
   matching header link so `/resources/creeds` is Study, `/resources` is
   Topics, `/books/believe` is About.
3. **Phone: only the chevron opened a door.** The row's name was a plain
   span, so tapping "About" (or Topics, Family, Study) did nothing unless the
   tap hit the 42 px chevron at the right edge. With four of the five rows
   now menus, the whole row is the toggle (one button with the same
   accessible name, "Show About links"); `lineHeight: inherit` keeps the
   48 px row rhythm of the builder's version.
