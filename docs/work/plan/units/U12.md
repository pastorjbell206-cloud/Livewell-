# U12 Family repairs and the structural validator

Card (MASTER-PLAN 10.6, 5.8 in full, 5.6 id freeze, 9.2 rows for
`validate-family.mjs` and the id freeze test): duplicate devotion ids fixed and
then frozen; "72 weeks" copy; the catechism's `weekOfYear` cap so Q53 to Q76
appear; the catalogue's "52-question" copy; `LoadFailed` on the three family
pages; each surface's translation named; hardcoded `rgba()` in the heroes;
"Ages 1--3". Root `family-*.json` naming kept; the validator globs it and checks
unique ids and questions, counts matching copy, bands where claimed, the year
map resolving, one translation per surface, and em-dashes outside quoted
Scripture. Catechism duplicates flagged only (U40 removes them). Done when the
validator fails on a seeded duplicate id and passes on the repaired data, and
Q53 to Q76 reach "this week."

Adds no reader-facing surface, so it retires none (one in, one out does not
apply); it repairs the three family pages in place.

## What changed

**Data (`client/public/family-devotions-2.json`, three lines).**
- The second `forgiveness` (Colossians 3:13, "When Someone Hurts You") is now
  `when-someone-hurts-you`; the second `obedience` (John 14:15, "Trusting Enough
  To Obey") is now `trusting-enough-to-obey`. The twelve devotions in
  `family-devotions.json` keep their ids, since those are the ones `/family`
  shows and the likeliest to be printed first. No URL used devotion ids yet, so
  no redirect was owed.
- The Genesis 1:1 devotion was the second "Made On Purpose"; it is now "God
  Called It Good", taken from its own big idea ("...a God who meant to make it
  and called it good"). 5.4 names this collision under the de-duplication.
  This is the only new reader-facing wording in the data, three words.

**The freeze (`scripts/data/family-devotion-ids.json`, new).** All 72 ids after
the repair, in data order, with a one-line note. The family gate fails when a
recorded id leaves the data without a `vercel.json` redirect from
`/family/devotions/<old>` to `/family/devotions/<a devotion that exists>`, and
when a devotion's id is not yet recorded. `node scripts/validate-family.mjs
--freeze` appends new ids and never removes one. The redirect entry is the
site's ordinary `vercel.json` redirect, so U17 needs nothing extra for a renamed
card to land.

**The gate (`scripts/validate-family.mjs`, rewritten; still the CI step
`node scripts/validate-family.mjs`, so `ci.yml` is unchanged).**
- Globs `client/public/family-*.json`; a file's name gives its kind
  (`family-devotions*.json` devotions, catechism, seasonal, reading plans, and
  U10's planned `family-year-overrides.json`). An unknown `family-*.json` fails
  until it is given a kind and a translation.
- Unique devotion ids (URL-safe) across every devotion file, unique devotion
  titles, catechism numbered 1 to N with unique questions. The five exact
  repeats (Q8/Q64, Q11/Q56, Q21/Q58, Q47/Q61, Q48/Q62) are listed in
  `KNOWN_CATECHISM_REPEATS` and printed as notes; a new repeat fails, and a pair
  that stops repeating must come off the list (so U40 updates it). The near
  repeats (Q49/Q53, Q50/Q54, Q45/Q63) are worded differently and are not caught
  by an exact match; U40 handles them by reading.
- Bands where claimed: a devotion whose `questions` is an object must carry both
  `younger` and `older` (`together` optional), the 5.2 shape U21 will use; every
  catechism entry carries its child's answer.
- Counts match copy: each reading plan's "N days" equals its days, and every
  "N questions" said of the catechism (within 160 characters of the word
  "catechism", or in a Catechism file) and every "N weekly devotions" in
  `client/src/pages`, `client/src/components`, `build-catalogue.mjs`,
  `help-printables.mjs`, `llms.txt`, and `index.html` must equal the data.
  Run against the base commit it reports the catalogue's "52-question" and the
  devotions page's "52 weekly devotions".
- One translation per surface: `PAGES` names each family page and the libraries
  it shows; each page's `<ScriptureNote rendering=...>` must name exactly those
  libraries' translations (BSB for devotions and catechism, paraphrase for the
  seasonal retellings, none for the references-only reading plans).
- No em-dash, and no `--` standing in for one, anywhere in the family files
  outside quoted Scripture (devotion `passageText`, catechism `scriptureText`)
  and other quoted spans. The seven em-dashes in the files are all in quoted BSB.
- The year map: once `client/src/lib/familyYear.generated.ts` exists, every id
  it names under a `devotion:` or `devotionId:` key must exist, and a map with
  no such keys fails with "teach scripts/validate-family.mjs how to read the
  year map." Absent today; the gate says "year map not generated yet."
- The BSB checks are unchanged from the first version.

**Pages.**
