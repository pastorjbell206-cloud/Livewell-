## What changed

**Data and build.**
- `scripts/lib/story-plan.mjs` (new) and `scripts/build-story-plan.mjs` (new)
  write `client/public/bible/guides/whole-story-2027.json` (new, 100 KB raw,
  30 KB gzip). The story path (all 1,189 chapters in story order) is cut into
  365 days by dynamic programming: least squares on each day's word count
  (Berean Standard Bible words from the text shards) against the average of
  1,988, never splitting a chapter and never crossing an act, so each of the
  eleven acts begins on a day of its own. Days run 1,346 to 2,655 words; Psalm
  119 is a day alone (2,291), the Songs of Ascent share one (2,162). Day 1 is
  Genesis 1-2, day 365 Revelation 20-22.
- Each of the 41 context guides (`/resources/context/:slug`) sits on the day
  that reads its first key text, parsed with `readRef` from
  `scripts/lib/bsb.mjs` (the shared parser). 32 days carry one or more; the
  most on one day is three (Exodus 20-22).
- The file carries each day's chapter runs, a label ("Genesis 1-2; Psalm 90";
  a one-chapter book by its name alone), chapter titles (from
  `notes-index.json`), the act, and the guides; act titles come from the
  storyline. No new writing.
- `--check` regenerates and compares byte for byte, and runs the soundness
  check (365 days; every chapter once, in story order; each day inside one
  act; each act beginning on its own day; each guide on one real day). Added
  to the CI content-gates step after `build-bible-text --check`.

**Problems found and fixed.**
1. *The reader's place was the first gap, which is a debt.* `nextUnreadDay`
   returned the first day not marked read, so a reader who joins in 2027 on
   today's day (the page's own default) and marks days 40, 41, 42 was sent
   back to day 1 on every visit ("Your place on this device: day 1"), and My
   Library said "Day 1 is next". That is the "how far behind" the page and
   section 2 forbid. Now `readerPlace` is the day after the furthest day read;
   when the last day is read but some are not, the first of those; null only
   when all 365 are read. Page, opening day and My Library use it. Tests: a
   late joiner opens on day 43; `[1, 2, 4]` gives 5; `[1, 2, 365]` gives 3.
2. *The open day's month could not be closed.* The year list forced that
   month open during every render, so tapping its summary closed and reopened
   it. Now the month opens only when the day changes (a step, a pick, today's
   day). Test: January closes; checked in Chromium at both sizes.
3. *"Psalms 51" under a heading that says "Psalm 51".* The day's chapter rows
   and the month spans used the book name; they now say "Psalm 51" as the
   label does. And 121 psalm titles from `notes-index.json` repeat the
   reference ("Psalm 51: Create in Me a Clean Heart" under "Psalm 51"); the
   generator now drops the repeat (`chapterTitle` in
   `scripts/lib/story-plan.mjs`), and the plan was regenerated. Tests on both.

   44 px or more (measured 44 at 1440, 53 at 390 for a two-line title).

   picked from the list). Now "begins here".
