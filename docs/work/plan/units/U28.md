## What changed

**Data and build.**
- `scripts/lib/story-plan.mjs` (new) and `scripts/build-story-plan.mjs` (new)
  write `client/public/bible/guides/whole-story-2027.json` (new, 100 KB raw,
  30 KB gzip). The story path (all 1,189 chapters in story order) is cut into
  365 days by dynamic programming: least squares on each day's word count
  (Berean Standard Bible words from the text shards) against the average of
  1,988, never splitting a chapter and never crossing an act, so each of the
  eleven acts begins on a day of its own. Days run 1,346 to 2,655 words; Psalm
  119 is a day alone (2,291), the Songs of Ascent share one (2,162). Day 1 is
  Genesis 1-2, day 365 Revelation 20-22.
- Each of the 41 context guides (`/resources/context/:slug`) sits on the day
  that reads its first key text, parsed with `readRef` from
  `scripts/lib/bsb.mjs` (the shared parser). 32 days carry one or more; the
  most on one day is three (Exodus 20-22).
- The file carries each day's chapter runs, a label ("Genesis 1-2; Psalm 90";
  a one-chapter book by its name alone), chapter titles (from
  `notes-index.json`), the act, and the guides; act titles come from the
  storyline. No new writing.
- `--check` regenerates and compares byte for byte, and runs the soundness
  check (365 days; every chapter once, in story order; each day inside one
  act; each act beginning on its own day; each guide on one real day). Added
  to the CI content-gates step after `build-bible-text --check`.

**Library code.**
- `client/src/lib/storyPlan.ts` (new): the plan's types and guarded fetch
  (cached for the session); `planDayFor(date)` (January 1, 2027 is day 1,
  counted on calendar days so a clock change cannot shift it); `dateForDay`,
  `longDate`, `shortDate`, `planMonths`; `dayFromQuery` (`?day=40`, only a
  real day); `chaptersOf`; the reader's place on the device (`readPlanDays`,
  `setPlanDay`, `nextUnreadDay`); `openingDay` (the address, then the reader's
  place, then today while 2027 runs, then day 1).
- `client/src/components/RemindMe.tsx`: one optional prop, `start`, passed to
  the `Reminder` that `lib/calendar.ts` already supported, so the reminder can
  begin on January 1. `lib/calendar.ts` itself needed no change.
- `client/src/lib/myProgress.ts`: My Library's "Pick up where you left off"
  lists the plan ("Reading plan · Day 4 is next") once this device has marked
  a day. Position only.

**The page** `client/src/pages/StudyBibleYear.tsx` (new) at
`/study/bible/guides/whole-story-2027`, with literal SEOMeta:
- `PageHead` (eyebrow to the Study Bible, one-line lede), sized to the column
  below.
- One day's card (cream-warm band, white card): "Day 40 of 365 · Tuesday,
  February 9" (`Position`), the day's label as its heading, its act (on the
  day an act begins: "Act Four begins today: Exodus and Sinai. Read what
  happens and the world behind it first.", linked to the act page), the
  chapters as 52 px rows opening the Study Bible on the story path
  (`?path=story`), "In its world" with the day's context guides, "Mark day N
  read" (then "Day N read"), Day N-1 and Day N+1, the reader's place and
  today's day when they differ from the open day (plain buttons; no count
  behind, no streak, no percentage), "Your place is kept on this device.
  Nothing is sent to LiveWell.", and `RemindMe` (daily: before 2027 it starts
  January 1 and runs 365 days; during 2027 from tomorrow through December 31;
  after 2027 for a year).
- The day shown is the one in the address; otherwise the day the page opened
  on stays put while the reader marks it read (it does not jump ahead under
  the thumb). Changing day replaces the address (`?day=41`), so a day can be
  shared and Back still leaves the page.
- "The year, day by day": twelve months as `details`; only open months render
  their rows (a month of rows in the page, not a year). Each row is a 44 px
  check (aria-pressed, "Day N read") and the day itself; picking one opens it
  in the card, scrolls to it, and moves focus to its heading.
- "How the days are made": two short paragraphs.
- `LoadFailed` with a retry if the plan does not arrive; the shared
  `SAVE_FAILED` line if the device will not store.
- Styles: a `.sy-` block in `client/src/index.css` (placed just before the
  `.tb-` block), tokens only, no hex.
