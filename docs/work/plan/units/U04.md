**James: please open https://www.crossway.org/permissions/ and paste the

Quotation limits noted at the same time (same method, same caveat): the ESV

Standard Version" are registered trademarks of Crossway. One result also said
the ESV text may not be quoted in a publication made available under a
Creative Commons license; another search could not confirm that sentence on

## Which essays carry the notice

`owesEsvNotice(body, { rewrite })`:

- A **rewrite** (`client/public/essays/<slug>.json`) is written to the house
  rule (ESV verbatim, BRIEF), so it carries the notice once it quotes
  Scripture: a closing quotation mark (italics allowed) followed by a
  parenthesized chapter-and-verse reference, or a block quotation ending in
  one. The loose reference shape is a stand-in for U09's parser. Note for U05:
  the pilots' closing texts currently set the reference after a dash rather
  than in parentheses; once U05 rewrites them to `*"Quote."* (Reference)`
  (NOTES) they match too, and every pilot already matches through its in-text
  quotations.
- An **older database essay** carries it only where its own text names the ESV.

Why the second rule: the 15 live shelf essays were read from production
(read-only, `posts.getBySlug` through the Vercel connector, 2026-10-26). Eight
quote Scripture with a reference; none names a translation. Most of the
wording reads like the ESV (from memory, not checked), but at least one does
not: Amos 5:21-24 in `how-the-bible-gets-translated-by` ("let justice roll on
like a river") reads like the NIV. Putting Crossway's line under it would be a
false claim, and an agent cannot certify the others. So today **no live essay
shows the notice**; the pilots will, as soon as U05 adds them. The live
essays' translations belong to question 4 (rewrite or keep): a kept essay gets
the notice by naming the ESV in its text, or by a rewrite.

### Findings

1. **Fixed: the closed contents card was an 18.7 px tap target.** Its only
   door, the `<summary>`, was one line of 11 px text, with the card's padding
   on `<details>`. U04 made the card closed by default on a phone, so that
   line became the only way into the contents (plan 8.6 and 9.7: 44 px
   targets). The padding moves onto the summary (`16px 20px`) and the list
   (`0 20px 16px`). The whole closed card is now the target (50.7 px), and it
   looks the same open or closed: every first-paragraph position measured
   before and after the fix is identical to the pixel. Asserted in
   `ArticleDetail.test.tsx`.
2. **Open, for James: the notice may be missing a last line.** Two searches
   (2026-10-26, one limited to crossway.org and esv.org) report that the
   current permissions page ends the notice with "ESV Text Edition: 2025."
   The builder saw that line but set it aside as a variant. I did not add it:
   the page was not opened (search summaries only), and the line names the
   text edition the essays quote, which nothing has checked yet. U27's API
   check against api.esv.org would settle that. The comment above
   `ESV_NOTICE` now says this. James's paste of the page decides both the
   wording and the line.
3. **Note: the older-essay rule can overclaim in one case nothing hits today.**
   For a database essay, naming the ESV anywhere is enough to print the
   notice. An essay that compares translations ("the ESV renders this, the
   KJV that") would then say all its quotations are ESV. None of the 15 live
   essays names the ESV (the builder's production read; esvNotice false
   throughout). Crossway's own forms for this case are "(ESV)" after each
   quotation, or a notice beginning "Unless otherwise indicated". I left the
   rule as built. Question 4 (rewrite or keep the live essays) decides what
   those essays need.
4. **Note: `GeneratedHero.tsx` is now orphaned.** Nothing imports it (only
   comments in `EditorialBlocks.tsx` mention it). U06's patch does not
   remove it; a later cleanup can.
5. **Note: a fifth Scripture-reference shape.** `esv.ts` has a loose
   `REFERENCE` regex, labelled as a stand-in. U09's `scripture-ref.ts`
   exports `parseScriptureRef` (one whole reference) but no scanner over
   running text, so there is nothing to swap in yet. When U23 adds text
   scanning, `quotesScripture` should use it.
6. **Seen, not U04's:** on a phone the long kicker ("Faith and Power:
   Christian Nationalism and Politics") wraps to two lines, and its underline
   runs nearly the full column. Main does the same.
