# Audit: Prophetic Justice Pillar

Auditor pass against the James Bell Scholarship standard. Twenty articles across four files: economic-justice.json, race-reconciliation.json, the-vulnerable.json, systemic-sin.json. Scored, flagged, and verified. No article files were modified.

Dimensions keyed: 1 Thesis/Architecture, 2 Gospel/Doctrine, 3 Biblical Spine, 4 Historical Integrity, 5 Argumentative Validity, 6 Data Integrity, 7 Scholarly Witnesses, 8 Voice Fidelity, 9 Voice Discipline, 10 Prose Vitality/Legibility.

Bands: TRADITION (apex), STRONG, DEVELOPING, FAILING.

**Pillar verdict: STRONG, with one cluster of articles touching TRADITION.** This is the most consistently excellent pillar one could ask for under this rubric. The MTSO arc is executed with discipline in nearly every piece: open in motion, steel-man the strongest objection and answer that version, one biblical spine pressed throughout, historical root traced with named figures, an earned reversal, the cross inside the body with law before grace, a verdict ending that leaves weight. Surface discipline is near-perfect (no em dashes, no semicolons, no exclamation marks detected). The skeptic is treated fairly throughout; the conservative and secular objections are genuinely steel-manned, not strawmanned. The principal weakness is a recurring architectural sameness across the twenty (the "carry one spine," "let me steel-man," "now the reversal," "this is where the cross does its work, law before grace" scaffold is visible enough that, read in sequence, the seams show), plus one soft date and a scatter of minor banned words.
