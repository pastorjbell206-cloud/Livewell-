# Living Well

The everyday-theology core that gives the site its name. These essays take the
weight of ordinary life — work, money, the body, friendship, death — and bring
the depth of the Christian tradition to bear on it, in a frame any thoughtful
reader can enter, churched or not.

## Register

This cluster is written in the **modern/accessible register** — the warm,
inviting voice modeled on writers like John Mark Comer, built on Keller's
architecture: open in a real, ordinary situation; state a bold thesis early
and in bold; then build the argument with load-bearing, dated scholars and
Scripture carried in naturally. The endings leave weight; they do not resolve.

This is not the prophetic-indictment register of the *Capture* and *After
Christendom* clusters. It is the same convictions, addressed to an individual
reader in their own ordinary week, with the door held open rather than the
floor cleared.

Every essay:
- opens in a concrete, ordinary situation (varied openings — no two alike)
- states a bolded thesis early
- carries 4–6 Scripture references, woven in, never proof-texted
- leans on named, dated scholars as structural witnesses
- closes on weight the reader carries out

## Voice rules (from CLAUDE.md — non-negotiable)

The forbidden words, phrases, and therapy-speak in CLAUDE.md apply in full. In
addition, the title and body register for this cluster specifically avoids:
no Latin in titles, no colon-subtitles, no "Not X. Y." in titles; no exclamation
marks; no signposting; no reader-directives ("ask yourself," "imagine if you");
no announced honesty ("let me be honest"). Titles are clear, direct, and
newcomer-legible.
