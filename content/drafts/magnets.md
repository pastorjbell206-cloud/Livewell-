# Lead Magnets — Ten Resources in Ship Order

Each magnet captures email, routes the subscriber into the right welcome
sequence, and points back to the relevant book. Each is designed as a PDF
(serious typesetting, real cover, Bell name on it). Lengths vary 12–40
pages depending on the content.

The magnets are the engine of email-list growth. The strategic doc says:
ship them in this order over the next twelve months.

---

## Magnet 1 — *The Skeptic's First Seven Essays*

**Slug:** `the-skeptics-first-seven-essays`
**Length:** ~32 pages designed PDF
**Track:** `doubt` · **Audience tag at signup:** `skeptic`

**Positioning:** The skeptic on-ramp. Seven essays in argument order, designed for one-sitting reading. The "From One Former Skeptic to Another" framing. Points the reader at Book 3 (*The Christian and the Skeptic*) and at the seven essays still going up at `/skeptic-track`.

**Contents:**
- Preface (1 page): one paragraph from Bell on why he is writing to the skeptic specifically. The "I came to faith from atheism at twenty-four" hook.
- Essay 1: The Questions That Actually Matter (5 pages)
- Essay 2: What Secular Explanations Still Have to Explain (5 pages)
- Essay 3: The Problem of Suffering, Honestly (5 pages)
- Essay 4: The Historical Jesus, Without the Shortcuts (5 pages)
- Essay 5: The Bible Without the Marketing (4 pages)
- Essay 6: Morality Without God — And With Him (4 pages)
- Essay 7: What Following This Actually Costs (3 pages)
- One-page reading list at the back: 12 books to read next, with one sentence each on why.

**Welcome sequence:** 5 emails over 4 weeks. Day 0: confirmation + link. Day 3: "Did you read essay 1? Here is the line that has mattered most to me." Day 7: essay 2 follow-up + Q&A invitation. Day 14: "Bell's path from atheism — a personal note." Day 28: book pitch for *The Christian and the Skeptic*.

**Slug:** `the-after-christendom-reading-list`

**Track:** `after-christendom` · **Audience tag at signup:** general

**Welcome sequence:** 4 emails. Each one delivers Bell's notes on 3-4 books from the list, plus the link to that essay on `livewellbyjamesbell.co`.

## Magnet 6 — *Marriage Conversation Cards*

**Slug:** `marriage-conversation-cards`
**Length:** 25 cards as PDF
**Track:** `marriage` · **Audience tag at signup:** `couple`

**Positioning:** 25 questions for couples to work through together over a weekend. Not the Esther Perel questions (covered elsewhere). The deeper questions the marriages that fail mostly never ask. Some are gentle; others are sharp. The cards are designed for couples to use in pairs — read, answer, reverse roles.

**Contents:**
- Preface (2 pages): how to use the cards. How not to.
- 25 questions, one per page, each with a short Bell note on what the question is really asking.
- Sample questions:
  - "What does your spouse do, when you are honest, that triggers your father?"
  - "What is the thing you have not said because you do not believe it would be received?"
  - "Where in our marriage am I performing rather than being known?"
  - "What did we agree to without ever discussing it?"
- A closing page: how to bring the conversations back into the rhythm of the marriage.

**Welcome sequence:** 5 emails over 5 weeks — each one introducing the next 5 questions, with Bell's pastoral notes on each.

## Notes on production

- Each magnet needs a designer pass. Budget $200–$500 per PDF for serious typesetting (the brand cannot ship Canva templates).
- Cover treatment: cream background, mustard rule, Cormorant title, charcoal author block. Consistent across all 10.
- Distribution: each magnet lives at `livewellbyjamesbell.co/free/<slug>` (already routed via `/lead-magnets/:magnetId`).
- Email infrastructure: Resend ($20/mo) or Substack — match the elite strategy doc's recommendation. ConvertKit if scaling past 5,000 subscribers.
- Conversion tracking: every magnet's signup is a tagged subscriber row in the DB. The audience tag drives the welcome sequence selection.
- Ship order: 1, 2, 3, 4 in the first 90 days. 5, 6, 7 in months 4–6. 8, 9, 10 in months 7–12.
