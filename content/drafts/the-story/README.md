# The Whole Story of the Bible

A five-essay cluster for the reader who has never heard the Bible as one story —
the skeptic, the curious, the lifelong churchgoer who knows the verses and has
never been shown the plot. Modern, accessible register (closer to John Mark
Comer than to the long-form prophetic essays), Keller architecture (open in the
real question, bold thesis early and bolded, build), warm and inviting, with the
self-implication and weight-bearing endings the brand requires.

This is a metanarrative cluster. Each essay stands alone, but they are written
to be read in order: one story, what broke it, how God began to fix it, the
moment it turned, how it ends.

## Notes for editing

- Each essay opens with one `> [PERSONAL STORY — James to supply]` placeholder
  near the top, with a specific suggestion and a drop-in marker. No private
  family scenes have been invented; all such material is left as placeholder or
  kept to public facts.
- Voice gate has been run clean against the full forbidden list: no forbidden
  words or phrases, no therapy-speak, no exclamation marks, no signposting, no
  reader-directives, no announced honesty. Scripture quotations follow standard
  English translations; one Revelation 21:3 quotation uses "Behold" rather than
  the NIV "Look!" to keep the no-exclamation rule clean.
- Titles follow the approved register: clear, direct, newcomer-legible, no Latin,
  no colon-subtitles, no "Not X. Y."
