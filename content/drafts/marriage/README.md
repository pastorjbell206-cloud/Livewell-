## Before publishing — required

**Fill every `[PERSONAL STORY]` placeholder with true material.** Each essay contains a placeholder on its own line marked `> [PERSONAL STORY — James to supply ...]`. These mark the spots where a specific personal scene belongs. They were left blank on purpose: the drafting brief forbids inventing private details about James's wife, marriage, or children. Do not publish with a placeholder still in the body, and do not let an editor fabricate the scene — these must be replaced with a true account in James's own words.

Public biographical facts (came to faith from atheism, raised without a father, five sons, working pastor) were used freely. Private marital and family scenes were not invented.
