# Parenting Track — Drafts

These are AI first drafts in the voice of James Bell, written against the Voice
& Tone, Forbidden Language, and Structural Moves in `CLAUDE.md`. They are
scaffolding, not finished essays. Every file is `status: draft` and awaits
James's voice pass before it goes anywhere near publish.

## Before publishing

1. **James's voice pass.** Read each essay aloud. The rhythm, the verdicts, and
   the self-implication should sound like you, not like a competent imitation of
   you. Cut what rings false. The draft is a starting block, not a finish line.

2. **Fill every `[PERSONAL STORY]` placeholder.** Each essay contains a
   `> [PERSONAL STORY — James to supply a true account ...]` block where lived
   material belongs. These exist because the writing guidelines forbid
   fabricating private details about James's wife or individual sons. A
   placeholder beats a fabrication. **Do not publish with a placeholder still in
   the text.** Replace each one with a true account, or cut the block and rework
   the surrounding paragraph.

3. **Voice check.** Confirm none of the forbidden words or phrases slipped in,
   no exclamation marks, no signposting, no reader-directives, no announced
   honesty. The publish-voice check rejects on these.
