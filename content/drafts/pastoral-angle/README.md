# The Pastoral Angle — Draft Essays

These are **AI first drafts** for the pillar *The Pastoral Angle* — bridge essays that
connect the political-capture thesis (see the *Capture by the Left* and *After Christendom*
pillars) to the actual work of pastoring real people through it. They are raw material
awaiting James's own voice pass — not for publication as written. Every file is marked
`status: draft` in its frontmatter.

The pillar's task is peer-to-peer, never preachy. The audience is working pastors and lay
leaders, and the register is candid and unsentimental — pastor to pastor — rather than
provocative. The writer is always inside the indictment ("we pastors," "I"), never pointing
from outside it. Where the political pillars name the idolatry, this pillar asks the harder
question: now that your congregation, your pulpit, your board, and your own heart have been
shaped by it, what do you actually do on Monday.

Each essay opens in a real ministry situation, states its thesis early (bolded), and builds
to an earned, weight-bearing conclusion in the Keller architecture. Eugene Peterson on the
pastoral vocation runs throughout as the load-bearing witness, joined by the scholars noted
below. Each carries one `> [PERSONAL STORY — James to supply]` placeholder near the top for
James to render from his own ministry; the drafts invent no private scenes.
