# Faith Crisis — Drafts (Doubt track)

These five essays are AI first drafts written toward the LiveWell voice. They
are not finished work. They exist to give James a page to react to instead of a
blank one. Every file carries `status: draft` and must pass through James's
voice pass before it goes anywhere near publish.

## What needs to happen before any of these ships

1. **Voice pass by James.** The drafts honor the documented voice (the "Not X.
   Y." period, short-long-short, self-implication, verdicts, endings that leave
   weight) and avoid the forbidden words and phrases. They still need the human
   ear that knows where a sentence is one beat too clever or one beat short of
   true. Read each one out loud. Cut what performs.

2. **Fill every `[PERSONAL STORY]` placeholder with true material.** This is
   non-negotiable. The drafting brief forbade inventing private scenes with
   named family. Each essay contains one placeholder line marking where a real,
   true account from James belongs. **Do not publish with a placeholder still in
   the file, and do not let the drafting fill one in — these must be James's own
   true accounts.**

   - `when-fear-rewrites-theology.md` — a season fear shaped his theology
   - `when-god-doesnt-make-sense.md` — a time the equation broke
   - `what-if-we-are-wrong.md` — his hardest night with the question
   - `dark-night-god-feels-absent.md` — his own dry season
   - `constantines-bargain.md` — watching the bargain at work in his ministry

   Five placeholders total, one per essay.

3. **Verify the scholarship.** The drafts name load-bearing witnesses (Charles
   Taylor, *A Secular Age*; Tom Holland, *Dominion*; James Davison Hunter, *To
   Change the World*; Lesslie Newbigin; John of the Cross; Jonathan Haidt;
   Eugene Peterson; Tim Keller; Bonhoeffer). Confirm every attribution before
   publish. Confirm the Scripture references read as James reads them.
