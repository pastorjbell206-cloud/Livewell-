# Why Bother With Church?

A five-essay long-form cluster for the dechurched person — the one who is done with the institution but not with Jesus. Written to honor the wound and refuse the conclusion that faith can be fully solitary. Warm, never scolding. Keller-style architecture (open in the real ache, bold thesis early, build to a weight-bearing ending). Modern/accessible register.

- Track: `theology`
- Audience: `individuals`
- Format: `essay`
- Status: `draft`

## Essays

1. **What If You're Done With Church but Not With Jesus?** — `done-with-church-not-jesus.md`
   Names the "dechurched but believing" reality without shame.
   Scholars: Jim Davis & Michael Graham, *The Great Dechurching* (2023); Charles Taylor, *A Secular Age* (2007).
   Scripture: John 6:66–68; Hebrews 10:24–25; Psalm 27:4.

2. **Can You Be a Christian on Your Own?** — `can-you-be-a-christian-alone.md`
   Why the New Testament knows no solo Christian, gently argued.
   Scholars: Dietrich Bonhoeffer, *Life Together* (1939); Joseph Hellerman, *When the Church Was a Family* (2009).
   Scripture: 1 Corinthians 12:12–27; Acts 2:42–47; Ephesians 2:19–22.

3. **When the Church Is the Thing That Hurt You** — `when-the-church-is-what-hurt-you.md`
   Church hurt named honestly; lament without excusing it; the bride vs. her failures.
   Scholars: Diane Langberg, *Redeeming Power* (2020); Chuck DeGroat, *When Narcissism Comes to Church* (2020).
   Scripture: Ezekiel 34; Matthew 18:6; Psalm 55:12–14.

4. **What Is Church Actually For?** — `what-is-church-actually-for.md`
   Not a service to consume — a body, a family, a witness.
   Scholars: Lesslie Newbigin; Stanley Hauerwas & William Willimon, *Resident Aliens* (1989).
   Scripture: Ephesians 4:11–16; Acts 2:42–47; John 13:34–35; 1 Peter 2:9–10.

5. **How Do You Find a Church Worth Joining?** — `how-to-find-a-church-worth-joining.md`
   Practical and honest — what to look for, what to forgive, what not to.
   Scholars: Eugene Peterson; Rosaria Butterfield, *The Gospel Comes with a House Key* (2018).
   Scripture: Acts 2:42; Hebrews 13:7; 1 Timothy 3; Romans 15:7.
