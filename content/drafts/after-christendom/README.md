# The Church After Christendom

Pillar series. Five long-form essays tracing the civilizational shift away from Christendom and holding hope without nostalgia. The argument across the set: what is ending in the West is not the Christian faith but the cultural arrangement that made it the default — and that ending, rightly read, returns the church to the conditions under which it first grew.

**Track:** `after-christendom`
**Audience:** individuals
**Format:** essay
**Status:** draft
**Register:** modern and accessible (Comer-style readability) over Keller architecture — steel-man the opposing or comfortable view first, state a bold thesis early and in bold, build to an inevitable conclusion. Edge is sober clarity and earned hope. Not fear-mongering, not nostalgia, not triumphalism. Self-implicated throughout.

## The essays

1. **`christendom-is-ending.md`** — "Christendom: A 1,700-Year Story That Is Ending." The arc from Constantine (312) and Theodosius (380) to now; what Christendom was and why it is ending. Scholars: Charles Taylor (2007), Tom Holland *Dominion* (2019), Alan Kreider (2016), Stuart Murray (2004). Scripture: Daniel 2, Matthew 13, John 18:36.

2. **`exile-is-not-the-end.md`** — "Exile Is Not the End." Jeremiah 29 and building in Babylon; exile as the church's actual posture, not catastrophe. Scholars: Walter Brueggemann *Cadences of Home* (1997), Lesslie Newbigin. Scripture: Jeremiah 29:4–7, Daniel 1, 1 Peter 1:1 / 2:11, Psalm 137.

3. **`witness-without-power.md`** — "Witness Without Power." The early church grew with no political power at all; weakness was the method. Scholars: Rodney Stark *The Rise of Christianity* (1996), Alan Kreider (2016), Stanley Hauerwas. Scripture: Acts 2–4, 1 Corinthians 1:18–31, 2 Corinthians 12:9.

4. **`what-dies-when-christendom-dies.md`** — "What Dies When Christendom Dies — and What Doesn't." Cultural Christianity versus biblical faith; what is worth grieving and what isn't. Scholars: Lesslie Newbigin, Dietrich Bonhoeffer (*Letters and Papers from Prison*), Charles Taylor (2007). Scripture: John 12:24, Matthew 16:25, Hebrews 12:27.

5. **`the-numbers-behind-the-decline.md`** — "The Numbers Behind the Decline, and the Ones That Lie." Reading the sociology carefully — the "nones," disaffiliation — what the data does and does not say. All statistics hedged with ranges and attribution. Scholars: Christian Smith, Ryan Burge *The Nones* (2021), Robert Putnam, Charles Taylor (2007). Scripture: Judges 7, 1 Kings 19, Matthew 7:13–14.
