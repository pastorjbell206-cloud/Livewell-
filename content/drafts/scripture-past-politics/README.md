This folder holds the long-form essays for the **"Reading Scripture Past Our
Politics"** pillar: the method under the whole project — teaching the reader to
see the lenses before they read a word. Track: `theology`. Audience:
`individuals`. Register: modern/accessible (Comer) with Keller architecture
(steel-man first, bold thesis early and bolded, build to an inevitable
conclusion). The edge here is teaching clarity and counterintuitive insight,
not provocation — these are formative / hermeneutics essays.
