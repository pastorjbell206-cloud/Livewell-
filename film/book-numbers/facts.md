# Numbers: forty years in the wilderness: the facts in the script, and where each was checked

This film is a rebuild of the Bible Explorer film `client/public/explorer/films/book-numbers.json`. The words are new; every claim is one the old film already made, and the old film's sources are its rows in `docs/work/elite/explorer-ledger.md`. Nothing was added from outside.

## Scripture (Berean Standard Bible)

Each quotation was checked word for word against `client/public/bible/text/` by `film/tools/write-script.py` on 2026-11-26.

- **Numbers 9:23** (shown on screen): “They camped at the LORD’s command, and they set out at the LORD’s command”
- **Numbers 14:31** (shown on screen): “But I will bring your children”
- **Numbers 6:24** (spoken): “May the LORD bless you and keep you”
- **Numbers 14:11** (shown on screen): “How long will they refuse to believe in Me”
- **Numbers 24:17** (shown on screen): “A star will come forth from Jacob”
- **Numbers 27:7** (shown on screen): “The daughters of Zelophehad speak correctly.”

## Notes

- Every sentence restates a caption of the old film or its "differ" note. "Balak of Moab" is the old film's phrase.
- The wars: the film says Christians read them in more than one way, that on every reading the commands were tied to one land and one time, and keeps word for word "no one may use any page of this book as a reason for violence against anyone". The three readings are not listed in the film; they stay on the page.
- The two counts are at Numbers 1 and 26 in the site's text. Each tally in the picture is a picture of a count, not a number.
- The old film's care line stays on the page word for word; none was written here.
