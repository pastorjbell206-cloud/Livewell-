Mark is the shortest of the four Gospels, and the fastest. One word keeps showing up. Immediately.

There's no Christmas story. A grown man walks out of the wilderness and says the kingdom of God is near.

For eight chapters, everyone keeps asking the same thing. Who is this?

He calms a storm with a word. He heals the sick. He tells a man his sins are forgiven.

Then, right in the middle, Jesus turns the question around.

But what about you? Who do you say I am?

Peter answers. You are the Christ.

From that moment, the whole book walks toward a cross. Because this king came to do what no one expected.

For even the Son of Man did not come to be served, but to serve, and to give His life as a ransom for many.

And at the cross, the one who finally says it out loud is a Roman soldier.

Truly this man was the Son of God!

Mark leaves you holding his question. Who do you say he is?
