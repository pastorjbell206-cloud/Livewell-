Two thousand years ago, the most powerful man on earth was called Caesar.

His face was on the coins. His armies were on the roads. And people called him lord.

Then a small group of people started saying three words. Jesus is Lord.

Today that sounds like something you'd hear in church. Back then it could get you killed.

Because if Jesus is Lord, Caesar isn't.

Jesus taught his followers to pray like this.

Your kingdom come, Your will be done, on earth as it is in heaven.

Look at what that prayer asks. It doesn't ask God to bless my side.

It asks for his kingdom to come. Which means mine has to make room.

Every nation is tempted to borrow God for its own story. Ours is too. So am I.

You can love your country. It's a good gift. But a good gift makes a terrible god.

Nations rise and nations fall. Rome did.

His kingdom is the one that can't be shaken.

So here's the question. Whose kingdom are you praying for?
