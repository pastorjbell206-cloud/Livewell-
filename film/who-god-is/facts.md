# Who God is: Father, Son and Holy Spirit: the facts in the script, and where each was checked

This film is a rebuild of the Bible Explorer film `client/public/explorer/films/who-god-is.json`. The words are new; every claim is one the old film already made, and the old film's sources are its rows in `docs/work/elite/explorer-ledger.md`. Nothing was added from outside.

## Scripture (Berean Standard Bible)

Each quotation was checked word for word against `client/public/bible/text/` by `film/tools/write-script.py` on 2026-11-26.

- **Deuteronomy 6:4** (spoken): “Hear, O Israel: The LORD our God, the LORD is One.”
- **1 Corinthians 8:6** (shown on screen): “yet for us there is but one God, the Father, from whom all things came and for whom we exist.”
- **John 1:1** (spoken): “In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God.”
- **Psalm 139:7** (spoken): “Where can I go to escape Your Spirit? Where can I flee from Your presence?”
- **Mark 1:11** (spoken): “You are My beloved Son; in You I am well pleased.”
- **Matthew 28:19** (shown on screen): “baptizing them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit”
- **Isaiah 40:18** (spoken): “To whom will you liken God? To what image will you compare Him?”
- **2 Corinthians 13:14** (spoken): “The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ, and the love of God, and the fellowship of the Holy Spirit be with all of you.”

## Notes

- Every sentence restates a caption of the old film or its "differ" note. Nothing was added from outside.
- God is never drawn, and the film gives no picture of the Trinity; the three everyday pictures (water, a clover, three people) appear only as the old film names them, as pictures that break. The words modalism and tritheism are left on the page.
- "One what, three whos", the Nicene Creed's words and the family catechism's "not a riddle to solve but a wonder to receive" are the old film's.
- Bible words spoken outside the chapter verses ("our Father in heaven", "my Lord and my God", "not lied to men, but to God", "in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit") are the old film's own quotations, there with their references.
- The old film has no care line and no "differ" note.
