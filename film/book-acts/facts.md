# Acts: to the ends of the earth: the facts in the script, and where each was checked

This film is a rebuild of the Bible Explorer film `client/public/explorer/films/book-acts.json`. The words are new; every claim is one the old film already made, and the old film's sources are its rows in `docs/work/elite/explorer-ledger.md`. Nothing was added from outside.

## Scripture (Berean Standard Bible)

Each quotation was checked word for word against `client/public/bible/text/` by `film/tools/write-script.py` on 2026-11-26.

- **Acts 1:8** (spoken): “you will be My witnesses in Jerusalem, and in all Judea and Samaria, and to the ends of the earth.”
- **Acts 28:31** (shown on screen): “Boldly and freely he proclaimed the kingdom of God”
- **Acts 2:17** (spoken): “I will pour out My Spirit on all people.”
- **Acts 7:60** (spoken): “Lord, do not hold this sin against them.”
- **Acts 10:34** (shown on screen): “I now truly understand that God does not show favoritism”
- **Acts 9:4** (spoken): “Saul, Saul, why do you persecute Me?”
- **Acts 1:1** (shown on screen): “I wrote about all that Jesus began to do and to teach”

## Notes

- Every sentence restates a caption of the old film or its "differ" note. Nothing was added from outside.
- Where Christians differ (how much of the pattern in Acts is for every church; the order of faith, baptism and receiving the Spirit; tongues as a regular sign) the script names the questions as the old note does and takes no side.
- Stephen's death is named in one sentence and never shown or described.
- "Faith in Jesus fulfills Israel's hope and does not replace it. The first believers are Jews" is the old film's sentence. Every person is the same dot.
- The Spirit and the risen Jesus are never drawn, only light and lines.
- The old film's care line stays on the page word for word; none was written here.
