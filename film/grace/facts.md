# Grace: the gift no one earns: the facts in the script, and where each was checked

This film is a rebuild of the Bible Explorer film `client/public/explorer/films/grace.json`. The words are new; every claim is one the old film already made, and the old film's sources are its rows in `docs/work/elite/explorer-ledger.md`. Nothing was added from outside.

## Scripture (Berean Standard Bible)

Each quotation was checked word for word against `client/public/bible/text/` by `film/tools/write-script.py` on 2026-11-26.

- **Romans 6:23** (spoken): “For the wages of sin is death, but the gift of God is eternal life in Christ Jesus our Lord.”
- **Matthew 20:15** (spoken): “Or are you envious because I am generous?”
- **Romans 5:8** (shown on screen): “But God proves His love for us in this: While we were still sinners, Christ died for us.”
- **Ephesians 2:8-9** (shown on screen): “For it is by grace you have been saved through faith, and this not from yourselves; it is the gift of God, not by works, so that no one can boast.”
- **Romans 6:1-2** (spoken): “Shall we continue in sin so that grace may increase? Certainly not!”
- **2 Corinthians 12:9** (spoken): “My grace is sufficient for you, for My power is perfected in weakness.”
- **Ephesians 4:32** (shown on screen): “Be kind and tenderhearted to one another, forgiving each other just as in Christ God forgave you.”

## Notes

- Every sentence restates a caption of the old film or its "differ" note. Nothing was added from outside.
- Where Christians differ (grace and the human will; faith alone, or faith with the sacraments and the life of the church) the old note's terms are used and no side is taken.
- The closing line was changed before recording from a repeat of the opening line to "Grace is the gift that no one earns", the film's own title in a sentence, because the first take rushed the repeated line.
- Romans 6:1-2 carries its own exclamation mark in the Berean text; it is the only one in the film.
- The old film's care line stays on the page word for word; none was written here.
