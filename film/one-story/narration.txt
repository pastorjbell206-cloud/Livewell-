The Bible is sixty-six books, written by many authors over more than a thousand years.

And it tells one story.

It opens with God making a good world.

In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth.

Then people decide they'd rather run it themselves. And everything cracks. With God, with each other, with the world itself.

God doesn't walk away. He chooses one family, Abraham's, and makes a promise.

All the families of the earth will be blessed through you.

The rest of the Old Testament follows that family. Rescued from slavery. Given a land. Ruled by kings. Carried into exile. And waiting.

Then the story does something no one saw coming. The author steps into it.

The Word became flesh and made His dwelling among us.

Jesus lives the life we didn't. He dies the death we earned. And he walks out of the grave.

The last page sounds like the first.

Behold, I make all things new.

You're living between those two pages. Find your place in the story.
