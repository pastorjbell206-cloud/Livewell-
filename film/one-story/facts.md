# One story: the facts in the script, and where to check each

None of these was checked against a primary source in this session. Each is standard and widely published; check before recording. Scripture lines are the Berean Standard Bible, copied from `client/public/bible/text/`.

- **Sixty-six books:** the Protestant canon, as the site's Study Bible has it.
- **"Many authors over more than a thousand years":** the usual account. No number of authors is given, since counts differ.
- **Genesis 1:1, John 1:14 (first sentence), Revelation 21:5 (God's words):** quoted word for word.
- **Genesis 12:3:** a clause of the verse, spoken with a capital A added for the sentence.
- **"Rescued from slavery. Given a land. Ruled by kings. Carried into exile.":** Exodus; Joshua; Samuel and Kings; 2 Kings 25.
- **For James:** "He dies the death we earned" states substitution plainly. Say if you want it softer or sharper.
