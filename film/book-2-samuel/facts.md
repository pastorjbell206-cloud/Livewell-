# 2 Samuel: the king and the promise: the facts in the script, and where each was checked

This film is a rebuild of the Bible Explorer film `client/public/explorer/films/book-2-samuel.json`. The words are new; every claim is one the old film already made, and the old film's sources are its rows in `docs/work/elite/explorer-ledger.md`. Nothing was added from outside.

## Scripture (Berean Standard Bible)

Each quotation was checked word for word against `client/public/bible/text/` by `film/tools/write-script.py` on 2026-11-26.

- **2 Samuel 5:3** (shown on screen): “And they anointed him king over Israel.”
- **2 Samuel 6:14** (shown on screen): “danced with all his might before the LORD”
- **2 Samuel 7:16** (spoken): “Your house and kingdom will endure forever before Me”
- **2 Samuel 12:13** (spoken): “I have sinned against the LORD.”
- **2 Samuel 18:33** (shown on screen): “O my son Absalom! My son, my son Absalom!”

## Notes

- Every sentence restates a caption of the old film or its "differ" note.
- Bathsheba: the film keeps the old sentence that the text places the whole responsibility on David, and adds from the old note that nothing in the story blames her. She is not drawn in any form.
- "The rape of Tamar. A son murdered. Absalom's revolt." are the old film's own words, named and not described. The picture is a house outline with a crack; Tamar is not drawn.
- The promise to David: the film says Jews before Christ read it of a son of David to come, Christians confess it kept in Jesus, and the two part over how. No side is argued.
- The old film's care line, and its word for anyone who has been sexually assaulted, stay on the page word for word; none was written here. This film should not be offered to younger viewers apart from that page.
