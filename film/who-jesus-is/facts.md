# Who Jesus is: the facts in the script, and where each was checked

This film is a rebuild of the Bible Explorer film `client/public/explorer/films/who-jesus-is.json`. The words are new; every claim is one the old film already made, and the old film's sources are its rows in `docs/work/elite/explorer-ledger.md`. Nothing was added from outside.

## Scripture (Berean Standard Bible)

Each quotation was checked word for word against `client/public/bible/text/` by `film/tools/write-script.py` on 2026-11-26.

- **John 1:1** (spoken): “In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God.”
- **John 1:14** (spoken): “The Word became flesh and made His dwelling among us.”
- **Hebrews 4:15** (shown on screen): “we have one who was tempted in every way that we are, yet was without sin.”
- **Mark 4:41** (spoken): “Who is this, that even the wind and the sea obey Him?”
- **Colossians 2:9** (shown on screen): “For in Christ all the fullness of the Deity dwells in bodily form.”
- **Hebrews 2:17** (shown on screen): “He had to be made like His brothers in every way”
- **Matthew 16:16** (spoken): “You are the Christ, the Son of the living God.”

## Notes

- Every sentence restates a caption of the old film or its "differ" note. Nothing was added from outside.
- "The Council of Chalcedon in 451: one person who is truly God and truly man" is the old film's, spoken as "the year four fifty-one".
- "Some say he was only a good teacher. The Honest Answer on that question weighs it" points to the site's own page, as the old film does; the film makes no claim about it.
- Jesus is never drawn: one bar of light throughout.
- The old film has no care line and no "differ" note.
