# Exodus: out of slavery, to God: the facts in the script, and where each was checked

This film is a rebuild of the Bible Explorer film `client/public/explorer/films/book-exodus.json`. The words are new; every claim is one the old film already made, and the old film's sources are its rows in `docs/work/elite/explorer-ledger.md`. Nothing was added from outside.

## Scripture (Berean Standard Bible)

Each quotation was checked word for word against `client/public/bible/text/` by `film/tools/write-script.py` on 2026-11-26.

- **Exodus 2:24** (spoken): “So God heard their groaning, and He remembered His covenant with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.”
- **Exodus 3:14** (spoken): “I AM WHO I AM.”
- **Exodus 15:2** (shown on screen): “The LORD is my strength and my song, and He has become my salvation.”
- **Exodus 20:2** (shown on screen): “I am the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of slavery.”
- **Exodus 40:34** (spoken): “the glory of the LORD filled the tabernacle.”

## Notes

- Every sentence restates a caption of the old film at the older level, including the Hebrew word that means both to serve as a slave and to serve in worship.
- Key chapters 3, 12, 14, 19, 20, 32, 34 and 40 are the book page's own list.
- The Passover night is named as the old film names it ("a night of great sadness in Egypt") and is never shown.
- Exodus 40:34 is spoken with a capital T because the clause opens a sentence.
- The old film's care line stays on its page word for word; none was written here.
