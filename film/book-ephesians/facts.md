# Ephesians: one new people in Christ: the facts in the script, and where each was checked

This film is a rebuild of the Bible Explorer film `client/public/explorer/films/book-ephesians.json`. The words are new; every claim is one the old film already made, and the old film's sources are its rows in `docs/work/elite/explorer-ledger.md`. Nothing was added from outside.

## Scripture (Berean Standard Bible)

Each quotation was checked word for word against `client/public/bible/text/` by `film/tools/write-script.py` on 2026-11-26.

- **Ephesians 1:10** (shown on screen): “to bring all things in heaven and on earth together in Christ.”
- **Ephesians 4:1** (shown on screen): “I urge you to walk in a manner worthy of the calling you have received”
- **Ephesians 2:8** (spoken): “For it is by grace you have been saved through faith”
- **Ephesians 2:14** (spoken): “For He Himself is our peace, who has made the two one”
- **Ephesians 4:32** (spoken): “Be kind and tenderhearted to one another, forgiving each other just as in Christ God forgave you.”
- **Ephesians 6:10** (shown on screen): “Finally, be strong in the Lord and in His mighty power.”
- **Ephesians 3:20** (shown on screen): “Now to Him who is able to do immeasurably more than all we ask or imagine”

## Notes

- Every sentence restates a caption of the old film or its "differ" note. Nothing was added from outside.
- The household passages: the script keeps the old film's sentences that they give no one a licence to control or harm another, that nothing in them asks anyone to stay where they or their children are in danger, and that the misuse of the words to slaves to defend slavery was wrong. On how churches read them, on being chosen and on who wrote the letter, the questions are named and no side is taken.
- The old note's sentence "If you are in an abusive marriage, seek safety first" stays on the page, word for word; the film does not rewrite it.
- Harm and slavery are named and never pictured: an open door with a clear way out, and a broken shackle with no person near it. Every person is the same dot.
- The old film's care line stays on the page word for word; none was written here.
