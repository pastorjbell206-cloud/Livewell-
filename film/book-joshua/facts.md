# Joshua: a promise kept: the facts in the script, and where each was checked

This film is a rebuild of the Bible Explorer film `client/public/explorer/films/book-joshua.json`. The words are new; every claim is one the old film already made, and the old film's sources are its rows in `docs/work/elite/explorer-ledger.md`. Nothing was added from outside.

## Scripture (Berean Standard Bible)

Each quotation was checked word for word against `client/public/bible/text/` by `film/tools/write-script.py` on 2026-11-26.

- **Joshua 1:2** (shown on screen): “cross over the Jordan into the land that I am giving to the children of Israel.”
- **Joshua 21:45** (shown on screen): “Not one of all the LORD’s good promises to the house of Israel had failed”
- **Joshua 4:24** (shown on screen): “so that all the peoples of the earth may know that the hand of the LORD is mighty”
- **Joshua 2:11** (shown on screen): “the LORD your God is God in the heavens above and on the earth below.”
- **Joshua 24:15** (spoken): “choose for yourselves this day whom you will serve”
- **Joshua 1:9** (spoken): “for the LORD your God is with you wherever you go.”

## Notes

- Every sentence restates a caption of the old film or its "differ" note.
- The hardest chapters: the film keeps the old sentences nearly word for word ("devoted to destruction at God's command, the young with the old"; "Christians read this in more than one way, and none makes it easy"; "No one may use these chapters to justify violence against any person or people"), and adds from the old note that on every reading it was tied to one land and one moment and never renewed. The picture for this scene is a book, three readers and one bounded patch of land: no battle, weapon, wall or fire anywhere in the film.
- The date Israel entered the land is not given, since Christians differ on it.
- Joshua 24:15 is the verse the old film quotes in its caption. Joshua 1:9 is spoken at the close with a capital F because the clause opens a sentence.
- The old film's care line stays on the page word for word; none was written here.
