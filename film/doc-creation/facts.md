## For James

**Left out on purpose.**
- The names attached to each view (Henry Morris, John Whitcomb and Ken Ham; Hugh Ross, Charles Hodge and C. John Collins; Meredith Kline, Henri Blocher and Bruce Waltke; Francis Collins, Denis Alexander and John Walton). They were cut so that no view carries extra weight from its famous names.
- Each view's further arguments, so that every view gets one claim and one reason of about the same length: flood geology and death before the fall (young-earth); "without common descent" and the analogical-days reading (old-earth); the sun on the fourth day and Genesis 2:5 (framework); common descent by name, the genetic and fossil evidence, and a historical Adam and Eve within early humanity (evolutionary creation). The pictures follow the file: separate long bands with new kinds of life brought in along them for old-earth, and one branching tree for evolutionary creation.
- The long history of reading the days: Basil's Hexaemeron, Augustine ("with time, not in time"), Luther and Calvin, the Westminster Confession's "six days", Ussher's 4004 BC, the gap view, Darwin, Hodge's What Is Darwinism?, Warfield, Humani Generis, Kline 1958, The Genesis Flood 1961, BioLogos and Walton. Also Justin Martyr, Irenaeus, Tertullian, the Arian debate over Proverbs 8 and Colossians 1:15, Lateran IV, Aquinas and Bonaventure, Vatican I and Barth.
- The JPS reading of Genesis 1:1 ("When God began to create"), Genesis 2 (dust, breath, the garden "to cultivate and keep it"), the great sea creatures, the Sabbath and Exodus 20:11, the Psalms, Job, Proverbs 8, Amos, Jeremiah, Isaiah 45:7, Jesus's appeals to "the beginning of creation", John 1:3, Hebrews 11:3, Acts 14 and 17, Romans 1 and 4:17, and 1 Timothy 4:4.
- The errors section (dualism, pantheism, emanation, eternal matter, naturalism, deism), except as the confession's own negatives: "The world isn't God, and it isn't an accident."
- The Hebrew, Greek and Latin words (bara', 'asah, yatsar, tohu wabohu, ktizō, kosmos, creatio ex nihilo). The film says "out of nothing".
- Whether the new creation is this world renewed or replaced, and the 2 Peter 3:10 manuscripts. The close says only what Revelation 21:5 says, and the picture is a sunrise, not old plants revived.

**Views named without choosing.** Young-earth creationism, old-earth creationism (the day-age view), the framework view and evolutionary creation, in the file's order, each in two sentences of about the same length (31 to 37 words), each in words its own holders use. In the picture each view is a column of the same width, ink and weight; each is lit while it is spoken and dimmed while the next is spoken, and all four come back to full strength together under one bracket: "DIFFERENT READINGS · THE SAME MAKER".

**Points to check.**
- "Each view argues from Scripture. They differ on how to read the days, not on who made the world." is a summary of the differ and definition sections, not a sentence in the file.
- "Other nations told it differently" borrows the file's phrase from its Psalms paragraph and applies it to the Enuma Elish.
- "Colossians says" names the book and not its writer, as the file does.
- Revelation 21:5 is read whole, including "Then He said, 'Write this down, for these words are faithful and true.'"
- The film is 599 words, at the top of the range.

**Names to listen for.** Enuma Elish, Babylonian, Theophilus of Antioch, Plato, Aristotle, Colossians, Isaiah.
