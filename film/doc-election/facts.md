# Chosen: the facts in the script, and where each comes from

Every fact below is taken from the Study Bible's doctrine page data, `client/public/bible/doctrines/election.json`. The section of that file is named for each line. Scripture is the Berean Standard Bible, copied character for character from `client/public/bible/text/`.

**On screen only.** The card names come from the same differ entries: Augustine, Calvin, the Canons of Dort, the Westminster Confession; Arminius, Wesley, Thomas Oden, Roger Olson; Robert Shank, William Klein, Brian Abasciano; the Formula of Concord (1577); Karl Barth (Church Dogmatics II/2, 1942). Each card's two short phrases restate its two spoken lines. "The transfiguration", "The Twelve", "The foundation of the world" and "Romans 9 to 11" are from the nt section. Counts in the pictures (nine families, four great peoples, seven dots in the "in Him" ring) are drawings, not claims. The Twelve are twelve.

## For James

- **Where the weight sits.** Seven of the ten chapters are what every tradition in the file shares: God's free choosing, its two marks (love, not merit; always for something), Abraham for the many, Deuteronomy 7:7, Jesus the Chosen One, what all confess (grace first, no predestining to sin, no fatalism, the gospel offered to all), the life section, and the file's own sentence of agreement as the last spoken line. The end card ("Chosen in love, for the sake of the world.") joins the two marks the file says run through every kind of election.
- **Named without choosing.** All five views in the differ section, in the file's own order: Augustinian and Reformed (unconditional election), Arminian and Wesleyan (conditional election), corporate election in Christ, Lutheran single predestination, and Christ as the elect one (Barth and his heirs). Each has exactly two lines and 44 words: one line of what its holders hold, one line of what they argue, each in the file's words. Characters run from 257 (Barth) to 275 (Arminian, corporate), so spoken time will differ by a second or two. On screen each view is the same card, same size, same colours, same single mustard rule, same time in the centre; the others sit equally quiet. The whole differ section is one chapter at one music level (0.7). The film never says which is right.
- **Order.** The views run in the file's order, which puts the Reformed view first and Barth's last. Your own church confesses the 1689 Second London Confession, which follows Dort, so you may want to judge whether first place reads as preference. The last spoken word inside the section is Barth's "good news"; the very next line turns to common ground, and the film's last word is the file's shared sentence, not any view's.
- **Left out on purpose.** Jacob and Esau, Malachi 1:2-3 and Romans 9:11-13 (the most argued-over texts; naming them fairly needs every view's reading, and they would tilt the opening). Ishmael's blessing, Amos 3:2 and the remnant, Saul and Judas (office can be lost), the Romans 8 chain, 1 Peter 2:9, and "a place where His name will dwell". The Hebrew and Greek words (bachar, eklegomai, proorizō, proginōskō). Augustine's earlier view of 394 (so the film does not present his later view as a growing up). Orange by name, Gottschalk, Aquinas, Luther 1525, Calvin's Institutes, Trent, the Remonstrance of 1610, Dort, Westminster, the 1689 Confession, Wesley and Whitefield (already told in the Great Awakening film), Fuller, Jansenism, the Catholic Catechism and the Orthodox Synod of Jerusalem, Newbigin and Wright, and supralapsarian and infralapsarian. Also the critics' question to Barth about universal salvation: no view's critics are given a voice, so each view is heard only as its holders put it. The errors of pride, license and anxious self-examination are left out except "no room for boasting".
- **Catholic and Orthodox teaching.** The file's differ section has no separate Catholic or Orthodox view (it counts "many Eastern Orthodox teachers" among the conditional view's holders, and gives Catholic and Orthodox teaching only in the history). The film follows the differ section, so neither is named as its own reading. If you want them, they belong in the file first.
- **Length.** 600 words, the top of the range; about three minutes forty by the estimate.
- **Names to listen for:** Augustinian (aw-guh-STIN-ee-un), Augustine (pick one way and keep it), Arminian (ar-MIN-ee-un), Wesleyan, Karl Barth (BART), Deuteronomy, transfiguration, foreknowledge and foreknows, predestined, fatalism, untraceable, and the "O," that opens Romans 11:33.
