# What sin is: the facts in the script, and where each was checked

This film is a rebuild of the Bible Explorer film `client/public/explorer/films/what-sin-is.json`. The words are new; every claim is one the old film already made, and the old film's sources are its rows in `docs/work/elite/explorer-ledger.md`. Nothing was added from outside.

## Scripture (Berean Standard Bible)

Each quotation was checked word for word against `client/public/bible/text/` by `film/tools/write-script.py` on 2026-11-26.

- **Mark 12:30** (shown on screen): “Love the Lord your God with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your mind and with all your strength.”
- **Genesis 3:5** (spoken): “you will be like God”
- **Isaiah 59:2** (spoken): “your iniquities have built barriers between you and your God”
- **Romans 3:23** (spoken): “for all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God”
- **1 John 1:9** (spoken): “If we confess our sins, He is faithful and just to forgive us our sins”

## Notes

- Every sentence restates a caption of the old film or its "differ" note. Nothing was added from outside.
- The definition is the family catechism's, as the old film gives it. How the first sin reaches everyone is named as debated, in the old note's terms, and the film does not choose.
- Bible words spoken outside the chapter verses (the two commands, "where are you?", "to seek and to save the lost") are the old film's own quotations.
- The picture is gentle on purpose: no one is shown crushed or cast out, no particular sin is illustrated, judgment is named and not shown.
- The old film's care line stays on the page word for word; none was written here.
