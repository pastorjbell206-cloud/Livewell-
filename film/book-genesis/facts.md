## Notes

- Every sentence restates a caption of the old film at the older level.

- The ten headings ("This is the account of", once "the book of the generations of") are at Genesis 2:4, 5:1, 6:9, 10:1, 11:10, 11:27, 25:12, 25:19, 36:1 and 37:2 in the site's text.
- Key chapters 1, 3, 9, 12, 15, 22, 32 and 50 are the book page's own list.
- Genesis 50:20 is spoken with a capital W because the clause opens a sentence; on screen it is shown as the verse has it.
- The old film's care line stays on its page word for word; none was written here.
