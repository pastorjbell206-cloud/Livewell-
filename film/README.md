# film/

Narrated films for LiveWell, made the way the Gather Church films were made.

Two scripts are here, words approved by James on 2026-11-26:

- `trust-the-bible/` : "Can you trust the Bible?", for /books/the-reliability-of-scripture
- `whose-kingdom/` : "Whose kingdom?", for /books/a-higher-allegiance

In each folder: `script.json` (chapters, kickers, music levels, lines), `words.md` (each line beside the paragraph of James's book it comes from) and `narration.txt` (the lines in order, one blank line between them, to paste into ElevenLabs as one take).

Not done yet, and only possible on James's Mac: copying the tools from `~/Projects/gather-church/film/`, the ElevenLabs take, timing, picture, music, mix, render and poster. The field names and `gap` here follow James's description of the Gather scripts; match them to `film/beliefs/script.json` there before building.

Before recording, check the text typed into ElevenLabs against `narration.txt`: the character count must match and the first word must be whole.

Nothing here is served by the site. A finished film goes on a page only when James says yes.
