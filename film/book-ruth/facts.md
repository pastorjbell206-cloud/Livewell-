# Ruth: loyal love in a hard time: the facts in the script, and where each was checked

This film is a rebuild of the Bible Explorer film `client/public/explorer/films/book-ruth.json`. The words are new; every claim is one the old film already made, and the old film's sources are its rows in `docs/work/elite/explorer-ledger.md`. Nothing was added from outside.

## Scripture (Berean Standard Bible)

Each quotation was checked word for word against `client/public/bible/text/` by `film/tools/write-script.py` on 2026-11-26.

- **Ruth 1:16** (spoken): “your people will be my people, and your God will be my God.”
- **Ruth 1:21** (shown on screen): “I went away full, but the LORD has brought me back empty.”
- **Ruth 2:12** (shown on screen): “under whose wings you have taken refuge.”
- **Ruth 2:20** (shown on screen): “The man is a close relative. He is one of our kinsman-redeemers.”
- **Ruth 4:17** (spoken): “He became the father of Jesse, the father of David.”
