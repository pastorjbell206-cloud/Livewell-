# Proverbs: skill for ordinary days: the facts in the script, and where each was checked

This film is a rebuild of the Bible Explorer film `client/public/explorer/films/book-proverbs.json`. The words are new; every claim is one the old film already made, and the old film's sources are its rows in `docs/work/elite/explorer-ledger.md`. Nothing was added from outside.

## Scripture (Berean Standard Bible)

Each quotation was checked word for word against `client/public/bible/text/` by `film/tools/write-script.py` on 2026-11-26.

- **Proverbs 1:7** (spoken): “The fear of the LORD is the beginning of knowledge”
- **Proverbs 4:18** (shown on screen): “The path of the righteous is like the first gleam of dawn”
- **Proverbs 1:20** (shown on screen): “Wisdom calls out in the street, she lifts her voice in the square”
- **Proverbs 15:1** (spoken): “A gentle answer turns away wrath, but a harsh word stirs up anger.”
- **Proverbs 19:17** (shown on screen): “Kindness to the poor is a loan to the LORD”
- **Proverbs 3:5** (spoken): “Trust in the LORD with all your heart, and lean not on your own understanding”

## Notes

- Every sentence restates a caption of the old film at the older level. "A proverb ... isn't a guarantee for every case" is the old film's own caution.
- Wisdom and Folly are drawn as voices from two doorways, never as figures.
- The five collections are drawn from `client/public/bible/notes/proverbs/intro.json` (1 to 9; 10:1 to 22:16; 22:17 to 24:34; 25 to 29; 30 to 31).
- The old film has no care line.
