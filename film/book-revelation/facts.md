# Revelation: the Lamb on the throne: the facts in the script, and where each was checked

This film is a rebuild of the Bible Explorer film `client/public/explorer/films/book-revelation.json`. The words are new; every claim is one the old film already made, and the old film's sources are its rows in `docs/work/elite/explorer-ledger.md`. Nothing was added from outside.

## Scripture (Berean Standard Bible)

Each quotation was checked word for word against `client/public/bible/text/` by `film/tools/write-script.py` on 2026-11-26.

- **Revelation 1:17** (spoken): “Do not be afraid. I am the First and the Last”
- **Revelation 1:3** (shown on screen): “Blessed is the one who reads aloud the words of this prophecy”
- **Revelation 4:8** (shown on screen): “Holy, Holy, Holy, is the Lord God Almighty, who was and is and is to come!”
- **Revelation 5:12** (spoken): “Worthy is the Lamb, who was slain”
- **Revelation 14:12** (shown on screen): “Here is a call for the perseverance of the saints”
- **Revelation 21:4** (spoken): “He will wipe away every tear from their eyes”
- **Revelation 22:20** (spoken): “Come, Lord Jesus!”

## Notes

- Every sentence restates a caption of the old film or its "differ" note. Nothing was added from outside.
- The four approaches are given in the old film's own words, with "many combine them, and faithful Christians hold each"; the three readings of the thousand years stay on the page. The film takes no side and names no date, nation or person.
- God and Christ are never drawn: the throne is an empty lit seat. No beast, battle or plague is pictured; judgment is named and not shown.
- Revelation 4:8 and 22:20 carry their own exclamation marks in the Berean text; they are the only ones in the film.
- The old film's care line stays on the page word for word; none was written here.
