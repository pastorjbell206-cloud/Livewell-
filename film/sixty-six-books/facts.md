This film is a rebuild of the Bible Explorer film `client/public/explorer/films/sixty-six-books.json`. The words are new; every claim is one the old film already made, and the old film's sources are its rows in `docs/work/elite/explorer-ledger.md`. Nothing was added from outside.

Each quotation was checked word for word against `client/public/bible/text/` by `film/tools/write-script.py` on 2026-11-26.

## Notes

- Counts, all from the old film: 66 books; 39 and 27; five books of law; the twelve short prophets; four Gospels; thirteen letters that name Paul, Hebrews with no named author, seven more. The picture also needs the shelf counts the old film implies but does not say: history 12 (Joshua to Esther), poetry and wisdom 5 (Job to the Song of Solomon), prophets 17, letters 21. They add up to 39 and 27.
- "Catholic and Orthodox Bibles include more in the Old Testament" is said without judgment, as the old film says it.
- "A book of visions" stands for the old film's "an apocalyptic vision".
