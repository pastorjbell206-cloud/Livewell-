# Leviticus: living near a holy God: the facts in the script, and where each was checked

This film is a rebuild of the Bible Explorer film `client/public/explorer/films/book-leviticus.json`. The words are new; every claim is one the old film already made, and the old film's sources are its rows in `docs/work/elite/explorer-ledger.md`. Nothing was added from outside.

## Scripture (Berean Standard Bible)

Each quotation was checked word for word against `client/public/bible/text/` by `film/tools/write-script.py` on 2026-11-26.

- **Leviticus 26:12** (spoken): “I will walk among you and be your God, and you will be My people.”
- **Leviticus 19:2** (shown on screen): “Be holy because I, the LORD your God, am holy.”
- **Leviticus 4:31** (shown on screen): “the priest will make atonement for him, and he will be forgiven.”
- **Leviticus 10:10** (shown on screen): “You must distinguish between the holy and the common”
- **Leviticus 16:30** (shown on screen): “because on this day atonement will be made for you to cleanse you”
- **Leviticus 19:18** (spoken): “love your neighbor as yourself. I am the LORD.”

## Notes

- Every sentence restates a caption of the old film at the older level or a sentence of its "differ" note.
- Where Christians differ: the film says only that all Christians read the book as God's word, none offer its sacrifices, and they differ on how its laws apply now; and it keeps the old film's sentence that nothing here gives anyone a reason for contempt toward any person. The views themselves (moral, ceremonial and civil; law and gospel; a covenant ended in Christ) are not listed in the film; they stay on the page.
- The six parts are drawn at chapters 1 to 7, 8 to 10, 11 to 15, 16, 17 to 26 and 27, from `client/public/bible/notes/leviticus/intro.json`. The bracket for the two halves (1 to 16, 17 to 27) is the picture's own choice.
- Sacrifice is named and never shown. The old film's care line, and its word for anyone made to feel unclean, stay on the page word for word; none was written here.
