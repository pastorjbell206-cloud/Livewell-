# The Bible: how we got it: the facts in the script, and where each was checked

This film is a rebuild of the Bible Explorer film `client/public/explorer/films/the-bible.json`. The words are new; every claim is one the old film already made, and the old film's sources are its rows in `docs/work/elite/explorer-ledger.md`. Nothing was added from outside.

## Scripture (Berean Standard Bible)

Each quotation was checked word for word against `client/public/bible/text/` by `film/tools/write-script.py` on 2026-11-26.

- **Hebrews 1:1** (shown on screen): “On many past occasions and in many different ways, God spoke to our fathers through the prophets.”
- **Romans 16:22** (spoken): “I, Tertius, who wrote down this letter, greet you in the Lord.”
- **2 Timothy 4:13** (shown on screen): “my scrolls, especially the parchments.”
- **Colossians 4:16** (shown on screen): “After this letter has been read among you, make sure that it is also read in the church of the Laodiceans”
- **2 Timothy 3:16** (spoken): “All Scripture is God-breathed and is useful for instruction, for conviction, for correction, and for training in righteousness”

## Notes

- Every sentence restates a caption of the old film or its "differ" note. Nothing was added from outside.
- "Every hand-made copy differs from every other in small ways. That is why scholars compare the copies" is the old film's sentence, kept without adding any number.
- The three tests for receiving a book, and "the church was recognizing books that had already proved themselves", are the old film's. Catholic and Orthodox Bibles' further Old Testament books are named as the old note names them, and the film does not choose between the traditions' accounts.
- "The New Testament was in Latin, Syriac and Coptic by the third century" is the old film's.
- No real or imitation Hebrew, Aramaic or Greek letters are drawn; nothing is drawn across a scroll.
- The old film has no care line and no separate list of differences beyond that note.
