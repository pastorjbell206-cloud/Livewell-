# Judges: a people coming apart: the facts in the script, and where each was checked

This film is a rebuild of the Bible Explorer film `client/public/explorer/films/book-judges.json`. The words are new; every claim is one the old film already made, and the old film's sources are its rows in `docs/work/elite/explorer-ledger.md`. Nothing was added from outside.

## Scripture (Berean Standard Bible)

Each quotation was checked word for word against `client/public/bible/text/` by `film/tools/write-script.py` on 2026-11-26.

- **Judges 2:10** (shown on screen): “another generation rose up who did not know the LORD or the works that He had done for Israel.”
- **Judges 2:16** (shown on screen): “Then the LORD raised up judges”
- **Judges 2:18** (shown on screen): “for the LORD was moved to pity by their groaning”
- **Judges 8:27** (shown on screen): “it became a snare to Gideon and his household.”
- **Judges 21:25** (spoken): “In those days there was no king in Israel; everyone did what was right in his own eyes.”

## Notes

- Every sentence restates a caption of the old film or its "differ" note.
- The darkest pages: Jephthah's daughter is named in one sentence, as the old film names her, and is not drawn. The last chapters are named as "a stolen idol, an outrage at Gibeah, and a civil war"; the old film's fuller description of Gibeah is not repeated. The picture is three shut doors.
- Where readers differ: the film names the three readings of the refrain about no king, and keeps the old sentence that the book gives no nation, no church and no person a warrant for violence against anyone.
- Judge markers sit at chapters 3, 6, 9, 11 and 13; chapters 17 to 21 are the part with no judge.
- The old film's care line stays on the page word for word; none was written here.
