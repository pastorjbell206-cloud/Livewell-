# Psalms: the prayer book of the Bible: the facts in the script, and where each was checked

This film is a rebuild of the Bible Explorer film `client/public/explorer/films/book-psalms.json`. The words are new; every claim is one the old film already made, and the old film's sources are its rows in `docs/work/elite/explorer-ledger.md`. Nothing was added from outside.

## Scripture (Berean Standard Bible)

Each quotation was checked word for word against `client/public/bible/text/` by `film/tools/write-script.py` on 2026-11-26.

- **Psalm 62:8** (shown on screen): “Trust in Him at all times, O people; pour out your hearts before Him.”
- **Psalm 41:13** (shown on screen): “Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel, from everlasting to everlasting. Amen and Amen.”
- **Psalm 13:1** (spoken): “How long, O LORD? Will You forget me forever?”
- **Psalm 150:6** (spoken): “Let everything that has breath praise the LORD!”
- **Psalm 93:1** (shown on screen): “The LORD reigns! He is robed in majesty”
- **Psalm 22:1** (spoken): “My God, my God, why have You forsaken me?”

## Notes

- Every sentence restates a caption of the old film at the older level.
- The five books are drawn at Psalms 1 to 41, 42 to 72, 73 to 89, 90 to 106 and 107 to 150, from `client/public/bible/notes/psalms/intro.json` and the blessings at 41:13, 72:19, 89:52 and 106:48.
- "The largest group of psalms are laments" is the old film's sentence; the piles of tiles in the picture are not a count.
- Psalm 150:6 keeps its own exclamation mark. The cross is two bare strokes; Christ is not drawn.
- The old film's care line stays on the page word for word; none was written here.
