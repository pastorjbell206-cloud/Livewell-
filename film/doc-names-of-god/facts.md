## For James

**How the name itself is handled.** God is never drawn: the names are words, letters, a door of light, a burning bush, a cloud on a mountain and a light the crowd faces at the end. The narrator never says the personal name. The film tells that Jewish practice, out of reverence, stopped speaking it aloud, and that the church inherited that practice. It never sets one people against the other. On screen the name appears only as the file writes it, the four letters YHWH, with no vowels added. If you'd rather the letters not appear at all, the four tiles can be left blank. That's a one-line change.

**Left out on purpose** (to keep the film near four minutes and on one line, from many names to one name you can call on):

- The pronunciation the file calls a scholarly reconstruction, and the form that arose from mixing the letters with the vowels of 'Adonai. Neither is spoken or shown. Left out for the same reason: Tyndale and the King James Version, the Jerusalem Bible, the Jehovah's Witnesses, the Sacred Name movement, and the Vatican's 2008 direction.
- Most of the history: the Dead Sea Scrolls, Papyrus Fouad 266, the Mishnah and the Day of Atonement, Jerome, Pseudo-Dionysius, Maimonides, Aquinas, Astruc and Wellhausen, Ugarit, McFague and Johnson and the answer to them.
- The uncertain origin of Shaddai, the LORD of Hosts, the Holy One of Israel, Rock, Shepherd, King and Redeemer (seen faintly in the first scene, not spoken), and the compound place-names (The LORD Will Provide and the others). The film keeps only the file's point that they aren't a menu of separate powers.
- The theology of the name's dwelling (Deuteronomy 12:5, 1 Kings 8:29), "for the sake of His name", Ezekiel 36, Proverbs 18:10, Malachi 1:11 and Zechariah 14:9.
- In the New Testament: Immanuel, Abba (and the Jeremias and Barr discussion), the one name of Matthew 28:19, Philippians 2:9-11 and the debate over which name is given, Acts 4:12, marana tha, John's "I am" sayings, and Revelation's titles.
- The other errors: the name as a magic word (the sons of Sceva, Matthew 7:22-23), "in Jesus' name" as a guarantee, salvation tied to pronouncing Hebrew correctly, and invented etymologies.

**Views named without choosing** (the two differ entries on Exodus 3:14, spoken in lines of 27 and 28 words, drawn as two panels of the same size and frame, each with the same ring of light and six dots, nothing highlighted):

- **I AM as God's self-existence.** God simply is and depends on nothing, and His presence follows from that. "Many in the classical tradition."
- **I AM as God's faithful presence.** A promise first, "I will be who I will be". Self-existence is implied, but presence is the point. "Many Old Testament scholars."
- No defender is named by name. The file names Augustine and Aquinas only for the first view, so naming them would give one side famous names the other lacks.
- The file's other pair (Exodus 6:3: the name's meaning newly revealed, or evidence of distinct traditions) is left out whole, both sides, so the film takes no side on the sources of the Pentateuch either.

**Choices to check:**

- The spoken verses are Exodus 20:7 and Revelation 22:4. I passed over Exodus 3:14 because two other films already quote it (book-exodus, who-made-god). I passed over Romans 10:13 because its BSB verse begins with a lowercase "for," so its words are spoken inside a narration line instead, with the reference on screen.
- The BSB's own words are also spoken inside narration lines, without a scripture object: "I AM WHO I AM", "This is My name forever", "I will surely be with you", the Exodus 34:6 words, "Jesus is Lord", "Everyone who calls on the name of the Lord will be saved". Each is shown on screen with its reference when it appears.
- "Scribes marked it so readers would say Lord instead" simplifies the file. The Masoretes wrote the vowels of 'Adonai, or sometimes 'Elohim. The screen label names 'Adonai.
- Spelling: the narration and captions write Elohim and El Shaddai, as the file's history and keyTexts do. The screen writes 'Elohim, 'El Shaddai and the other names with the apostrophes, as the definition does.
- "A party" in the list of misuses is the file's own word (errors). It may be heard politically. It sits beside "a nation's cause", also the file's.
- Pronouns for God are capitalized (He, His, Him), to match the BSB on screen and the other films.
- At 599 words the script is at the top of the 480 to 600 range. The estimate puts the film near 3 minutes 49 seconds with the closing card.

**Names to listen for** (hard for the voice):

- "The LORD" and "LORD" should be read "the Lord" (seven times, two of them in Exodus 20:7). "I AM WHO I AM" should be read plainly, not shouted.
- Elohim (el-oh-HEEM). El Shaddai (el shah-DYE). Melchizedek (mel-KIZ-uh-dek). Abram (AY-brum). Hagar (HAY-gar). Sinai (SY-nye). shem (SHEM). HaShem (hah-SHEM). kyrios (KEER-ee-ohss). Yeshua (yeh-SHOO-uh).
- The narration never asks the voice to say the personal name. Check that the voice doesn't read "LORD" letter by letter.
