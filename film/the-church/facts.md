# What the church is: the facts in the script, and where each was checked

This film is a rebuild of the Bible Explorer film `client/public/explorer/films/the-church.json`. The words are new; every claim is one the old film already made, and the old film's sources are its rows in `docs/work/elite/explorer-ledger.md`. Nothing was added from outside.

## Scripture (Berean Standard Bible)

Each quotation was checked word for word against `client/public/bible/text/` by `film/tools/write-script.py` on 2026-11-26.

- **1 Corinthians 12:12** (shown on screen): “The body is a unit, though it is composed of many parts.”
- **1 Peter 2:5** (shown on screen): “you also, like living stones, are being built into a spiritual house”
- **Acts 2:42** (spoken): “They devoted themselves to the apostles’ teaching and to the fellowship, to the breaking of bread and to prayer.”
- **Matthew 28:20** (spoken): “And surely I am with you always, even to the end of the age.”
- **Ephesians 4:5** (shown on screen): “one Lord, one faith, one baptism”

## Notes

- Every sentence restates a caption of the old film or its "differ" note. Nothing was added from outside.
- The opening definition and "God saves us into a family, not alone" are the family catechism's, as the old film gives them.
- Where churches differ (who is baptized and how; led by bishops, by elders, or by the congregation) the old note's terms are used and the film does not choose.
- Every person is the same dot; the three ways of leading are shown only by how seats are arranged.
- The old film's care line for people hurt in a church stays on the page word for word; none was written here.
