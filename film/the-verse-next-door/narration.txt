You've seen this one. On gym walls. On locker room doors.

I can do all things through Christ who gives me strength.

It sounds like a promise that you'll win.

So here's the first rule of reading the Bible. Read the verse next door.

Paul wrote this letter as a prisoner, in chains.

And one sentence earlier, he tells you what all things means.

Being filled and being hungry. Having plenty and having need.

All things isn't winning. It's the full days and the hungry days. It's being all right when you lose.

That's a better promise than the one on the wall. It doesn't say you'll get what you want.

It says Christ will be enough when you don't.

So before you quote a verse, read the ones around it.
