# Read the verse next door: the facts in the script, and where to check each

None of these was checked against a primary source in this session. Each is standard and widely published; check before recording. Scripture lines are the Berean Standard Bible, copied from `client/public/bible/text/`.

- **Philippians 4:13:** quoted word for word.
- **"Paul wrote this letter as a prisoner, in chains":** Philippians 1:7 and 1:13-14.
- **"One sentence earlier":** Philippians 4:12. The exact clause is shown on screen. The narration gives it in plain words ("Being filled and being hungry. Having plenty and having need.") and does not present that as a quotation.
- **"On gym walls. On locker room doors.":** a general observation, with no particular place or person claimed.
