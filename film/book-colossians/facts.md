# Colossians: complete in Christ: the facts in the script, and where each was checked

This film is a rebuild of the Bible Explorer film `client/public/explorer/films/book-colossians.json`. The words are new; every claim is one the old film already made, and the old film's sources are its rows in `docs/work/elite/explorer-ledger.md`. Nothing was added from outside.

## Scripture (Berean Standard Bible)

Each quotation was checked word for word against `client/public/bible/text/` by `film/tools/write-script.py` on 2026-11-26.

- **Colossians 1:7** (shown on screen): “You learned it from Epaphras, our beloved fellow servant”
- **Colossians 3:1** (shown on screen): “since you have been raised with Christ, strive for the things above”
- **Colossians 1:17** (spoken): “He is before all things, and in Him all things hold together.”
- **Colossians 2:17** (shown on screen): “These are a shadow of the things to come, but the body that casts it belongs to Christ.”
- **Colossians 3:14** (spoken): “And over all these virtues put on love, which is the bond of perfect unity.”
- **Colossians 3:23** (shown on screen): “Whatever you do, work at it with your whole being, for the Lord and not for men”
- **Colossians 2:10** (spoken): “And you have been made complete in Christ”

## Notes

- Every sentence restates a caption of the old film or its "differ" note. Nothing was added from outside.
- Who wrote the letter: the three views are given in the old note's own terms (Paul himself; a follower after his death, as many scholars since the 1800s have argued; Timothy with his approval) and no side is taken. Where and when it was written, and what the teaching drew on, stay on the page.
- "Firstborn means the heir with first place, not the first thing made, as the church confessed at Nicaea" and "Israel's feasts and Sabbaths were good gifts, a shadow pointing ahead, not a lie" are the old film's sentences.
- The household passage keeps the old film's sentences (no licence to control or harm; no one asked to stay in danger; the misuse for slavery was wrong). The old note's "seek safety first" sentence stays on the page word for word.
- Christ is never drawn; "the image of the invisible God" is shown as light only.
- The old film's care line stays on the page word for word; none was written here.
