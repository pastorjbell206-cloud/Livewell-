# Nehemiah: rebuilding the walls, and a people: the facts in the script, and where each was checked

This film is a rebuild of the Bible Explorer film `client/public/explorer/films/book-nehemiah.json`. The words are new; every claim is one the old film already made, and the old film's sources are its rows in `docs/work/elite/explorer-ledger.md`. Nothing was added from outside.

## Scripture (Berean Standard Bible)

Each quotation was checked word for word against `client/public/bible/text/` by `film/tools/write-script.py` on 2026-11-26.

- **Nehemiah 1:4** (shown on screen): “When I heard these words, I sat down and wept.”
- **Nehemiah 2:18** (shown on screen): “and they set their hands to this good work.”
- **Nehemiah 4:9** (spoken): “So we prayed to our God and posted a guard against them day and night.”
- **Nehemiah 5:9** (shown on screen): “What you are doing is not right.”
- **Nehemiah 8:8** (shown on screen): “explaining it and giving insight, so that the people could understand what was being read.”
- **Nehemiah 13:31** (spoken): “Remember me, O my God, with favor.”

## Notes

- Every sentence restates a caption of the old film or its "differ" note. 445 BC and fifty-two days are the old film's figures.
- The last reforms: the film says he acts, "sometimes harshly", that the text reports it without comment, and keeps the old sentence that nothing in it gives anyone today a warrant to strike or harm anyone. Nothing harsh is drawn.
- Opponents of the wall are plain dots with no mark of who they are.
- The old film's care line stays on the page word for word; none was written here.
