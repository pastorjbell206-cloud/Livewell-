# 1 Samuel: the heart God sees: the facts in the script, and where each was checked

This film is a rebuild of the Bible Explorer film `client/public/explorer/films/book-1-samuel.json`. The words are new; every claim is one the old film already made, and the old film's sources are its rows in `docs/work/elite/explorer-ledger.md`. Nothing was added from outside.

## Scripture (Berean Standard Bible)

Each quotation was checked word for word against `client/public/bible/text/` by `film/tools/write-script.py` on 2026-11-26.

- **1 Samuel 2:7** (shown on screen): “The LORD sends poverty and wealth; He humbles and He exalts.”
- **1 Samuel 7:12** (shown on screen): “Thus far the LORD has helped us.”
- **1 Samuel 3:10** (spoken): “Speak, for Your servant is listening.”
- **1 Samuel 8:7** (shown on screen): “they have rejected Me as their king.”
- **1 Samuel 16:7** (spoken): “For man sees the outward appearance, but the LORD sees the heart.”
- **1 Samuel 18:1** (shown on screen): “Jonathan loved him as himself.”

## Notes

- Every sentence restates a caption of the old film or its "differ" note.
- The command against Amalek: the film says it is read in more than one way and none makes it easy, and keeps the old sentence that no people living today is Amalek and no one may use the chapter to justify violence or contempt toward any person or people. The readings themselves stay on the page. The picture is a book and three readers.
- "Readers weigh why" the request for a king was a rejection of God is said without choosing a reading.
- The seven stages are drawn at chapters 1 to 3, 4 to 7, 8 to 12, 13 to 15, 16 to 20, 21 to 27 and 28 to 31, from `client/public/bible/notes/1-samuel/intro.json`.
- No battle, giant or weapon is drawn. The old film's care line stays on the page word for word.
