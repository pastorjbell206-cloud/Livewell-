# Faith: trusting the one who promised: the facts in the script, and where each was checked

This film is a rebuild of the Bible Explorer film `client/public/explorer/films/faith.json`. The words are new; every claim is one the old film already made, and the old film's sources are its rows in `docs/work/elite/explorer-ledger.md`. Nothing was added from outside.

## Scripture (Berean Standard Bible)

Each quotation was checked word for word against `client/public/bible/text/` by `film/tools/write-script.py` on 2026-11-26.

- **Hebrews 11:1** (shown on screen): “Now faith is the assurance of what we hope for and the certainty of what we do not see.”
- **Genesis 15:6** (spoken): “Abram believed the LORD, and it was credited to him as righteousness.”
- **Hebrews 12:2** (shown on screen): “Let us fix our eyes on Jesus, the author and perfecter of our faith”
- **Mark 9:24** (spoken): “I do believe; help my unbelief!”
- **Romans 10:17** (shown on screen): “Consequently, faith comes by hearing, and hearing by the word of Christ.”
- **James 2:17** (shown on screen): “So too, faith by itself, if it does not result in action, is dead.”
- **2 Corinthians 5:7** (spoken): “For we walk by faith, not by sight.”

## Notes

- Every sentence restates a caption of the old film or its "differ" note. Nothing was added from outside.
- "Churches describe the link between faith and works in different words, and none of them teaches that faith earns salvation" is the old film's sentence; the four descriptions in the old note stay on the page, and no side is taken.
- "The same Abraham also doubted and schemed" and "doubt brought to Jesus is still coming to Jesus" are the old film's.
- Mark 9:24 carries its own exclamation mark in the Berean text; it is the only one in the film.
- The old film has no care line.
