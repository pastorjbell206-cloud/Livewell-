# Can you trust the Bible? The facts in the script, and where to check each

None of these was checked against a primary source in this session (no library access here). Each is standard and widely published; check before recording.

| Line | Fact | Where to check |
|---|---|---|
| "thousands of those copies still exist" | More than 5,000 Greek New Testament manuscripts are catalogued, besides early translations. | The Kurzgefasste Liste of the Institute for New Testament Textual Research (INTF), Münster. |
| "In 1947, shepherds found clay jars in a cave near the Dead Sea" | Bedouin shepherds found the first scrolls at Qumran. The find is usually dated to early 1947; some accounts say late 1946. | The Israel Museum's Dead Sea Scrolls pages; the Leon Levy Dead Sea Scrolls Digital Library. |
| "a scroll of Isaiah about a thousand years older than any Hebrew copy we had" | The Great Isaiah Scroll (1QIsaa) is dated to about 125 BC. The oldest complete Hebrew Bible, the Leningrad Codex, is dated AD 1008 or 1009; the Aleppo Codex, about AD 930. The script says "Hebrew" on purpose: Greek copies of Isaiah from the fourth century AD were already known. | The Israel Museum's Digital Dead Sea Scrolls (the Great Isaiah Scroll). |
| "Small differences in spelling and wording. The same book." | The scroll has many differences of spelling and some of wording from the later Hebrew text, and carries all 66 chapters. "The same book" is a summary, not a number; no percentage is claimed. | The same. |
| "some scholars said King David was only a legend" | Before 1993 no inscription outside the Bible named David, and some scholars argued he was not a historical king. | Any survey of the "minimalist" debate of the 1970s to 1990s. |
| "In 1993, archaeologists dug up a broken stone. Carved on it: the House of David." | The Tel Dan inscription, found in 1993 (more pieces in 1994) by Avraham Biran's dig in northern Israel. Most scholars read its Aramaic letters as "House of David"; a few dispute the reading. | Biran and Naveh, Israel Exploration Journal 43 (1993) and 45 (1995); the Israel Museum, where it is kept. |
| "All Scripture is God-breathed." | 2 Timothy 3:16, Berean Standard Bible, the verse's first clause. | `client/public/bible/text/2-timothy/3.json` |
