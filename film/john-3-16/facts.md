# John 3:16 in its chapter: the facts in the script, and where each was checked

This film is a rebuild of the Bible Explorer film `client/public/explorer/films/john-3-16.json` (its "older" captions, its `think` question and its `differ` note), with its rows "john 1, 2" to "john 7", "john, differ" and "john, care" in `docs/work/elite/explorer-ledger.md`. The other sources are the Study Bible notes on John 3 (`client/public/bible/notes/john/3.json` and `client/public/bible/notes/john/verses/3.json`), the Study Bible notes on Numbers 21 (`client/public/bible/notes/numbers/21.json`) and the doctrine page `client/public/bible/doctrines/love.json`. Nothing was added from outside.

The old film's care line ("This moving picture tells of a real death, Jesus' death on the cross. ...") stays on the page, word for word, and is not in the narration. The snakes and the cross are named, never described.

## Scripture (Berean Standard Bible)

Each quotation was checked word for word against `client/public/bible/text/` by `film/tools/write-script.py` on 2026-11-27.

- **John 3:2** (shown on screen, first sentence of Nicodemus's words): "Rabbi, we know that You are a teacher who has come from God."
- **John 3:3** (spoken, from "no one"): "no one can see the kingdom of God unless he is born again."
- **John 3:8** (shown on screen, first two sentences; the first sentence is also spoken inside a line): "The wind blows where it wishes. You hear its sound, but you do not know where it comes from or where it is going."
- **Numbers 21:8** (spoken, the Lord's words): "Make a fiery serpent and mount it on a pole. When anyone who is bitten looks at it, he will live."
- **John 3:14-15** (spoken, whole): "Just as Moses lifted up the snake in the wilderness, so the Son of Man must be lifted up, that everyone who believes in Him may have eternal life."
- **John 3:16** (spoken, whole): "For God so loved the world that He gave His one and only Son, that everyone who believes in Him shall not perish but have eternal life."
- **John 1:10** (shown on screen, last clause; spoken inside a line as "the world that did not recognize him"): "the world did not recognize Him."
- **John 3:17** (spoken, whole): "For God did not send His Son into the world to condemn the world, but to save the world through Him."
- **John 3:19** (spoken inside a line, not a scripture card; BSB checked by hand): "The Light has come into the world, but men loved the darkness rather than the Light"
- **John 19:39** (shown on screen, first clause): "Nicodemus, who had previously come to Jesus at night"

Numbers 21:4 is not quoted: its BSB wording ("journey") trips the banned-word check, so the film says only that the people spoke against God and Moses (21:5).

## Claims, by chapter

**sign**
- From about 1980 Rollen Stewart held up signs reading "John 3:16" at televised sporting events, making the reference one of the best known in the English-speaking world: John 3 notes, `interpretation`, third paragraph.
- The verse belongs to a conversation: old film frame "night" (older).

**night**
- Nicodemus a Pharisee; "most likely" sat on the Sanhedrin, the council in Jerusalem: John 3 notes, `historical` ("Archōn tōn Ioudaiōn ... most likely means that he sat on the Sanhedrin"); verses/3.json v1-2 `history`; BSB John 3:1.
- Jesus calls him Israel's teacher: BSB John 3:10; notes `cultural`.
- He may have wished not to be seen, or came at the hour when serious study was done: notes `cultural` (citing 1QS 6:6-8 for study at night; the film does not name the scroll).
- "This man comes out of the dark toward the Light": notes `cultural`, word for word; old film frame "night"; ledger "john 1, 2".

**born**
- Jesus answers the compliment by naming a need: notes `teach.outline` 1-3; verses/3.json v3 `context`.
- The Greek word (anōthen) means again or from above; Nicodemus hears "a second time" (deuteron); most commentators think Jesus means at least from above, a birth whose source is God: notes `words` G0509, nearly word for word; verses/3.json v3 `grammar`.
- The verb is passive; a person does not bear himself: notes `words` G1080 ("a person does not bear himself"); verses/3.json v3 `grammar`.
- Ezekiel promised a new heart and God's Spirit; what startled was that Israel's own teacher needed it: notes `cultural` ("what startled was Jesus' demand that Israel's own teacher needed it"); old film frame "born"; ledger "john 1, 2"; BSB Ezekiel 36:26-27.

**wind**
- One word (pneuma, Hebrew ruach) means wind, breath and spirit: notes `cultural` and `words` G4151G.
- Required of "the most religious man in the room"; no one can produce the new birth by effort, heritage, or standing: notes `doctrines` regeneration, word for word ("family" is the film's plain word for "heritage").
- Interactive moment 1 ("Pause here").

**serpent**
- The people spoke against God and Moses; venomous snakes; many died; they confessed; the Lord's command; whoever looked lived: BSB Numbers 21:5-9; Numbers 21 notes `summary`.
- Jesus reaches for a story Nicodemus knew: old film frame "lifted" (older).
- Jewish interpreters were careful to say the metal object did not heal; the Mishnah says Israel was healed when they looked upward and set their hearts on their Father in heaven (m. Rosh Hashanah 3:8): John 3 notes `cultural`; verses/3.json v14-15 `ot` Numbers 21:8-9.

**lifted**
- "Lifted up" on a pole would make a first-century reader think of crucifixion, carried out in public: notes `cultural` ("the Roman punishment for slaves and rebels, carried out in public where everyone could see"; the film keeps only "in public view").
- The same word (hupsoō) means exalted; John uses both senses: notes `words` G5312; verses/3.json v14-15 `grammar`; BSB John 12:32-33.
- Faith compared to the look: "not an achievement, but turning toward what God has provided": verses/3.json v14-15 `theology` faith, nearly word for word. Interactive moment 2 ("Weigh a fair question").

**so**
- Houtōs most naturally means "in this way", not simply "so much"; Robert Gundry and Russell Howell (Novum Testamentum 41, 1999) argued that it points back to the lifting up of verse 14; the degree is not denied but the word directs attention to its shape, a Son given and lifted up: notes `words` G3779, nearly word for word; verses/3.json v16 `grammar`.
- God's love is why there is a cross: old film frame "loved" (older), word for word; ledger "john 4, 5".

**world**
- Kosmos in John usually means humanity, often organized in opposition to God; astonishing because of the world's hostility, not its size: notes `words` G2889, nearly word for word; verses/3.json v16 `theology` love ("humanity turned away from God"); BSB John 1:10.
- The think question ("Which is harder for you to believe: that he loves the world, or that he loves you in particular?"): old film `think.older`, lightly reworded. Interactive moment 3.

**believes**
- Believing means trusting yourself to a person more than agreeing with a statement: old film frame "believes" (older), nearly word for word; ledger "john 4, 5".
- Eternal life is the life of the age to come, received now: verses/3.json v14-15 `grammar` ("it means the life of the age to come, received now").
- Arminian readers take verse 16 to mean God's saving intention in sending the Son extends to every person; Reformed readers such as John Owen (The Death of Death in the Death of Christ, 1648) hold that God's love for the world issues in a special saving purpose for the elect while the offer to all remains sincere: notes `hard` question 1, second paragraph, nearly word for word; verses/3.json v16 `theology` salvation; old film `differ`; ledger "john, differ". No side taken.

**light**
- Not to condemn but to save: BSB John 3:17; notes `christ` third paragraph; ledger "john 6".
- There is a verdict: BSB John 3:18-19; notes `teach.pitfalls` (verse 17 does not deny judgment).
- "Loved" in verse 19 is the same verb as God's love in verse 16: verses/3.json v19-21 `grammar`; notes `doctrines` love.
- The deepest reason people avoid the Light is moral, not intellectual; we fear exposure: notes `teach.outline` 19-21, nearly word for word.

**daylight**
- John does not say how Nicodemus answered: notes `story`; old film frame "daylight"; ledger "john 7".
- He returns in 7:50-52 to ask that Jesus be given a fair hearing, and in 19:39 to help bury him: notes `story`; BSB John 7:50-51; 19:39 ("about seventy-five pounds" of myrrh and aloes is the BSB's own wording).
- His faith grew slowly, and John follows him with evident sympathy: notes `teach.pitfalls` ("people whose faith grew slowly, like Nicodemus's own"; "John follows him out of the dark with evident sympathy").

**titleCard**
- "Not only how much God loved the world, but how: a Son given, and lifted up where anyone can look." A summary of the G3779 note (houtōs: the degree not denied, attention directed to its shape) and the look of Numbers 21:8; no new claim.

## Left out

- The water of 3:5 (baptism, Ezekiel's cleansing, physical birth): a real difference among churches, cut for length; the notes' second hard question sets it out.
- Those who never heard: the old film's `differ` note links the Honest Answer; the film does not raise it.
- Hezekiah's destruction of the bronze snake (2 Kings 18:4), the Wisdom of Solomon, monogenēs ("one and only" vs "only begotten"), the Genesis 22 echo, where the quotation marks close (verse 15 or 21), Calvin's "whosoever" and D. A. Carson's view: all in the notes, all cut for length.
