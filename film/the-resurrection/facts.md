# The resurrection: the facts in the script, and where each was checked

This film is a rebuild of the Bible Explorer film `client/public/explorer/films/the-resurrection.json`. The words are new; every claim is one the old film already made, and the old film's sources are its rows in `docs/work/elite/explorer-ledger.md`. Nothing was added from outside.

## Scripture (Berean Standard Bible)

Each quotation was checked word for word against `client/public/bible/text/` by `film/tools/write-script.py` on 2026-11-26.

- **1 Corinthians 15:4** (shown on screen): “that He was buried, that He was raised on the third day according to the Scriptures”
- **Luke 24:5-6** (spoken): “Why do you look for the living among the dead? He is not here; He has risen!”
- **John 20:18** (spoken): “I have seen the Lord!”
- **Luke 24:39** (shown on screen): “Look at My hands and My feet. It is I Myself.”
- **Romans 6:9** (spoken): “death no longer has dominion over Him.”
- **1 Corinthians 15:20** (spoken): “But Christ has indeed been raised from the dead, the firstfruits of those who have fallen asleep.”
- **John 14:19** (spoken): “Because I live, you also will live.”

## Notes

- Every sentence restates a caption of the old film or its "differ" note. Nothing was added from outside.
- Bible words spoken outside the chapter verses ("look at his hands and feet", the broiled fish, the first sheaf, "faith is futile") follow the old film's own quotations and references.
- The body is never shown, and the risen Jesus is never drawn: one bar of light. Death and grief are named gently and not pictured.
- "It doesn't take away grief. It gives grief a hope" is the old film's sentence.
- The old film's care line stays on the page word for word; none was written here.
