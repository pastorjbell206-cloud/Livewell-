## Scripture (Berean Standard Bible)

Each `scripture` field was checked word for word against `client/public/bible/text/` by `film/tools/write-script.py` on 2026-11-27. Quotations inside a line with no `scripture` field were checked by hand against the same files.

- **Genesis 9:13** (spoken): “I have set My rainbow in the clouds, and it will be a sign of the covenant between Me and the earth.”
- **Genesis 15:5** (spoken): “Now look to the heavens and count the stars, if you are able.” The line also speaks the rest of the verse, “So shall your offspring be” (checked by hand).
- **Genesis 15:6** (spoken inside a line, part only; checked by hand): “Abram believed the LORD”.
- **Genesis 15:17** (spoken): “a smoking firepot and a flaming torch appeared and passed between the halves” (the verse goes on “of the carcasses”).
- **Exodus 19:4** (spoken): “I carried you on eagles’ wings and brought you to Myself.”
- **Exodus 20:2** (spoken inside a line; checked by hand): “I am the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt” (the verse goes on).
- **Exodus 24:8** (spoken inside a line; checked by hand): “This is the blood of the covenant” (the verse goes on).
- **2 Samuel 7:16** (spoken): “Your house and kingdom will endure forever before Me, and your throne will be established forever.”
- **Jeremiah 31:31** (spoken inside a line; checked by hand): “a new covenant with the house of Israel and with the house of Judah”.
- **Jeremiah 31:33** (spoken): “I will put My law in their minds and inscribe it on their hearts.” The next line also speaks, unmarked, the same verse's “I will be their God, and they will be My people” (checked by hand).
- **Jeremiah 31:34** (paraphrased): God “will remember their sins no more”.
- **Luke 22:20** (spoken): “This cup is the new covenant in My blood, which is poured out for you.”
- **Hebrews 8:13** (spoken inside a line; checked by hand): “By speaking of a new covenant, He has made the first one obsolete”.
- **Hebrews 8:8** (paraphrased): “God found fault with the people”, rendered “finds fault with the people, not God's law” after the Hebrews intro.
- **Romans 11:1** (spoken): “did God reject His people? Certainly not!” (the "!" is inside the verse).
- **Revelation 21:3** (spoken): “They will be His people, and God Himself will be with them as their God.”
- Paraphrased without quotation marks: Genesis 9:10 (every living creature); Genesis 12:3 (all the families of the earth); 2 Samuel 7 (David wants to build God a house).
