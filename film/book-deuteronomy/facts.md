# Deuteronomy: love the LORD with all your heart: the facts in the script, and where each was checked

This film is a rebuild of the Bible Explorer film `client/public/explorer/films/book-deuteronomy.json`. The words are new; every claim is one the old film already made, and the old film's sources are its rows in `docs/work/elite/explorer-ledger.md`. Nothing was added from outside.

## Scripture (Berean Standard Bible)

Each quotation was checked word for word against `client/public/bible/text/` by `film/tools/write-script.py` on 2026-11-26.

- **Deuteronomy 1:1** (shown on screen): “These are the words that Moses spoke to all Israel”
- **Deuteronomy 5:3** (shown on screen): “with all of us who are alive here today.”
- **Deuteronomy 6:5** (spoken): “And you shall love the LORD your God with all your heart”
- **Deuteronomy 7:8** (shown on screen): “But because the LORD loved you”
- **Deuteronomy 24:15** (shown on screen): “You are to pay his wages each day before sunset”
- **Deuteronomy 30:19** (spoken): “I have set before you life and death, blessing and cursing.”

## Notes

- Every sentence restates a caption of the old film or its "differ" note.
- The command about the peoples of Canaan: the film says Christians read it in more than one way, that on every reading it was tied to one land and one time, and keeps word for word "No one may use these chapters to justify violence against any person or people." The readings are not listed in the film.
- Chapter 28: the film keeps the old sentence that the blessings and curses speak to a nation about a land and are never God's verdict on one person's illness or loss. The picture shows a field, never a people.
- Deuteronomy 6:5 is spoken as a clause, as the old film shows it. The four parts are drawn at 1 to 4, 5 to 26, 27 to 30 and 31 to 34.
- The old film's care line stays on the page word for word; none was written here.
