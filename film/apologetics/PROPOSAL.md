# Apologetics films: can the Bible be trusted? (proposal, 2026-11-26)

James asked (relayed from the Gather session, 2026-11-26): several apologetics films on the Bible's reliability, 3 to 5 minutes, "very creative, very professional, very interactive", then more across theology and doctrine.

Every film is built only from a page already live on LiveWell. Each of those pages has its own verification record (sources checked) in `docs/work/elite/apol-pilot/`. No number, date or scholar enters a film unless it is in the page and its record. Scripture is quoted from the BSB word for word, checked by `write-script.py`. Where a film needs a sentence the page does not have, it gets `[JAMES INPUT NEEDED]` and waits.

Each film follows the pages' own shape: the objection at its strongest, what the evidence shows, what it does not prove, and where to read more. That honesty is what makes these work with a skeptic.
