# Isaiah: the Holy One who comforts: the facts in the script, and where each was checked

This film is a rebuild of the Bible Explorer film `client/public/explorer/films/book-isaiah.json`. The words are new; every claim is one the old film already made, and the old film's sources are its rows in `docs/work/elite/explorer-ledger.md`. Nothing was added from outside.

## Scripture (Berean Standard Bible)

Each quotation was checked word for word against `client/public/bible/text/` by `film/tools/write-script.py` on 2026-11-26.

- **Isaiah 6:3** (spoken): “Holy, holy, holy is the LORD of Hosts; all the earth is full of His glory.”
- **Isaiah 40:1** (shown on screen): ““Comfort, comfort My people,” says your God.”
- **Isaiah 7:9** (shown on screen): “If you do not stand firm in your faith, then you will not stand at all.”
- **Isaiah 1:18** (shown on screen): “Though your sins are like scarlet, they will be as white as snow”
- **Isaiah 53:5** (spoken): “But He was pierced for our transgressions”
- **Isaiah 65:17** (shown on screen): “For behold, I will create new heavens and a new earth.”
- **Isaiah 40:8** (spoken): “the word of our God stands forever.”

## Notes

- Every sentence restates a caption of the old film at the older level.
- The Servant: the film keeps both of the old film's sentences ("Jewish interpreters have often read the Servant as Israel. The New Testament sees all three in Jesus.") and the picture shows two readers treated alike. Isaiah 53:5 is spoken as a clause.
- The book's three parts are drawn at chapters 1 to 39, 40 to 55 and 56 to 66. The film says nothing about who wrote each part, as the old film says nothing.
- The vision of chapter 6 is a doorway and rising lines; no throne or figure is drawn.
- The old film's care line stays on the page word for word; none was written here.
