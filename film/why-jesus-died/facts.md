# Why did Jesus die?: the facts in the script, and where each was checked

This film is a rebuild of the Bible Explorer film `client/public/explorer/films/why-jesus-died.json`. The words are new; every claim is one the old film already made, and the old film's sources are its rows in `docs/work/elite/explorer-ledger.md`. Nothing was added from outside.

## Scripture (Berean Standard Bible)

Each quotation was checked word for word against `client/public/bible/text/` by `film/tools/write-script.py` on 2026-11-26.

- **1 Corinthians 15:3** (shown on screen): “that Christ died for our sins according to the Scriptures”

- Every sentence restates a caption of the old film or its "differ" note. Nothing was added from outside.
- "Blaming the Jewish people for his death is a lie that Christians must reject plainly. The New Testament places all of us inside the guilt" is the old film's sentence, kept word for word. Every person in the picture is the same dot, and one outline is drawn round everyone.
- The ways of explaining the cross are each given in the old film's terms and none is chosen; "the creed doesn't choose a theory" is the old film's. "Christians read Isaiah's servant as Jesus, and many Jewish readers read him as the people of Israel" is the old note's.
- "The cross does not change God's heart toward us; it shows it" is the Study Bible note the old film quotes. "This is not an angry Father against an unwilling Son" is the old film's.
- The death is named and never shown: a bare cross only.
- The old film's care line stays on the page word for word; none was written here.
