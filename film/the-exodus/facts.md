## Notes

- The plagues and the death of the firstborn are named once, never described. The drowning of Pharaoh's army is not narrated. No line blames the Egyptian people or any people group; Pharaoh and his question carry the conflict. Deuteronomy 26:6 ("the Egyptians mistreated us") was left out for that reason.
- The historical views (early date, thirteenth-century date, numbers as written, *elef* as unit, a smaller group) are named as the sources give them; the film says it doesn't choose. "Defenders answer" and "critics reply" attribute both sides.
- The old film's care line ("This part of the story has death and fear in it...") stays on the page, word for word, and is not in the narration.
- Hard names for the voice: Finkelstein, Silberman, Hoffmeier, Merneptah, Rameses, Phinehas, elef, Miriam, Livy, Romulus, Sinai, Canaan, "First Kings 6:1", "1446 BC", "1208 BC".
