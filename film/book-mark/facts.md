# Mark: the story at a run: the facts in the script, and where each was checked

This film is a rebuild of the Bible Explorer film `client/public/explorer/films/book-mark.json`. The words are new; every claim is one the old film already made, and the old film's sources are its rows in `docs/work/elite/explorer-ledger.md`. Nothing was added from outside.

## Scripture (Berean Standard Bible)

Each quotation was checked word for word against `client/public/bible/text/` by `film/tools/write-script.py` on 2026-11-26.

- **Mark 1:1** (spoken): “This is the beginning of the gospel of Jesus Christ, the Son of God.”
- **Mark 4:41** (shown on screen): “Who is this, that even the wind and the sea obey Him?”
- **Mark 10:45** (spoken): “For even the Son of Man did not come to be served, but to serve, and to give His life as a ransom for many.”
- **Mark 16:7** (shown on screen): “But go, tell His disciples and Peter”
- **Mark 15:39** (spoken): “Truly this man was the Son of God!”

## Notes

- Every sentence restates a caption of the old film. Nothing was added from outside.
- Jesus is never drawn; his way is a mustard line. The crucifixion is named and not shown: a bare cross, and the centurion as one small square.
- Mark 15:39 carries its own exclamation mark in the Berean text; it is the only one in the film.
- The old film's care line stays on the page word for word; none was written here.
