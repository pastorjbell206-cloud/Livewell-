# Jeremiah: the prophet who wept: the facts in the script, and where each was checked

This film is a rebuild of the Bible Explorer film `client/public/explorer/films/book-jeremiah.json`. The words are new; every claim is one the old film already made, and the old film's sources are its rows in `docs/work/elite/explorer-ledger.md`. Nothing was added from outside.

## Scripture (Berean Standard Bible)

Each quotation was checked word for word against `client/public/bible/text/` by `film/tools/write-script.py` on 2026-11-26.

- **Jeremiah 1:5** (shown on screen): “Before I formed you in the womb I knew you”
- **Jeremiah 1:10** (shown on screen): “to build and plant.”
- **Jeremiah 7:4** (shown on screen): “Do not trust in deceptive words”
- **Jeremiah 20:9** (spoken): “His message becomes a fire burning in my heart, shut up in my bones”
- **Jeremiah 29:11** (shown on screen): “to give you a future and a hope.”
- **Jeremiah 31:33** (spoken): “I will put My law in their minds and inscribe it on their hearts.”

## Notes

- Every sentence restates a caption of the old film at the older level.
- The two editions of Jeremiah (the shorter Greek and the longer Hebrew), which the old film's note sets out without taking a side, are not mentioned in the film; they stay on the page.
- "Its call to surrender to Babylon was a word for one moment, not a rule about politics" is the old film's sentence.
- The temple scene is drawn as any building used as a charm; nothing marks a people.
- The old film's care line stays on the page word for word; none was written here.
