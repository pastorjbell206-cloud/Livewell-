# John: the Word made flesh: the facts in the script, and where each was checked

This film is a rebuild of the Bible Explorer film `client/public/explorer/films/book-john.json`. The words are new; every claim is one the old film already made, and the old film's sources are its rows in `docs/work/elite/explorer-ledger.md`. Nothing was added from outside.

## Scripture (Berean Standard Bible)

Each quotation was checked word for word against `client/public/bible/text/` by `film/tools/write-script.py` on 2026-11-26.

- **John 1:14** (spoken): “The Word became flesh and made His dwelling among us.”
- **John 13:1** (shown on screen): “Jesus knew that His hour had come”
- **John 2:11** (shown on screen): “Jesus performed this, the first of His signs, at Cana in Galilee.”
- **John 6:35** (spoken): “I am the bread of life.”
- **John 1:12** (shown on screen): “to those who believed in His name, He gave the right to become children of God”
- **John 13:34** (spoken): “A new commandment I give you: Love one another.”
- **John 20:31** (spoken): “these are written so that you may believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God”

## Notes

- Every sentence restates a caption of the old film. Nothing was added from outside.
- "The verb believe comes ninety-eight times in John, and the noun faith never does" is the old film's count (written there as 98).
- The caution about "the Jews" is the old film's sentence, kept nearly word for word: John usually means the Jerusalem authorities, and Jesus and nearly everyone in the story are Jews. The picture draws every dot alike and rings only the few on the platform.
- Jesus is never drawn. The cross is bare.
- The old film's care line stays on the page word for word; none was written here.
