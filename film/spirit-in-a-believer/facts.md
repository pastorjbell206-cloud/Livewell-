# The Holy Spirit in a believer: the facts in the script, and where each was checked

This film is a rebuild of the Bible Explorer film `client/public/explorer/films/spirit-in-a-believer.json`. The words are new; every claim is one the old film already made, and the old film's sources are its rows in `docs/work/elite/explorer-ledger.md`. Nothing was added from outside.

## Scripture (Berean Standard Bible)

Each quotation was checked word for word against `client/public/bible/text/` by `film/tools/write-script.py` on 2026-11-26.

- **Romans 8:9** (shown on screen): “And if anyone does not have the Spirit of Christ, he does not belong to Christ.”
- **John 15:5** (spoken): “I am the vine and you are the branches.”
- **Galatians 4:6** (shown on screen): “God sent the Spirit of His Son into our hearts”
- **Galatians 5:22-23** (spoken): “But the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, and self-control.”
- **1 Corinthians 12:7** (shown on screen): “Now to each one the manifestation of the Spirit is given for the common good.”
- **Romans 8:26** (spoken): “the Spirit helps us in our weakness.”

## Notes

- Every sentence restates a caption of the old film or its "differ" note. Nothing was added from outside.
- Where churches differ (whether tongues, prophecy and healing continue as in Acts; whether the baptism of the Spirit is a distinct later experience) the old film's own words are used and the film does not choose.
- The definitions are the family catechism's, as the old film gives them.
- The Spirit is never drawn as a dove, flame or figure: only formless light and a line of life in the vine.
- The old film has no care line.
