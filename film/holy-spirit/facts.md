# The Holy Spirit through the Bible: the facts in the script, and where each was checked

Written on 2026-11-27 for batch 15. Sources, and nothing else: the old Bible Explorer film `client/public/explorer/films/holy-spirit.json` (older captions, its `differ` note) with its rows in `docs/work/elite/explorer-ledger.md` (rows "The Holy Spirit, waters" to "Holy Spirit, Come", lines 242 to 252); the doctrine page `client/public/bible/doctrines/spirit.json`; the Study Bible notes `client/public/bible/notes/genesis/1.json`, `ezekiel/37.json`, `joel/2.json` and `acts/2.json`; and the Honest Answer "how-can-god-be-three-and-one" in `client/src/data/answers.ts`. ## Scripture (Berean Standard Bible)

Each quotation was checked word for word against `client/public/bible/text/` by `film/tools/write-script.py` on 2026-11-27.

- **Genesis 1:2** (spoken): “And the Spirit of God was hovering over the surface of the waters.”
- **Job 33:4** (spoken): “The Spirit of God has made me, and the breath of the Almighty gives me life.”
- **1 Samuel 16:13** (spoken): “the Spirit of the LORD rushed upon David from that day forward.”
- **Numbers 11:29** (spoken inside a line, no scripture field; checked by hand against the BSB file): “I wish that all the LORD’s people were prophets” (the verse goes on; its closing "!" is not quoted).
- **Ezekiel 36:26** (spoken): “I will remove your heart of stone and give you a heart of flesh.”
- **Ezekiel 36:27** (spoken inside a line, no scripture field; checked by hand): “I will put My Spirit within you”.
- **Ezekiel 37:3** (spoken): “Son of man, can these bones come to life?” and Ezekiel's reply “only You know” (spoken inside a line; checked by hand).
- **Ezekiel 37:11** (paraphrased, not in quotation marks): the exiles' saying, our bones are dried up and our hope has perished.
- **Joel 2:28** (spoken): “I will pour out My Spirit on all people. Your sons and daughters will prophesy”
- **Mark 1:11** (spoken): “You are My beloved Son; in You I am well pleased.”
- **John 14:16** (spoken): “another Advocate to be with you forever”
- **Acts 2:2** (spoken): “Suddenly a sound like a mighty rushing wind came from heaven and filled the whole house”
- **Acts 2:16** (spoken inside a line, no scripture field; checked by hand): “this is what was spoken by the prophet Joel”
- **1 Corinthians 3:16** (shown on screen): “Do you not know that you yourselves are God’s temple, and that God’s Spirit dwells in you?”
- **Revelation 22:17** (spoken): “The Spirit and the bride say, “Come!”” (the "!" is inside the verse).
- Paraphrased without quotation marks: 2 Peter 1:21 ("carried along by the Holy Spirit", the BSB's words); Mark 1:10 (heavens breaking open, Spirit descending like a dove); Ezekiel 37:7-10 (bones come together, no breath, prophesy to the breath, they stand up); Acts 2:3, 2:11 (tongues like flames of fire; the wonders of God in their own languages); Acts 5:3-4 (lying to the Holy Spirit is lying to God).
