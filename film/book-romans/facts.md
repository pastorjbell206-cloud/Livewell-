# Romans: the good news, set out: the facts in the script, and where each was checked

This film is a rebuild of the Bible Explorer film `client/public/explorer/films/book-romans.json`. The words are new; every claim is one the old film already made, and the old film's sources are its rows in `docs/work/elite/explorer-ledger.md`. Nothing was added from outside.

## Scripture (Berean Standard Bible)

Each quotation was checked word for word against `client/public/bible/text/` by `film/tools/write-script.py` on 2026-11-26.

- **Romans 1:16** (spoken): “I am not ashamed of the gospel, because it is the power of God for salvation to everyone who believes”
- **Romans 3:21** (shown on screen): “But now, apart from the law, the righteousness of God has been revealed”
- **Romans 3:23** (spoken): “for all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God”
- **Romans 3:24** (shown on screen): “justified freely by His grace through the redemption that is in Christ Jesus.”
- **Romans 8:1** (spoken): “there is now no condemnation for those who are in Christ Jesus.”
- **Romans 15:7** (shown on screen): “Accept one another, then, just as Christ accepted you”
- **Romans 1:7** (shown on screen): “To all in Rome who are loved by God and called to be saints: Grace and peace to you”

## Notes

- Every sentence restates a caption of the old film or its "differ" note. Nothing was added from outside.
- Where Christians differ ("works of the law"; faith in Christ or the faithfulness of Christ) the script names both readings as the old note does and takes no side.
- Jewish and Gentile believers are drawn exactly alike. "God's word has not failed" about Israel is the old film's sentence.
- The cross is bare.
- The old film's care line stays on the page word for word; none was written here.
