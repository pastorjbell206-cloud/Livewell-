# The oldest report: the facts in the script, and where to check each

None of these was checked against a primary source in this session. Each is standard and widely published; check before recording. Scripture lines are the Berean Standard Bible, copied from `client/public/bible/text/`.

- **"A little over twenty years after the cross":** 1 Corinthians is usually dated AD 53 to 55, and the crucifixion AD 30 or 33.
- **"Something he says was handed on to him":** 1 Corinthians 15:3, "what I received I passed on to you".
- **1 Corinthians 15:3-5:** quoted word for word, from "that Christ died".
- **Cephas is Peter:** John 1:42.
- **"Most scholars who study this passage, including many who aren't Christians, think Paul received these words within a few years of the events":** the common view. Check it before recording against James D. G. Dunn, Jesus Remembered (2003), and Gerd Lüdemann, The Resurrection of Jesus (1994), who is not a believer. If it cannot be confirmed in their own words, cut the sentence to "Many scholars think Paul received these words very early."
- **More than five hundred, most still alive:** 1 Corinthians 15:6, given as a paraphrase.
- **For James:** the script claims only that the report is early, not that this proves the resurrection, and it says so.
