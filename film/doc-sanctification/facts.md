## For James

**Left out on purpose.** The Hebrew and Greek word study (qadash, hagiasmos and its count), Isaiah 6, the Didache and the desert monks, Aquinas, the Councils of Carthage (418), Vienne and Trent, Luther's simul iustus et peccator and the Antinomian disputes, Calvin, the Heidelberg and Westminster confessions, Owen, Spener, Wesley's Plain Account, Phoebe Palmer, Ryle, Warfield, William Durham and the Assemblies of God, John Murray, and the Romans 7 debate (Augustine, Luther and Calvin against Kümmel). The errors section is used only for the question "does it matter how you live?" and Paul's answer; antinomianism, legalism, perfectionism and quietism are never named. "Sexual sin" at Corinth and "handles sexuality" in the life list are left out for length, not to soften anything.

**Views named without choosing.** Reformed; Lutheran; Wesleyan-Holiness; Keswick; Catholic and Orthodox (together, as the file has them). On screen they are five equal panels, each lit only while it is spoken, all left equal at the end. Each panel's picture comes from the file's own wording: a decisive break and a climb that stays under a dashed line ("never complete in this life"); water and a dot that goes down and rises again ("a daily return to baptism"); a heart filling ("so fill the believer with love"); a door and a steady line ("decisive act of surrender", "consistent victory"); a cup and growth toward light ("through the sacraments", "likeness to God").

**Worth a second look.**
- "Christians differ on how far holiness reaches in this life" is the film's own frame for the differ section, not a sentence from the file.
- The Augustine line ("no one in this life is without sin") comes just before the five views. It is history, attributed to him, and the Wesleyan line says plainly that their teaching "isn't sinless perfection", but a Wesleyan listener might hear the order as a lean.
- "Not trusting your own progress" (Lutheran) paraphrases "progress ... is not the ground of confidence".
- "Because God has no end" paraphrases "since God is infinite".
- The closing card, "Already his. Still growing.", is the film's own line, not a quote.
- 599 words, at the top of the 480 to 600 range.

**Names to listen for.** Sanctification; Corinth; Sinai; Leviticus; Ezekiel; Hebrews; Philippians; Gregory of Nyssa (NISS-uh); Augustine; Wesleyan; Keswick (KEZ-ick, the w is silent); theosis (thee-OH-sis); Zechariah; Thessalonians.
