## For James

**Left out on purpose.**
- The names attached to each millennial view (Augustine, Vos and Hoekema; the Puritans, Edwards and Warfield; Papias, Justin, Irenaeus and Ladd; Darby, Scofield, Ryrie and Walvoord). They were cut so that no view carries more weight from its famous names.
- The preterist question (how much of the Olivet Discourse was fulfilled in AD 70), the "this generation" saying in Mark 13:30, and Schweitzer, France and Wright's readings of it. It's a separate debate and needs its own film.
- The Seventh-day Adventist Church's origin among Miller's followers. The film names one man's calculation and no living church.
- The three Greek words (parousia, epiphaneia, apokalypsis), the man of lawlessness (2 Thessalonians 2), the "meeting in the air" debate over apantēsis, the Didache, Justin's two comings, Nicaea 325, the Reformation confessions, the Montanists and Münster.
- "Nor the Son" from Matthew 24:36. The film paraphrases the verse ("Only the Father knows it") and doesn't quote it.
- The dispensational details "reigns from Jerusalem" and "God turns again to Israel" (Romans 11). The film gives the view's own core claim, that God's promises to Israel will be kept to the letter. It ties no passage to any modern nation, event or person and sets no date.

**Views named without choosing.** Amillennialism, postmillennialism, historic premillennialism and dispensational premillennialism, with the rapture's timing given as "most say before". In the picture each view is a timeline of the same weight and style. Every timeline has the same mustard return dot, and one line joins them all: "One return, confessed by all."

**Points to check.**
- Changed in review: the file's "it could be today" became "no one knows the hour", because historic premillennialists and postmillennialists hold that some events come first.
- Changed in review: "the angels" became "two men dressed in white" (Acts 1:10).
- "The creeds never set a date" is a summary of the history and errors sections, not a sentence in the file.
- Acts 1:11 is read whole, including "they said".

**Names to listen for.** Galilee; Constantinople ("three eighty-one"); marana tha (MAH-rah-nah THAH); amillennialists (AY-mil-LEN-ee-uh-lists); postmillennialists; premillennialists; dispensational; William Miller; "eighteen forty-three or eighteen forty-four"; "October twenty-second, eighteen forty-four"; Revelation 20.
