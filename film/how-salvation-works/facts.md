# How salvation works: the facts in the script, and where each was checked

This film is a rebuild of the Bible Explorer film `client/public/explorer/films/how-salvation-works.json`. The words are new; every claim is one the old film already made, and the old film's sources are its rows in `docs/work/elite/explorer-ledger.md`. Nothing was added from outside.

## Scripture (Berean Standard Bible)

Each quotation was checked word for word against `client/public/bible/text/` by `film/tools/write-script.py` on 2026-11-26.

- **Genesis 1:27** (shown on screen): “So God created man in His own image”
- **Romans 3:23** (shown on screen): “for all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God”
- **Ephesians 2:9** (spoken): “not by works, so that no one can boast.”
- **John 3:16** (spoken): “For God so loved the world that He gave His one and only Son, that everyone who believes in Him shall not perish but have eternal life.”
- **1 Peter 3:18** (spoken): “For Christ also suffered for sins once for all, the righteous for the unrighteous, to bring you to God.”
- **Romans 4:25** (shown on screen): “He was delivered over to death for our trespasses and was raised to life for our justification.”
- **Mark 1:15** (shown on screen): “Repent and believe in the gospel!”
- **2 Corinthians 5:17** (spoken): “if anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation.”
- **Revelation 21:4** (shown on screen): “and there will be no more death or mourning or crying or pain”

## Notes

- Every sentence restates a caption of the old film or its "differ" note. Nothing was added from outside.
- Where churches differ (the place of baptism, God's choosing and ours, whether a believer can fall away) the three questions are named as the old note names them and the film does not choose. "Christians differ on many details of the last things. On this hope they speak together" is the old film's.
- "Not to win over an unwilling God" and "the Father and the Son are not on opposite sides here" are the old film's sentences; the picture never sets them against each other.
- Bible words spoken outside the chapter verses ("repent and believe in the gospel") are the old film's own quotations.
- The old film's care line, which quotes Romans 5:8, stays on the page word for word; none was written here.
