## How each film is made (unchanged, longer)
3 to 4 minutes (about 560 to 680 words). Built only from pages already on LiveWell whose sources were checked: the old film and its ledger, the hard-question pages and their source records, and the Honest Answers. Each script has a facts.md naming the source of every claim. Scripture is the Berean Standard Bible, checked word for word by machine. Each film gives the objection at its strongest, the evidence, the honest limits, and two or three moments that turn to the viewer ("Pause here and ask yourself..."). Where Christians differ, the film names the views and takes no side.
