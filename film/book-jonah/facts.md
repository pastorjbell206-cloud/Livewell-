# Jonah: the prophet who ran from mercy: the facts in the script, and where each was checked

This film is a rebuild of the Bible Explorer film `client/public/explorer/films/book-jonah.json`. The words are new; every claim is one the old film already made, and the old film's sources are its rows in `docs/work/elite/explorer-ledger.md`. Nothing was added from outside.

## Scripture (Berean Standard Bible)

Each quotation was checked word for word against `client/public/bible/text/` by `film/tools/write-script.py` on 2026-11-26.

- **Jonah 1:3** (shown on screen): “Jonah, however, got up to flee to Tarshish, away from the presence of the LORD.”
- **Jonah 1:17** (shown on screen): “Now the LORD had appointed a great fish to swallow Jonah”
- **Jonah 2:6** (spoken): “But You raised my life from the pit, O LORD my God!”
- **Jonah 3:10** (shown on screen): “He relented from the disaster He had threatened to bring upon them.”
- **Jonah 4:2** (spoken): “I knew that You are a gracious and compassionate God, slow to anger”
- **Jonah 4:11** (shown on screen): “So should I not care about the great city of Nineveh”

## Notes

- Every sentence restates a caption of the old film or its "differ" note.
- Where readers differ (history or a teaching story), the script names both views and "Some hold both", as the old note does, and takes no side. The picture gives three identical dots the same line to the same scroll.
- "Jesus took this as the sign of Jonah, a picture of his own death" is the old film's sentence. The cross is bare; no figure is drawn.
- The fish is one flat shape with no eye or mouth, and the man is never shown inside it.
- The old film's care line stays on the page word for word; none was written here.
