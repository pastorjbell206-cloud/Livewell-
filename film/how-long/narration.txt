Some people think faith means never complaining to God. The Bible disagrees.

How long, O LORD? Will You forget me forever? How long will You hide Your face from me?

That's a prayer. It's in the Bible's own prayer book, and it isn't alone. More psalms sound like this than any other kind.

They're called laments.

And they follow a path. Turn to God. Tell him what's wrong, plainly. Ask him to act. Then choose to trust him.

Watch David make that turn. Nothing around him has changed yet.

But I have trusted in Your loving devotion; my heart will rejoice in Your salvation.

Lament isn't the opposite of faith. It's what faith sounds like when it hurts.
