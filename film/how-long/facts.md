# How long?: the facts in the script, and where to check each

None of these was checked against a primary source in this session. Each is standard and widely published; check before recording. Scripture lines are the Berean Standard Bible, copied from `client/public/bible/text/`.

- **Psalm 13:1 and 13:5:** quoted word for word, without the heading ("For the choirmaster. A Psalm of David.").
- **"More psalms sound like this than any other kind":** lament is the largest group of psalms in the standard classifications (Gunkel; Westermann, Praise and Lament in the Psalms). No fraction is claimed.
- **The path (turn, complaint, ask, trust):** the usual outline of a lament psalm, and the one the site's lament study guide teaches.
- **"Watch David":** the psalm's heading names David.
