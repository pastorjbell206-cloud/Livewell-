A king takes another man's wife. Then he has the man killed to cover it up.

The king's name is David. And for a while, nothing happens.

Then a prophet named Nathan walks in. He doesn't accuse the king. He tells him a story.

A rich man owns huge flocks. A poor man owns one little lamb, raised like a daughter.

A guest shows up. And the rich man takes the poor man's lamb for dinner.

David is furious. That man deserves to die, he says.

Nathan looks at him and says four words.

You are that man!

Here's why the story worked. David could see the sin clearly, as long as it belonged to somebody else.

So can we. We're experts on other people's faults and strangers to our own.

A king could have had Nathan killed. David says six words instead.

I have sinned against the LORD.

The way out of a blind spot starts when you let someone tell you the truth.
