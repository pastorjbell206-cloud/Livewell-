# You are that man: the facts in the script, and where to check each

None of these was checked against a primary source in this session. Each is standard and widely published; check before recording. Scripture lines are the Berean Standard Bible, copied from `client/public/bible/text/`.

- **The king takes another man's wife and has him killed:** 2 Samuel 11 (Bathsheba and Uriah).
- **"For a while, nothing happens":** the child is born before Nathan comes (2 Samuel 11:27 to 12:1).
- **Nathan's story:** 2 Samuel 12:1-4. "Raised like a daughter" rests on verse 3, "it was like a daughter to him".
- **"That man deserves to die":** David's words in 2 Samuel 12:5, given as a paraphrase, not a quotation.
- **"You are that man!":** 2 Samuel 12:7, four words, with the verse's own exclamation mark.
- **"I have sinned against the LORD.":** 2 Samuel 12:13, six words.
- **For James:** the teaching line ("experts on other people's faults and strangers to our own") is new wording for the idea of Blind Spots, not a quotation from it.
