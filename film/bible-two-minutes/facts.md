# The whole Bible in two minutes: the facts in the script, and where each was checked

This film is a rebuild of the Bible Explorer film `client/public/explorer/films/bible-two-minutes.json`. The words are new; every claim is one the old film already made, and the old film's sources are its rows in `docs/work/elite/explorer-ledger.md`. Nothing was added from outside.

## Scripture (Berean Standard Bible)

Each quotation was checked word for word against `client/public/bible/text/` by `film/tools/write-script.py` on 2026-11-26.

- **Genesis 1:31** (shown on screen): “And God looked upon all that He had made, and indeed, it was very good.”
- **Genesis 12:3** (shown on screen): “all the families of the earth will be blessed through you.”
- **Exodus 20:2** (shown on screen): “I am the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of slavery.”
- **2 Samuel 7:16** (shown on screen): “your throne will be established forever.”
- **Jeremiah 31:33** (shown on screen): “I will put My law in their minds and inscribe it on their hearts.”
- **1 Corinthians 15:3-4** (shown on screen): “that Christ died for our sins according to the Scriptures, that He was buried, that He was raised on the third day according to the Scriptures”
- **Acts 1:8** (shown on screen): “you will be My witnesses in Jerusalem, and in all Judea and Samaria, and to the ends of the earth.”
- **Revelation 21:5** (spoken): “Behold, I make all things new.”

## Notes

- Every sentence restates a caption of the old film at the older level. "A tent so he can live among them" is the old film's "the tabernacle so that he can dwell among them". "Fifty days later" is the old film's "Fifty days after Passover".
- The flood, the murder, the burning of Jerusalem and the cross are named, never shown.
- The old film's care line stays on its page word for word; none was written here.
- The film runs about two and a half minutes, not two.
