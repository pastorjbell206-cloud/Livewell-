# Creation and the image of God: the facts in the script, and where each was checked

This film is a rebuild of the Bible Explorer film `client/public/explorer/films/image-of-god.json`. The words are new; every claim is one the old film already made, and the old film's sources are its rows in `docs/work/elite/explorer-ledger.md`. Nothing was added from outside.

## Scripture (Berean Standard Bible)

Each quotation was checked word for word against `client/public/bible/text/` by `film/tools/write-script.py` on 2026-11-26.

- **Genesis 1:1** (spoken): “In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth.”
- **Genesis 1:31** (shown on screen): “And God looked upon all that He had made, and indeed, it was very good.”
- **Genesis 1:27** (spoken): “So God created man in His own image; in the image of God He created him; male and female He created them.”
- **Isaiah 43:7** (shown on screen): “everyone called by My name and created for My glory, whom I have indeed formed and made.”
- **Genesis 2:15** (shown on screen): “Then the LORD God took the man and placed him in the Garden of Eden to cultivate and keep it.”
- **Psalm 8:5** (shown on screen): “You crowned him with glory and honor.”
