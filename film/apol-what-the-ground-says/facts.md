## For James

- "Oppressed" stands for Mesha's "afflicted" (the page's 1884 wording), checked in sense against the modern dictionary the page cites.
- Takes no side on how large David and Solomon's kingdom was, or on how to read the deliverance of Jerusalem.
- Names to listen for: Tel Dan, Aramaic, Aram, Mesha, Omri, Moab, Ahab, Sennacherib, Hezekiah.
