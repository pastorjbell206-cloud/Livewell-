# 1 Corinthians: a gifted church, coming apart: the facts in the script, and where each was checked

This film is a rebuild of the Bible Explorer film `client/public/explorer/films/book-1-corinthians.json`. The words are new; every claim is one the old film already made, and the old film's sources are its rows in `docs/work/elite/explorer-ledger.md`. Nothing was added from outside.

## Scripture (Berean Standard Bible)

Each quotation was checked word for word against `client/public/bible/text/` by `film/tools/write-script.py` on 2026-11-26.

- **1 Corinthians 1:2** (shown on screen): “To the church of God in Corinth, to those sanctified in Christ Jesus and called to be holy”
- **1 Corinthians 7:1** (shown on screen): “Now for the matters you wrote about”
- **1 Corinthians 2:2** (spoken): “For I resolved to know nothing while I was with you except Jesus Christ and Him crucified.”
- **1 Corinthians 12:27** (spoken): “Now you are the body of Christ, and each of you is a member of it.”
- **1 Corinthians 13:4** (spoken): “Love is patient, love is kind.”
- **1 Corinthians 15:20** (spoken): “But Christ has indeed been raised from the dead”
- **1 Corinthians 16:14** (spoken): “Do everything in love.”

## Notes

- Every sentence restates a caption of the old film or its "differ" note. Nothing was added from outside.
- The hard passages (chapter 7, head coverings in chapter 11, tongues and the place of women in leading worship in chapters 12 to 14) are named as the old note names them, with the three things readers weigh, and no side is taken.
- The old film lists no key chapters, so none are listed; "start with chapters one to four" is its own advice.
- "Paul's counsel near the end" refers to 1 Corinthians 16:14, in the last chapter.
- The body is drawn as joined shapes, never as a figure. The cross is bare.
- The old film's care line stays on the page word for word; none was written here.
