# Prayer: the facts in the script, and where each was checked

This film is a rebuild of the Bible Explorer film `client/public/explorer/films/prayer.json`. The words are new; every claim is one the old film already made, and the old film's sources are its rows in `docs/work/elite/explorer-ledger.md`. Nothing was added from outside.

## Scripture (Berean Standard Bible)

Each quotation was checked word for word against `client/public/bible/text/` by `film/tools/write-script.py` on 2026-11-26.

- **Matthew 6:10** (spoken): “Your kingdom come, Your will be done, on earth as it is in heaven.”
- **Psalm 100:4** (shown on screen): “Enter His gates with thanksgiving and His courts with praise”
- **Philippians 4:6** (spoken): “Be anxious for nothing, but in everything, by prayer and petition, with thanksgiving, present your requests to God.”
- **1 John 1:9** (shown on screen): “If we confess our sins, He is faithful and just to forgive us our sins”
- **Luke 22:42** (spoken): “Yet not My will, but Yours be done.”
- **Romans 8:26** (shown on screen): “the Spirit Himself intercedes for us with groans too deep for words.”

## Notes

- Every sentence restates a caption of the old film or its "differ" note. Nothing was added from outside.
- The definitions of prayer and of confession are the family catechism's, as the old film gives them.
- "Some prayers, even for someone to get better, are not answered the way we longed for, and that hurts. The Bible doesn't hide it" is the old film's caption. The old film's care line on this stays on the page word for word; the film does not restate it.
- The spoken line of the Lord's Prayer is Matthew 6:10; the old film shows 6:9-10.
- God is never drawn; no sickbed, grave or praying-hands symbol appears.
