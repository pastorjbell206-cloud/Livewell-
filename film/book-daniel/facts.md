# Daniel: the Most High rules: the facts in the script, and where each was checked

This film is a rebuild of the Bible Explorer film `client/public/explorer/films/book-daniel.json`. The words are new; every claim is one the old film already made, and the old film's sources are its rows in `docs/work/elite/explorer-ledger.md`. Nothing was added from outside.

## Scripture (Berean Standard Bible)

Each quotation was checked word for word against `client/public/bible/text/` by `film/tools/write-script.py` on 2026-11-26.

- **Daniel 1:17** (shown on screen): “To these four young men God gave knowledge and understanding”
- **Daniel 2:44** (shown on screen): “the God of heaven will set up a kingdom that will never be destroyed”
- **Daniel 4:25** (spoken): “the Most High rules over the kingdom of mankind”
- **Daniel 3:18** (spoken): “But even if He does not, let it be known to you, O king, that we will not serve your gods”
- **Daniel 6:22** (shown on screen): “My God sent His angel and shut the mouths of the lions.”
- **Daniel 7:13** (shown on screen): “I saw One like the Son of Man coming with the clouds of heaven.”

## Notes

- Every sentence restates a caption of the old film or its "differ" note.
- Where Christians differ: the film names both readings of the four kingdoms in the old film's own words and says careful readers differ on when the book was finished. No date is given and no side is taken; the two rows of kingdoms are drawn exactly alike.
- "The visions are pictures, not a code for the news" is the old film's sentence.
- "One like a son of man" is drawn as a line and a ring, never a figure. The furnace and the lions are not drawn.
- Two of the script's unspoken verses (Daniel 1:17 and 2:44) are not shown in the picture.
- The old film's care line stays on the page word for word; none was written here.
