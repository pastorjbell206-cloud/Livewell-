# Matthew: God with us: the facts in the script, and where each was checked

This film is a rebuild of the Bible Explorer film `client/public/explorer/films/book-matthew.json`. The words are new; every claim is one the old film already made, and the old film's sources are its rows in `docs/work/elite/explorer-ledger.md`. Nothing was added from outside.

## Scripture (Berean Standard Bible)

Each quotation was checked word for word against `client/public/bible/text/` by `film/tools/write-script.py` on 2026-11-26.

- **Matthew 1:23** (shown on screen): “they will call Him Immanuel” (which means, “God with us”).”
- **Matthew 7:28** (shown on screen): “When Jesus had finished saying these things”
- **Matthew 5:17** (spoken): “I have not come to abolish them, but to fulfill them.”
- **Matthew 4:17** (shown on screen): “Repent, for the kingdom of heaven is near.”
- **Matthew 16:16** (spoken): “You are the Christ, the Son of the living God.”
- **Matthew 28:19** (shown on screen): “Therefore go and make disciples of all nations”
- **Matthew 28:20** (spoken): “And surely I am with you always, even to the end of the age.”

## Notes

- Every sentence restates a caption of the old film. Nothing was added from outside.
- Jesus is never drawn. The cross is bare.
- Nothing in the script or picture blames the Jewish people.
- The old film's care line stays on the page word for word; none was written here.
